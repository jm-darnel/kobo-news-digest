NYPD fatally shoots driver on Belt Parkway following suspected abduction

By Michael Sol Warren · Gothamist · November 15, 2026

Police shot and killed a man in Brooklyn on Saturday night after responding to reports of a woman trapped and screaming for help from inside a van in Coney Island.

“ She has confirmed that she was in the van and was screaming and exited the vehicle before the police officers first arrived,” Assistant Chief Charles Minch, the commander of the NYPD’s Patrol Borough Brooklyn South, said at a press conference.

“ The male caller said that he saw a white van and further stated, ‘There's a girl kicking and screaming in there,’” Minch said. “The female caller stated that she heard the girl saying, ‘Let me go,’ multiple times.”

“The driver began to drive forwards and backwards, ramming both police and occupied civilian vehicles indiscriminately,” Minch said.

Minch said police then tried to use a taser and pepper spray against the driver, but neither was “effective, and the driver continued to ram nearby vehicles.”

Multiple officers then shot the driver, Minch said. It is unclear how many officers fired, or how many shots were fired.

“ The driver of the vehicle was wanted for an unlawful imprisonment last month and also a domestic assault earlier this month,” Minch said. “The victim in both cases was the female who had been in the white van.”

Investigations are ongoing into both the suspected kidnapping and the fatal police shooting. Minch said the shooting was recorded on police body cameras.
