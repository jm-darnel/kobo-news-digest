The Trust For Governors Island Releases Its Annual Climate Solutions Challenge

Harlem World · November 17, 2026

This initiative, presented through the Governors Island Climate Piloting Program, offers early-stage companies and innovators the opportunity to test and scale climate products and services in a real-world environment. Over 3-to-18-month periods, participants receive rent-free physical space on Governors Island, where they gain access to valuable technical support, visibility through events and marketing, and networking opportunities designed to connect participants with funders, investors, customers, and the public.

“The Trust’s Climate Piloting Program is a powerful example of what’s possible when we invest in the people and solutions driving a more sustainable future,” said Clare Newman, President and CEO of the Trust for Governors Island. “On Governors Island, innovations are brought to life and tested in full view of the public—whether you engage by exploring our resilient parkland, composting your food waste, or learning about the extraordinary companies showcasing their solutions. We are proud to share their work with the public and look forward to welcoming even more to the Island next year.”

“With 2027 projected to be one of the hottest years on record, this challenge couldn’t come at a more critical time,” said Lauren Wang, Director of Climate Programs at the Trust for Governors Island. “Each year, the Climate Solutions Challenge creates new pathways for climate action by offering companies and nonprofits the opportunity to demonstrate their products in a real-world setting and move closer to bringing them to market. Beyond providing this valuable testing ground, the program helps participants build momentum, connect with new funding opportunities, and pursue additional awards and partnerships to further their impact.”

2027 Climate Solutions Challenge: Cooling

Proposals are due by January 8, 2027, with winners announced in April 2027. More information, including webinars and the climate piloting application, can be found online at www.govisland.org/challenge.

Governors Island is at the forefront of researching and demonstrating solutions that can be scaled globally. Its unique waterfront environment complements an award-winning park engineered for climate change; nearly one million annual visitors; opportunities for education, research, and piloting; public artworks engaging with climate issues; and a growing community of educational, nonprofit, and commercial tenants focused on climate solutions — including Billion Oyster Project, the Urban Assembly New York Harbor School, NYU Gallatin WetLab, and the soon-to-open Buttermilk Labs.
