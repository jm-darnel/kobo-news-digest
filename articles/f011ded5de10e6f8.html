Rep. Jeffries talks midterm wins, Pete Hegseth impeachment and speaker vote

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 10, 2026

Political anchor Errol Louis sat down with Rep. Hakeem Jeffries, who is poised to become the next speaker of the U.S. House.

He spoke about how Democrats won back the House, how he plans to take on the Trump administration, including a vote on impeaching Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth that could come as soon as Wednesday, and how he'll deliver on affordability promises for New York City.
