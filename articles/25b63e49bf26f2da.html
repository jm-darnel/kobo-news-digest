Assemblymember Tony Simone Calls For Housing Justice For Veterans From Harlem To Hollis

Harlem World · November 10, 2026

Assemblymember Tony Simone calls for passage of the Fair Access to Co-ops for Veterans Act (H.R.1803) introduced by U.S. Rep. Grace Meng (D-Queens).

This bill makes permanent the authority of the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) home loan guarantee program to guarantee loans for a veteran’s purchase of stock or membership in a cooperative housing corporation.

“Growing up, my family was priced out of New York as rising rents made it increasingly difficult to afford a home here,” said Assemblymember Tony Simone (D-Manhattan). “As the son of a veteran, I believe those who served our country deserve every opportunity to put down roots and build financial security for their families. Co-ops are a cornerstone of New York City’s housing landscape, and the Fair Access to Co-ops for Veterans Act would ensure veterans can access this important path to homeownership.”

“We must always be there to support our veterans.”

“Veterans deserve the opportunity to put down roots in the communities they call home and my legislation to make it easier for veterans to purchase co-ops would help those who served our nation own homes in New York and across the country,” said U.S. Rep. Grace Meng (D-Queens). “Thank you to Assemblymember Simone and everybody who has expressed support for this important bill. We must always be there to support our veterans.”

New York is taking action to address its housing affordability crisis by building more homes, expanding homeownership opportunities, and protecting tenants. These solutions are outlined in Assemblymember Simone’s State of Yes report, which lays out a roadmap for making housing more affordable and accessible for all New Yorkers.

As we recognize Veterans Day, we must do more than thank those who served; we must ensure they have access to the benefits and opportunities they earned. The federal government should close this gap and ensure veterans can use their VA home loan benefits to purchase co-ops, one of New York City’s most common paths to homeownership. The Fair Access to Co-ops for Veterans Act would help make that possible.

Former Congresswoman Carolyn Maloney said: “I first introduced the Veterans Housing Fairness Act in 1995, after learning that a constituent had attempted to purchase a residential cooperative unit, but was told that his VA loan could not be used to buy the property. VA loans should be available for all types of housing. There is no reason New York City coops should be excluded. With this action veterans in New York City and around the country will see their housing options expand. I commend all of my colleagues who are continuing this important work – especially Rep Grace Meng and AM Tony Simone.”

“…our veterans who sacrifice so much to protect our great nation…”

“Access to affordable housing is a critical priority, especially for our veterans who sacrifice so much to protect our great nation,” Assemblyman Steve Stern said. “For many, co-ops are a way to become homeowners in a limited and increasingly out of reach housing market. As Chairman of the Assembly Veterans’ Affairs Committee, I urge Congress to pass the ‘Fair Access to Co-ops for Veterans Act’ to open more affordable opportunities for our heroes and their families to become homeowners.”

“As a veteran entering my 30s, I want to put down roots in the neighborhood I’ve called home for the past six years. But when I looked into using my VA home loan to buy an apartment on the Upper West Side, I discovered how limited my options were. VA loans cannot be used to purchase co-ops, and only condominiums in VA-approved buildings qualify. There is just one VA-approved building on the entire Upper West Side. If nothing is for sale there, veterans like me are out of luck. This is a challenge veterans across the borough face, and why we’ve been urging Congress to pass the Fair Access to Co-ops for Veterans Act. Those who served our country deserve the opportunity to put down roots and build wealth through homeownership.” Jacob Kimbarow, Chair of the Young Democrats of America Veterans Caucus

Annemarie Gray, Executive Director, Open New York: “Veterans earned their home loan benefit, and in New York City they can barely use it. Co-ops make up a huge share of the apartments that working people here can afford to buy, yet a federal rule keeps VA loans out of nearly all of them, which leaves veterans competing over a handful of approved condo buildings in a city already short on homes. Congress should pass the Fair Access to Co-ops for Veterans Act. We thank Assemblymember Simone for leading on this, and Open New York will keep fighting for a city with enough homes for everyone who wants to stay.”

“Those who served our country deserve every opportunity to build wealth…”

“Homeownership is one of the most important pathways to building generational wealth and long-term financial stability. In New York City, where more than 22% of veterans identify as Hispanic or Latino, expanding access to homeownership is especially important to the communities we serve. The Fair Access to Co-ops for Veterans Act would remove an outdated barrier and allow veterans to use their VA home loan benefits to purchase co-op apartments, expanding access to one of New York City’s most important forms of affordable homeownership. Those who served our country deserve every opportunity to build wealth and a secure future for themselves and their families.” Paul Cibrano, Incoming 2027 President, NAHREP Upper Manhattan Chapter.
