Turkey Giveaway in Inwood Feeds 300 Families Ahead of Thanksgiving

Harlem World · November 25, 2026

On a brisk morning outside the Academy Medical office on Academy Avenue in Inwood, hundreds of families lined up for something that has become a neighborhood ritual: a free Thanksgiving turkey.

Healthfirst and SOMOS Community Care hosted their annual giveaway yesterday and gave birds to more than 300 families. Two baseball legends, Hall of Famer Mariano Rivera and MLB All-Star Bartolo Colón, stopped by to greet the crowd.

Meeting Neighbors Where They Are

Healthfirst frames the giveaway as part of its wider mission. “Healthfirst’s ongoing collaboration with SOMOS Community Care is just one example of our commitment to ensuring New Yorkers have access to affordable, quality healthcare in their community,” said Dr. Paul Amajor, Director of Strategic Health Initiatives at Healthfirst.

He described the Inwood event as “a meaningful annual tradition in which we meet our members where they are and support their access to nutritious food this holiday.” That approach, bringing resources directly to the neighborhood instead of asking residents to seek them out, runs through much of what the two organizations do together.
