These are New Yorkers' favorite books of 2026

By Hannah Frishberg · Gothamist · November 19, 2026

This year has proven no exception: A press release from the library describes the list, chosen by librarians and other NYPL staffers, as “uniquely informed by the literary interests of actual New Yorkers.” It includes 260 books that range from bestsellers to foreign translations to sleeper hits across genres.

Highlights from the roundup’s three age-based sublists (adults, teens and kids, plus 25 Spanish children’s book titles) include Emily St. John Mandel’s future-set novel “Exit Party,” Joshua Jelly-Schapiro’s Harry Belafonte biography “Daylight Come” and, in the Kids category, Georgina Chadderton’s graphic memoir “Oh Brother.”

NYPL also released a list of the year’s Notable Audiobooks.

Library officials described the list as an annual "love letter" to New Yorkers and other readers, curated by real New Yorkers with genre expertise and community connections.

Many of the list’s top titles that had lengthy wait times for checkout even ahead of the list's release are now available via no-wait digital access to anyone with a library card. All items on the Notable Audiobooks list are also available for no-wait digital access through year’s end.

NYPL is hosting two free author talks next month: Some of the year’s buzziest fiction authors (Valeria Luiselli, Douglas Stuart and Vincent Yu) are at the Stavros Niarchos Foundation Library on Dec. 3. Then, at Staten Island’s Stapleton Library branch on Dec. 9, will be an author discussion of the year’s best mysteries and thrillers.
