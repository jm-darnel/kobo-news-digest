Communities come together to spread Thanksgiving cheer

By AmNews Staff Reports · Amsterdam News · November 25, 2026

Al Sharpton’s National Action Network hosts Thanksgiving dinner

Harlem Mothers and Fathers S.A.V.E. holds Thanksgiving turkey giveaway

Harlem Mothers and Fathers S.A.V.E. distributed Thanksgiving turkeys to Harlem families in need living at 300, 301, 306, 309, 310 and 311 W. 128th St. Founded in 2006, the Harlem-based nonprofit supports families affected by gun violence and works to prevent youth violence through activism, education and victim services.

Bronx Borough President Vanessa L. Gibson partners with Hot 97, WBLS and local leaders for annual turkey drive

On Nov. 19, Bronx Borough President Vanessa L. Gibson joined Hot 97, WBLS, state Sen. Gustavo Rivera, Assemblymember John Zaccaro Jr. and City Council Member Eric Dinowitz to host a Thanksgiving turkey drive at Tracey Towers. The drive aimed to ease financial pressures and ensure families had food for a holiday celebration. The event also featured a live broadcast of Hot 97’s “Mornings with Mero,” followed by Gibson presenting a citation of merit to Goya Foods for 90 years of community service.

Brooklyn Borough President Antonio Reynoso distributes 60,000 pounds of food

On Nov. 20, Brooklyn Borough President Antonio Reynoso — in partnership with UA3, MUNA Social Services and the New York City Department of Parks and Recreation — hosted his annual Thanksgiving food distribution outside Brooklyn Borough Hall. Staff distributed about 60,000 pounds of fresh produce, nonperishables and other food items to more than 120 houses of worship, community kitchens and community-based organizations addressing food insecurity and homelessness. New York Liberty player Breanna Stewart and her wife, Marta Xargay Casademont, also joined to help hand out food.

Newark Mayor Ras Baraka distributes Thanksgiving meals with LL Cool J, Jim Jones and others

Newark Mayor Ras J. Baraka and HelloFresh distributed 4,000 turkeys and 4,000 boxes of side-dish ingredients to city residents at The Temple of Hip-Hop, a cultural community center at 55 Ludlow St. The free drive-thru and walk-up event provided families with a whole turkey, russet and sweet potatoes, fresh cranberries, apples and carrots, along with step-by-step recipe instructions. U.S. Rep. LaMonica McIver and special guests LL Cool J, Jim Jones, Dave East, Fabolous and Maino helped hand out turkeys during the event.

Win and FeedingNY partner to bring holiday meals to homeless families

On Nov. 23, Win — the largest provider of shelter and supportive housing for families experiencing homelessness in New York City and the nation — partnered with FeedingNY to pack and distribute 3,500 Thanksgiving dinners to homeless families across the city. Hundreds of volunteers assembled each meal, which included a full-sized turkey and traditional accompaniments, before they were hand-delivered to shelters across all five boroughs later that day.

Teens from Jack and Jill of America’s Brooklyn Chapter lead food giveaway

On Nov. 21, more than 30 teens from the Brooklyn Chapter of Jack and Jill of America helped provide Thanksgiving turkeys and trimmings to between 150 and 200 local families at Cornerstone Baptist Church. The teens also participated in a discussion led by Jennifer Jones Austin, CEO and executive director of the Federation of Protestant Welfare Agencies and chair of the New York City Civilian Complaint Review Board, exploring New York City’s affordability crisis and the role young people can play in strengthening their communities.

UFT hosts Thanksgiving feast and coat drive for students in temporary housing

Mount Sinai brings cancer screening education and turkey giveaway to Harlem

On Nov. 19, Mount Sinai Tisch Cancer Center, Community Healthcare Network, New York Common Pantry and the King Towers Resident Association hosted “Thankful for Screening,” a free community health event. Attendees learned about screenings for prostate, breast, lung and cervical cancers, and optional on-site prostate screenings were available inside a mobile unit alongside scheduling assistance for other exams. Following each mandatory 45-minute educational session, participants received a free Thanksgiving turkey.

Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade

This year marks the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade . Thousands of spectators are expected to line the route from the Upper West Side to Midtown to watch floats, marching bands, celebrities and Santa Claus make their way through Manhattan. Giant balloons include Billy Brightpaw, Bluey, Hello Kitty, Snoopy and Mickey Mouse, while Queen Latifah, Wyclef Jean, Keke Palmer and Leslie Odom Jr. are among this year’s performers.
