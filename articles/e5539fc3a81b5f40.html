Yankees To Hire Brandon Hyde As Bench Coach

By Steve Adams · MLB Trade Rumors (Yankees) · November 25, 2026

Hyde, 53, had a four-year career as a minor leaguer but has spent the bulk of his baseball life managing and coaching. He skippered the Orioles from 2019-25 but was fired after Baltimore’s 15-28 start to the 2025 season. He’s been in the Rays’ baseball operations department as a senior advisor for the past year. Hyde spoke to the Mariners about their managerial vacancy this offseason, but that job ultimately went to Cubs director of player development Jason Kanzler.

Prior to his time in Baltimore, Hyde spent several seasons as a first base coach and bench coach with the Cubs, where he worked under Rick Renteria and Joe Maddon. He’s also been the bench coach for the Marlins and spent five years as a manager in Miami’s minor league ranks.

Hyde checks one box on Boone’s coaching staff, but there’s still work to be done. Hitting coach James Rowson departed the organization and was hired by the Giants with the title of “offensive coordinator.” The Yankees interviewed former Dodgers hitting coach Aaron Bates after he unexpectedly left Los Angeles, but Bates has joined Ausmus in Houston as the new Astros’ hitting coach.
