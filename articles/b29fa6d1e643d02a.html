Fowl weather alert: Thanksgiving Day in NYC to bring cornucopia of wind and rain

By Rosemary Misdary · Gothamist · November 26, 2026

Break out the Scrabble board. Thanksgiving Day will be an indoor affair this year in New York City.

City leaders announced that Macy's would postpone its 100th annual Thanksgiving Day parade to Saturday and Mayor Zohran Mamdani warned families they may be hunkered down together for longer than planned this holiday.

"You may have to stay late," he said during a briefing alongside NYPD Commissioner Jessica Tisch. "There is no destination worth risking your safety."

Heavy rains are expected to accompany the strong winds. The downpour could begin late Thursday morning and may last through the night, forecasters say, dropping as much as 2 inches of rain.

Minor to moderate flooding is likely in coastal and flood-prone areas, officials warned, especially during the high tides at around 9 a.m. and 9 p.m. on Thursday.

"The worst conditions are expected later in the day," Mamdani said Wednesday evening, urging drivers to stay off the roads. "Winds will be strong enough [Thursday] to down tree limbs and power lines."

He warned people living in basement apartments to be especially vigilant.

"Make sure you know your exits and move to a higher floor," he said. "Never stay in or go back to a flooded basement."

There is a silver lining. The parade rain date on Saturday has a forecast that is mostly sunny and could reach highs in the mid-50s.

David Giambusso contributed reporting.
