Yankees Select Carlos Lagrange

By Charlie Wright · MLB Trade Rumors (Yankees) · November 17, 2026

4:45 pm: The Yankees have officially announced the move. The club also announced that left-hander Kyle Carr and outfielder Garrett Martin were added to the 40-man roster.

4:06 pm: The Yankees are expected to select the contract of right-hander Carlos Lagrange , per Francys Romero of BeisbolFR . Adding Lagrange to the 40-man roster protects him from the upcoming Rule 5 Draft.

Lagrange made the jump to Triple-A this past season. He struck out opponents at a near-30% clip, a skill he’s shown consistently in the minors. He had an 11.6% walk rate, also something he’s shown throughout his pro career. A spike in homers contributed to a 4.48 ERA.

Lagrange had worked almost exclusively as a starter until this season. He made a career-high eight relief appearances in 2026, compared to 11 starts. The additional versatility will give him a better chance to earn a spot on New York’s pitching staff. The Yankees have a deep group of starters headlined by reigning Cy Young winner Cam Schlittler . Gerrit Cole , Carlos Rodon , Max Fried , and Will Warren round out the rotation. Ryan Weathers and Clarke Schmidt are slated for bullpen roles, at least to start the year.

Carr was taken in the third round of the 2023 draft. After a decent start to the year at Double-A, he moved to Triple-A and struggled. Carr finished the season with a 4.58 ERA across 21 appearances (20 starts) between the two levels. He’s shown decent strikeout ability in the minors, but walks have tended to be an issue.

Martin, 26, had a solid campaign split between Double-A and Triple-A. He posted a 135 wRC+ with 24 homers and 18 stolen bases across both levels. He’s shown a solid mix of power and speed since joining the organization as a minor league free agent in 2023.
