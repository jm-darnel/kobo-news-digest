Frank DiLella's theater picks: 'Other Desert Cities,' 'Schmigadoon!' and more

By Adolfo Mena Cejas, Frank DiLella · NY1 · November 13, 2026

With the many entertainment choices New York City has to offer, it can be difficult to make a choice. Look no further. “On Stage” host Frank DiLella has curated a list of “must-see” shows this week, directing you to the best of Broadway, off-Broadway and beyond.

“Other Desert Cities”

Two of television’s most beloved dignitaries are holding court on Broadway. Allison Janney and Julia Louis-Dreyfus are currently starring as sisters on opposite ends of the political spectrum in the Broadway revival of “Other Desert Cities.” Written by Jon Robin Baitz, “Other Desert Cities” takes place over the holidays in Palm Springs, where a family gathering takes a dramatic turn when secrets are exposed.

”Schmigadoon!”

Over at The Nederlander Theatre, there’s a new doctor in the house. Actor Juan Pablo Di Pace from the TV series “Fuller House” and the reboot of “Dallas” is starring as “Doc” in “Schmigadoon!” He stopped by the “On Stage” studio to chat about his big Broadway debut.

“Pre-Existing Condition”

And finally, on off-Broadway over at the Greenwich House Theater, catch “Pre-Existing Condition” by Marin Ireland. The play explores the aftermath of an abusive relationship and the challenges of moving forward. “Pre-Existing Condition” features a rotating cast including Hari Nef, Edie Falco and Whitney White.

For the full schedule, and cast information, go to the website.
