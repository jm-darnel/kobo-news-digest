Brooklyn teen's missing cat found hiding after building collapse

By Spectrum News NY1 · NY1 · November 16, 2026

This Brooklyn cat may have used up one of its nine lives.

Isaac the cat went missing after the building next door to his apartment collapsed.

His owner, 13-year-old Zariah Coleman, had pleaded with officials to help rescue him — and they did.

A Department of Buildings staff member and a member of the nonprofit organization Flatbush Cats found Isaac around noon Sunday inside the second-floor apartment at 1323 Flatbush Avenue.

Isaac was safely brought out of the building and taken to a veterinarian for a checkup.

Zariah and her mother were waiting outside the apartment when officials emerged with the cat, allowing them a brief reunion.
