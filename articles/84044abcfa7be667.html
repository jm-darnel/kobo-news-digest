Yankees news: Catcher market is already drying up

By Andrés Chávez · Pinstripe Alley · November 27, 2026

NY Post | Mark W. Sánchez : The Yankees are open to bringing in a catcher, especially if he’s a good defender behind the plate and hits from the right side. They may need to hurry up, though, because the market might be drying up: Tyler Stephenson returned to the Reds, Gary Sánchez committed his future to the Marlins, and Ryan Jeffers accepted the Twins’ qualifying offer. Sánchez goes through some of the Yanks’ remaining options, including Carson Kelly, Austin Hedges, Kyle Higashioka, and more.

Heavy : Jonathan Loáisiga agreed to sign a two-year deal with the Yankees this week. As it turns out, according to New York Post’s Jon Heyman, the reliever actually turned down a more lucrative offer from the Mets to return to the Bronx.

NY Post | Greg Joyce : With Paul Goldschmidt signing a one-year deal with the Mets, he wraps up a productive two-year stint with the Yankees. The veteran first baseman revealed the reasons why he decided to move in a different direction. “My top concern was not, ‘Hey, how do I go to someplace where I get the most playing time?’ ” Goldschmidt said Wednesday morning. “My concern was going to a team where it felt right … and just have an opportunity to be on a team that has a chance to win and is going for it. Obviously, the Mets are in that.” The former NL MVP will likely platoon with Jared Young at first base in Queens.

MLB.com | Thomas Harrigan : Harrigan takes a look at the Yankees’ bullpen heading into 2027: signings already made, unsigned free agents, and where the unit stands at this moment, a few days until the looming lockout starts on Dec. 1.
