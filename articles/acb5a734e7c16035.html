David Bednar To Accept Qualifying Offer From Yankees

By Charlie Wright · MLB Trade Rumors (Yankees) · November 17, 2026

Closer David Bednar is expected to accept the $23.15MM qualifying offer from the Yankees, as first reported by Tyler Kepner of The Athletic .

It’s an unexpected development for Bednar, who was set to be one of the top available relievers on the market. He’ll skip the chance at a multi-year deal and return to New York. The Yankees have now retained their closer and one of their top lefties from last season after agreeing to a one-year deal with Tim Hill on Sunday.

Bednar wasn’t the only player to accept the QO. Twins catcher Ryan Jeffers also accepted it. The other six players to receive the offer declined it. One of them, Jake Bauers , already signed with a new team (Red Sox).

By agreeing to the $23.15MM contract, Bednar becomes the highest-paid reliever ever in terms of annual value. Dodgers closer Edwin Diaz set the mark last season with his three-year, $69MM contract ($23MM AAV). It’s a small edge for Bednar, but a record nonetheless.

Bednar delivered a strong campaign on the verge of free agency. He posted a 2.47 ERA across a career-high 69 1/3 innings. The righty saved 35 games as the top ninth-inning option in one of the best bullpens in the league. Bednar saw his elite strikeout rate fall to an above-average level (27.1%), but he also pushed his walk rate down to 7.1%.

General manager Brian Cashman has frequently talked this offseason about the bullpen being a priority. The plan right now seems to be running it back with the same group. Bednar and Hill have been retained, and the club is reportedly pursuing a reunion with Paul Blackburn . Fernando Cruz might be subtracted from the unit as he deals with a significant elbow injury. Outside of Cruz, it could be largely the same relief corps that ranked second in the league in ERA.

Bringing back Bednar could remove the Yankees from the race for the other expensive relievers on the market. Jorge Castillo of ESPN had reported that left-hander Adrian Morejon could become a target if Bednar ended up elsewhere. That scenario is now out the window. With a healthy rotation pushing Clarke Schmidt and Ryan Weathers into bullpen roles, the club doesn’t necessarily need another major addition.
