A new NYC Thanksgiving tradition: Stocking up at the legal weed dispensary

By James Ramsay · Gothamist · November 25, 2026

This column originally appeared in the Looped In NYC newsletter. Sign up here.

For many Americans, Thanksgiving promises two things: a feast on the table and an extended cast of relatives seated around it.

And for some, there’s one substance that can enhance the experience of both.

“I can't be around my family sober,” said Campbell Saverein, who was picking up weed at the SoHo dispensary Mighty Lucky before heading to Long Island for the holiday.

“It really helps me, personally,” she said. “ We get into political arguments — someone's mad at some skirt being too short on Instagram.”

“As somebody who's been selling cannabis illegally, then legally, Thanksgiving specifically was the biggest sales holiday for me in the entire year — bigger than 4/20,” said Beau Allulli, the store’s co-owner.

Veteran stoners, we learned, know their way around a Thanksgiving high. Pre-rolls. Edibles. Volcanoes. Dabs. A lemony bud that one customer said pairs well with her mashed potatoes.

“I have a recipe where I make it kind of like patatas bravas style, so lemony and spicy,” said Stephanie Villavicencio, who popped into the store while visiting from Miami, where recreational weed is illegal. (“I carry it in smell-proof things.”)

William Diaz, who celebrates Thanksgiving with his family in the Bronx, said there’s no need for a sneaky walk at this point — all the adults, ranging in age from 23 to 75, take part.

“We smoke first, before the turkey. And then pray. And then eat,” he said.

Hmac, an artist and weed delivery driver, said she’s “definitely getting high on Thanksgiving,” but the party starts even before the walk.

“The morning of any holiday in our family, we have killer-dillers and cinnamon rolls,” she said.

A killer-diller?

“It's like Orange Julius mixed with vodka,” she explained. “It's, like, game over. Starts your day off right. We get goofy, for sure.”

Getting goofy, if you can handle it, sounds fantastic. But say you’re an uninitiated cannabis user — surely you’d want to avoid paranoia, drowsiness or absently staring into the bowl of cranberry sauce. Is there a way into this for the uninitiated?

“If I was going to curate something for you, you'd start out with probably a low-dose sativa,” Allulli said, walking me through a case of products.

“And then maybe, right before dinner, go for a little bit of a CBG-combined edible. That will increase the appetite,” he said. “ And then, after dinner, for me, it would be smoking some sort of fat indica. Really something that's gonna just sort of, like, totally mellow me out.”
