New documentary revisits Mets’ emotional return after 9/11

On Sept. 21, 2001, just 10 days after the Sept. 11 attacks, Mike Piazza sent Shea Stadium into a frenzy with a home run during the first professional sports game in New York since the tragedy. The moment offered a brief sense of joy and togetherness for a city still reeling from the attacks.

A new documentary, “10 Days Later,” looks back at that night through the stories of the people who were there — inside and around the stadium. The film, directed by Celia Aniskovich, premieres next week at the DOC NYC festival.

Aniskovich joined “Mornings On 1” to discuss the film and the lasting significance of that night.
