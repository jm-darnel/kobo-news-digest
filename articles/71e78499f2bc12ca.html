What Democratic victories mean for New York politics

By Rocco Vertuccio · NY1 · November 8, 2026

New York’s political leaders gathered in Puerto Rico this week for the annual SOMOS conference, coming off major Democratic victories in Tuesday’s elections.

Gov. Kathy Hochul attended after winning reelection over Republican challenger Bruce Blakeman. Mayor Zohran Mamdani, U.S. House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries and state Assembly Speaker Carl Heastie were also among those who made the trip.

Jeffries is positioned to become the next speaker of the House of Representatives following Democratic gains nationwide. Heastie, meanwhile, is preparing to lead an Assembly with an expanded Democratic supermajority.

Boris Heersink, an associate professor of politics at Fordham University, joined "Weekends On 1" Sunday to discuss what the election results could mean for both parties and the political landscape ahead.
