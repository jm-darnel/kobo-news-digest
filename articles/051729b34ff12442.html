Council speaker weighs in on collapse, consumer protections

By Spectrum News NY1 · NY1 · November 12, 2026

City Council Speaker Julie Menin is addressing two major issues facing New Yorkers: the deadly building collapse in Brooklyn and a proposed crackdown on surveillance pricing.

She’s also backing a new consumer protection measure that would ban businesses from using personal data and algorithms to set individualized prices — a practice known as surveillance pricing.

It would empower the Department of Consumer and Worker Protection to fine businesses up to $1,000 per violation. It also includes a corrective-action provision for small businesses that promptly address violations, as well as exemptions for legitimate discounts, including buy-one-get-one-free offers and loyalty programs for veterans and students.

She joined “Mornings On 1” Thursday to discuss more.
