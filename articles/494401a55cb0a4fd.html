Today on Pinstripe Alley – 11/17/26

By Jake Devin · Pinstripe Alley · November 17, 2026

At first glance, this might seem like another ordinary November Tuesday, but peer closer, and you’ll see that today is a day of great import. The seven free agents that still have qualifying offers hanging out there have until 4 pm EST to make their decisions. On top of that, teams have until 6 pm today to shield players eligible for the Rule 5 Draft by adding them to the 40-man roster. Perhaps these just sound like boring logistical issues to you, but this is what will have to qualify for excitement for now.

We’ll have coverage of those key offseason deadlines today, with Nick on hand to report on David Bednar’s qualifying offer decision, and Peter set to discuss the Yankees’ 40-man decisions. Also, we’ll see the announcement of this season’s Silver Slugger winners, Peter grades Brent Headrick’s stellar 2026 campaign, and Jonathan wishes a happy 60th birthday to Jeff Nelson.
