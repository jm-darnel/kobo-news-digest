Princeton doctoral student from China detained by ICE in Washington, DC

By Arya Sundaram · Gothamist · November 20, 2026

A Princeton University doctoral student was detained by federal immigration agents at Reagan National Airport in Washington, D.C., on her way home from presenting research at a neuroscience conference, according to university officials and her colleagues.

Rodney D. Priestley, dean of Princeton’s Graduate School, said the university was arranging legal counsel for the student, Eva Yi Xie.

“News of Eva's detention is distressing for me and many members of the Princeton University community,” Priestley said in a statement. “We hope Eva will soon be able to resume her studies at Princeton.”

Xie is a third-year neuroscience doctoral student, according to her website. She graduated from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in May 2024. She emigrated from China in 2016, according to the Daily Princetonian, the university’s student newspaper, which first reported the news of Xie’s arrest.

“She went to a conference to present her work, and now she’s in a detention center,” the Brody Lab wrote on X. “She should be home.”
