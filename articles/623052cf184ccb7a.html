We Raise Hope Holds New York Gala In Support Of Healthcare And Education In Nepal

Harlem World · November 19, 2026

We Raise Hope, the nonprofit organization dedicated to improving the lives of underserved communities in rural Nepal, brought its mission of compassion, education and sustainable development to the heart of Manhattan with a charity gala at the prestigious Metropolitan Club.

Hosted by the organization and co-founded by Italian jewelry designer and philanthropist Alberto Luzzi, the evening united New York’s philanthropic community in support of initiatives expanding access to healthcare, education, clean water and economic opportunity in Nepal. Internationally recognized Himalayan spiritual leader and meditation master Yogmata Keiko Aikawa joined the occasion as special guest, bringing a personal connection to the region and its people.

For We Raise Hope co-founder Alberto Luzzi, the evening represented an opportunity to connect New York’s charitable community with the challenges and possibilities facing rural Nepal. The Italian-born entrepreneur, whose jewelry brand operates from a flagship store in Manhattan’s SoHo neighborhood, has brought his entrepreneurial experience and personal commitment to humanitarian causes to the organization. Under his leadership, We Raise Hope has supported initiatives addressing the interconnected needs of healthcare, education, infrastructure and economic opportunity, recognizing that lasting progress requires investment in the people and institutions that sustain communities.

“…devoted much of her life to promoting peace, compassion and human understanding…”

Adding an international spiritual and humanitarian dimension to the gathering was special guest Yogmata Keiko Aikawa, whose personal journey through the Himalayas and decades of charitable engagement closely align with the mission of We Raise Hope. The Japanese-born spiritual teacher, author and meditation master has devoted much of her life to promoting peace, compassion and human understanding through her teachings and philanthropic initiatives. Her extensive training in the Himalayas has shaped a philosophy emphasizing the connection between individual well-being, service to others and the responsibility to contribute positively to the wider world.

Notable attendees included: Alberto Luzzi, Katzie Guy Hamilton, Alexander Singh Grewal, Dr. Mahua Bose, Nic Canal, Agnes Kudikis, Jean Shafiroff, Yogmata Keiko Aikawa, and Keiko Aoki.

About We Raise Hope:

We Raise Hope is a New York-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to improving the lives of underserved communities in rural Nepal through sustainable development and community-led initiatives. Founded entirely by volunteers, the organization works with local leaders and partners to expand access to essential healthcare, education, clean water, technology and economic opportunity. Its initiatives include supporting Bodgaun Primary Hospital in Nepal’s Sindhupalchowk District, funding teachers and medical professionals, improving educational resources and digital literacy, and developing infrastructure that strengthens local communities. Through its commitment to long-term partnerships and sustainable solutions, We Raise Hope seeks to empower children and families, promote self-sufficiency and create lasting opportunities for future generations. About Alberto Luzzi:

Alberto Luzzi is a fine jewelry house in SoHo, New York, founded by Roman designer Alberto Luzzi and his business partner Nicolas Canal. Alberto Luzzi brings over 20 years’ experience in jewelry to collections designed in New York and crafted in Italy and Bali, ranging from sterling silver and gold vermeil, including the house’s signature silver mesh, to fine jewelry in 14K and 18K solid gold with diamonds, emeralds and aquamarines, with engagement rings and custom-made pieces. The flagship store is at 102 Thompson Street, SoHo, New York. About Yogmata Keiko Aikawa:

Born Yamanashi, Japan, 1945, Yogmata Keiko Aikawa has been interested in and practiced yoga, meditation and health regimens since her teenage. Becoming a yoga instructor, she held classes in many culture centers and actively taught asana yoga to thousands of students. At age 39, Yogmata first met Pilot Babaji, a prominent yoga and meditation master, who had visited Japan for a television program, and following which, he invited her for training in the Himalayas. Yogmata then traveled to the Himalayas and underwent authentic yoga training for the path to enlightenment, eventually achieving Samadhi, the supreme state of yoga and meditation.

Since 1991, Yogmata has now performed Public Samadhi, which is renowned as proof of enlightenment, 18 times in India, to promote world peace. And has received admiration from all over India. Currently, Yogmata travels the world to guide people to truly essential living through giving lectures and teaching on meditation.

In 2016, Yogmata gave a speech, as a guest of honor, at the International Yoga Day event held at the United Nations HQ, upon the request of the Indian government. In October 2016 and May 2017, she presented keynote lectures and taught meditation for Peace at events held in United Nations. Most recently, Yogmata delivered keynote addresses as a Guest of Honor at the United Nations Headquarters in New York on the Second World Meditation Day in December 2025 and the International Day of Yoga in June 2026. Yogmata is also renowned as an author and has sold over 1,000,000 copies of her books world-wide to-date. Her upcoming book Gateways to Nirvana: Meditations from a Himalayan Master will be released in March 2027.
