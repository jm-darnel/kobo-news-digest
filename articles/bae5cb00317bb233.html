1 puppy dead, another fighting for its life after dog family abandoned in NJ cold

By Catalina Gonella · Gothamist · November 22, 2026

An animal rescue group in New Jersey said it’s working to save a family of dogs found abandoned in the cold on Saturday.

"The remaining were freezing, alongside a frightened and protective mom and dad,” AHS said in the post. “The family was rushed to the shelter for immediate veterinary care and upon evaluation vets discovered one puppy suffering with critical injuries.”

Two of the surviving puppies are in foster care, according to AHS's social media post. The mother, whom the organization is calling Freya, and her other two puppies still need an experienced foster home, the group said.

AHS is seeking donations to help cover veterinary treatment, food and other supplies for the dogs.
