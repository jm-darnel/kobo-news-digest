Mariano Rivera Joins NYC Students For Youth-Led Health Conversation At NewYork-Presbyterian

Harlem World · November 24, 2026

Baseball legend Mariano Rivera recently participated in a youth-led, health-focused press conference led by local Upper Manhattan students from NewYork-Presbyterian’s youth development programs.

During the event on Nov. 14 at NewYork-Presbyterian/Columbia University Irving Medical Center, students from the Lang Youth Medical Program, The Uptown Hub and Turn 2 Us interviewed Rivera about his career, health, and experiences in professional baseball, including how he recovered from injuries. Rivera was also presented with an honorary white coat by the Division of Community and Population Health, and students got to meet and interact with both Rivera and VIP attendee Bartolo Colón.

“Giving young people the chance to engage directly with professional athletes can expand their sense of what’s possible and provide experiences they might not otherwise have,” says Davina Prabhu, vice president of the Ambulatory Care Network in the Division of Community and Population Health at NewYork-Presbyterian. “It reflects how NewYork-Presbyterian invests in young people as part of our broader commitment to the health and wellbeing of the communities we serve.”
