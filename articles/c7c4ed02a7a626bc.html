Rain on the parade: Expect a wet and windy Thanksgiving Day in NYC

By Ramsey Khalifeh · Gothamist · November 23, 2026

New York City is expecting rainfall, heavy winds and coastal flooding this Thursday, as families across the five boroughs prepare their Thanksgiving meals, according to the National Weather Service forecasts.

As is tradition, thousands will flock to Manhattan’s 6th Avenue to watch the Macy’s parade hoist up several-hundred-pound balloons. But NWS meteorologist Dave Radell warned of rain and wind that could shake up the festivities.

“The wind gusts are going to be ramping up really during the parade … If we start getting sustained winds certainly above 20, 30, 35 miles an hour, that is certainly going to have an impact on the parade itself,” Radell said.

“So for folks going to the parade, expect a windy and wet experience,” he added.

While Thursday’s storm will be particularly wet, it shouldn't top off any historical Thanksgiving records. The wettest Thanksgiving in terms of rainfall on record was in 2006, with 1.72 inches. Before that, 1985 tied with 1935 at 1.5 inches of rain.

As for the warmest Thanksgiving, in both 2007 and 1933, the temperature reached the upper 60s and almost 70 degrees. This Thursday’s forecast is much cooler, with highs in the 50s.
