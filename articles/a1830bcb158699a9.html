The NYC-DSA wants AOC to run for president. Will she?

By Shannon Ryan · NY1 · November 16, 2026

“If [Ocasio-Cortez] makes this choice [to run for president] she’s not coming into this alone,” Gustavo Gordillo, co-chair of NYC-DSA, told Spectrum News. “She has the foot soldier army that [the DSA has] built, in so many states.”

For months, Ocasio-Cortez has publicly mulled a U.S. Senate or presidential bid, promising to make a decision after the November election.

Gordillo said that he sees the overwhelmingly blue results as her green light.

“We think that the United States is at a turning point, and we need a representative from the democratic socialist movement to bring our agenda forward to the national stage,” he said.

A September Gallup poll found 43% of Americans now hold a positive view of socialism.

“I think the environment is as good as it’s ever going to get for a democratic socialist,” communications strategist Andrew Kirtzman said.

Kirtzman largely chalks the favorable environment for the DSA to President Donald Trump.

“It created an opening for parties such as the DSA to come in and say, you know, someone’s got to stand up to this guy,” he told Spectrum News.

Still, Kirtzman questions whether Ocasio-Cortez will be able to ensure that her own democratic socialist base stands with her as she works to moderate herself, in an effort to capture moderates and independents needed to clinch the presidency

“The biggest problem is the Democratic primary. She knows it,” Kirtzman said. “The problem [with] movement candidates is that followers demand ideological purity, and that’s going to be her problem. You cannot win the presidency running an ideologically pure campaign.”

When asked about Ocasio-Cortez’s attempts to moderate herself, Gordillo told Spectrum News, “For me, I think she is committed to a working-class economic agenda. And I think she does take steps to enlarge the coalition that is fighting for that agenda.” He added, “And I think that it’s welcome, it’s a welcome effort to expand the coalition that is going to get behind Medicare for all, that is going to get behind raising the minimum wage.”

DSA chapters in Los Angeles, Boston and Chicago have all passed similar resolutions encouraging Ocasio-Cortez to run.

Ocasio-Cortez’s team told Spectrum News Monday she is not ready to make an announcement on whether she plans to do so.

Last week, a “Ready for AOC” PAC launched. While separate from the NYC-DSA, the group is helmed by several DSA operatives.

“They have a goal of soliciting and collecting 1 million signatures across the country to propel AOC into the presidency and also to set up the groundwork and the field operation that can feed into a presidential campaign,” Gordillo explained.
