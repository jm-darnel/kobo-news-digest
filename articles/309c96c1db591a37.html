The Salvation Army And The CMA CGM Foundation Deliver 2,000 Thanksgiving Meals To Families in Harlem

Harlem World · November 13, 2026

In addition to turkeys, families received the essential ingredients for a traditional Thanksgiving meal, including mashed potatoes, gravy and other holiday staples, ensuring they had everything needed to prepare a special holiday meal.

Thanksgiving meals were distributed to neighbors on a first-come, first-served basis. The immense need in the community was evident even before the event began, as the first recipient arrived and camped out 12 hours ahead of the distribution to ensure he would receive a Thanksgiving meal.

“Every meal distributed is an opportunity to ease some of the financial pressure families face and provide support when it is needed most,” said Major Neek Fils Aime, leader of The Salvation Army’s Harlem Temple Community Center. “We feel incredibly blessed to continue serving our local community and are grateful for the continued partnership of the CMA CGM Foundation, whose generosity helps us make a meaningful difference in the lives of local families.”

“Giving back is at the heart of who we are. Through this partnership, we are not simply distributing meals. We are helping strengthen communities and create moments of dignity, connection and hope for families who need it most,” said Natasha Griffin, Chief Executive Officer of CMA CGM North America. “The dedication of The Salvation Army and our employee volunteers demonstrates what is possible when people come together with a shared purpose.”

Now in its seventh year, the partnership has evolved into a sustained community impact initiative that extends well beyond the holiday season. Running through 2027, the program supports monthly nutrition programs, food pantries, emergency food distributions and special holiday events. In New York City alone, the CMA CGM Foundation and The Salvation Army expect to provide approximately 100,000 meals throughout the year, contributing to a national goal of more than 500,000 meals for individuals and families facing food insecurity.

About the CMA CGM Foundation

Founded in 2005 and chaired by Tanya Saadé Zeenny, the CMA CGM Foundation focuses on humanitarian action and access to education. It embodies and translates the family values and solidarity of the CMA CGM Group, a global leader in transport and logistics, into concrete actions. In response to crises, the Foundation leverages the expertise of the CMA CGM Group to deliver essential humanitarian supplies. It also supports access to quality education, professional training and integration, as well as educational and social innovation. To date, the Foundation has transported 180,000 tonnes of humanitarian aid to 117 countries, supported more than 630 projects in France, Lebanon and around the world, and assisted 67 entrepreneurs through its social incubator, Le Phare.
