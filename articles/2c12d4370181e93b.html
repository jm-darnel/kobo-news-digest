Jazz Age fashions return to Governor’s Island for the Lawn Party's fall edition

By Stephanie Keith · Gothamist · November 8, 2026

On a brisk autumn day, ferries to Governor’s Island filled with feathers, silk crepe, chiffon and wool tweed of passengers headed to the Jazz Age Lawn Party, a Roaring ‘20s-inspired event celebrating its 21st anniversary with a special autumn edition.

Participants spread their blankets on the ground under a canopy of graceful sycamores whose leaves were a bright yellow and orange. A large dance floor had been laid out where host and founder Michael Arenella played jazz with his Dreamland Orchestra.
