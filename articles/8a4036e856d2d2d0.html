Building a Stronger Economic Future for New York City

Operating under the slogan “NYC’s Voice for Minority Small Business,” NYCMSBCC is positioning itself as an advocate for entrepreneurs seeking greater access to capital, business education, mentorship, procurement opportunities and sustainable business growth.

Together, these organizations represent a growing network dedicated to entrepreneurship, mentorship, economic development and community advancement.

Experienced Leadership Driving a New Business Movement

NYCMSBCC was founded by Phil Andrews, a longtime business advocate and president of the Long Island African American Chamber of Commerce, Inc.

Andrews brings extensive chamber leadership experience, including initiatives celebrating Minority Enterprise Development Month, National Small Business Week, minority-owned media, entrepreneurship education and business networking.

His work has focused on building partnerships that connect small businesses with financial institutions, corporate leaders, government agencies and educational resources.

“The success of our Times Square gathering reflects the tremendous potential that exists when entrepreneurs, business leaders and community organizations come together around a shared vision,” Andrews said. “Our mission is to ensure that minority and women business owners have access to the relationships, resources and opportunities necessary to compete, grow and succeed in one of the world’s most dynamic business markets.”

Expanding Opportunities Beyond Times Square

Building on the momentum of the event, NYCMSBCC plans to advance initiatives focused on business education, access to capital, supplier diversity, mentoring, professional networking and advocacy.

These initiatives are intended to help established businesses expand while creating pathways for emerging entrepreneurs and the next generation of business leaders.

A Call for Membership

NYCMSBCC invites minority-owned, women-owned and other small businesses, entrepreneurs, corporations and professional service providers throughout the five boroughs to become part of its growing business network.

Membership offers opportunities to build valuable relationships, participate in educational programming, increase business visibility and contribute to a collective voice advocating for small business growth.
