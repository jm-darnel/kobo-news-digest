Yankees sign lefty reliever Brent Suter

By Joshua Diemert · Pinstripe Alley · November 23, 2026

After agreeing to terms with Tim Hill last week , the Yankees added another lefty to the relief corps on Monday, signing Brent Suter to a one-year $3.75 million contract, plus a club option for 2027. That option is worth $3.5 million and carries a quarter-million buyout. The veteran southpaw just completed his 10th year in MLB, splitting time between the Angels and Atlanta, going South at the Trade Deadline to help that club lock up the NL East.

In 91 innings, 54 appearances, Suter had a 3.36 ERA and 3.53 FIP while reliably getting more than three outs at a time. He doesn’t feature a lot of swing-and-miss, which is something of a calling card for the Yankees lately, but boasts some of the best contact control in the sport. A sinker-changeup combination has lead Suter to be in the bright red sections of his Statcast chart in exit velocity, hard hit, and groundball rates, with 99th percentile extension playing up hit fastball offerings that sit below 90 mph.

Suter’s a very old school kind of pitcher, and aesthetically at least should be a fun pairing with Hill, something of a vintage gunslinger himself. He also responded very positively after being dealt away from the Angels, and that should encourage fans that when paired with a team that understands how to develop pitching, especially relief pitching, he should be able to step up a little more.
