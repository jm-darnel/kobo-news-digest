City to demolish Brooklyn building damaged in deadly Flatbush collapse

By Phil Corso · Gothamist · November 18, 2026

New York City officials say they will demolish a Brooklyn apartment building that was severely damaged in last week's collapse of a neighboring building that killed eight people.

“Given the dangerous condition of the building and the imperative to make sure no one else gets hurt, ordering for the controlled safe demolition of the building is the only available option,” Buildings Commissioner Ahmed Tigani said in a statement.

Officials said tenants also cannot return to retrieve their belongings because of unstable floors and asbestos contamination.

City officials said displaced tenants are receiving hotel accommodations through the American Red Cross through at least the end of November.
