Businessman John Catsimatidis reacts to Republican losses in midterm elections

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 16, 2026

As Democrats prepare to take control of Congress in January, Republicans are reckoning with their losses and considering what comes next for the party.

Businessman John Catsimatidis, the owner of the Gristedes supermarket chain and chairman of the Red Apple Group, joined NY1 political anchor Errol Louis on “Inside City Hall” Monday to talk about the party’s losses.

He was a candidate for mayor in 2013, and his daughter, Andrea Catsimatidis, is chair of the Manhattan Republican Party.
