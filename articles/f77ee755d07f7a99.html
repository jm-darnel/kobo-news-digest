Frank DiLella's theater picks: 'Wanted,' 'Midnight' and more

With the many entertainment choices New York City has to offer, it can be difficult to make a choice. Look no further. “On Stage” host Frank DiLella has curated a list of “must-see” shows this week, directing you to the best of Broadway, off-Broadway and beyond.

"Wanted"

First up, there’s a new musical on the way to Broadway. “Wanted” is now in previews at the James Earl Jones Theatre.

Inspired by a true story, the show centers on Mary and Martha Clarke, twin sisters on the run who are determined to protect their family’s legacy.

“Wanted” officially opens on Broadway Nov. 8.

"Midnight"

Heading now to the Off-Broadway scene, check out “Midnight” at the Daryl Roth Theatre.

Writer, performer, director and choreographer Todrick Hall is a musical force of nature. His latest show is playing to sold-out crowds, with Hall starring as Rail opposite musical powerhouse Ayana George as Ethel.

Hall and George sat down with “On Stage” host Frank DiLella to talk about the production and the special connections it has created with audiences.

"The Exhibitionist"

And finally, down at the SoHo Playhouse, don’t miss Emmy Award winner Jeff Hiller in the new solo comedy “The Exhibitionist.”

Hiller plays Justin, described as the McMillon Museum of Art’s oldest and most charming intern, a man pursuing his lifelong dream of becoming an artist.

Hiller stopped by the “On Stage” studio to talk with DiLella about his return to the stage.
