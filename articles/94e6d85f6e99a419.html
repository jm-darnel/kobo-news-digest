What’s open and closed in NYC on Veterans Day 2026

By Spectrum News NY1 · NY1 · November 11, 2026

Veterans Day is Wednesday, Nov. 11, honoring the men and women who have served in the U.S. military.

Here’s what to know about what’s open and closed across New York City.

Public transit

Subways, buses, the Long Island Rail Road and Metro-North Railroad will run, though riders should check for any planned service changes before traveling.

New York Waterway ferry services will run on a regular schedule. Active-duty military members and veterans with valid military or veteran identification can ride for free on all routes.

Alternate side parking

Alternate side parking rules will be suspended for the holiday.

Parking meters will remain in effect, as will other parking regulations, including no stopping, no standing and no parking restrictions that are in effect seven days a week.

Sanitation

There will be no trash, recycling or curbside compost collection on Wednesday.

Residents who normally receive Wednesday collection should put their materials at the curb Wednesday evening for collection beginning Thursday, Nov. 12.

Government offices

All government offices will be closed for the federal holiday, including the Department of Motor Vehicles, city and state courts and Social Security offices.

Schools and libraries

New York City public schools will be closed on Veterans Day.

All branches of the city's public library systems will also be closed Wednesday.

Post offices

Post offices will be closed on Veterans Day, and there will be no regular mail delivery, according to the U.S. Postal Service.

Regular mail delivery and retail services will resume Thursday, Nov. 12.

Banks and financial markets

Most banks will be closed for the federal holiday.

However, the New York Stock Exchange and Nasdaq will remain open and operate on their regular trading schedules.

Veterans Day Parade

Drivers should expect street closures around the parade route, including:

• Fifth Avenue between East 23rd Street and West 45th Street

• Broadway between East 23rd Street and West 29th Street

• West 24th Street between Seventh Avenue and Fifth Avenue

• West 25th Street between Sixth Avenue and Fifth Avenue

• East and West 26th, 27th and 28th streets around Madison Square Park

Additional nearby streets may be closed at the discretion of the NYPD.
