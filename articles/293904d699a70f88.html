Former NYPD detective ordered held in jail in fatal George Washington Bridge shooting

By Ben Feuerherd · Gothamist · November 23, 2026

A New York City judge on Monday ordered a former NYPD detective to be held in jail until his trial in the killing of a man during an apparent road rage incident on the George Washington Bridge.

A Manhattan grand jury indicted Victor Romero on a second-degree murder charge for the Oct. 20 killing, according to the district attorney’s office.

Dozens of people came to court to support the man who was killed, Koran Irvine. They marched through the hallways outside the courtroom before the court appearance, and cheered after the judge ordered Romero held in jail.

Romero, 62, who was an NYPD detective before he retired in 2001, has pleaded not guilty. His attorney, Joel Cohen, declined to comment Monday.

Prosecutors said Romero fired at Irvine’s car on the George Washington Bridge after their vehicles nearly collided as they both approached the upper-level onramp.

After the shooting, prosecutors say, Romero may have tried to obstruct the investigation into the killing by wiping down the driver’s side of his car at a gas station and by cleaning the inside center console of the car.

Prosecutors said Romero gave police a revolver after the shooting, saying that was the gun he usually carried with him. However they say the murder weapon was a different gun: a 9mm pistol they found in Romero’s home.
