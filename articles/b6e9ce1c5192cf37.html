Yankees news: Cody Bellinger wins Platinum Glove honors

SABR : The Yankees’ Cody Bellinger was already awarded his second career Gold Glove for his excellent work in left field in 2026, but now he’s taken home an even higher honor. Since 2011, Rawlings has also given out one Platinum Glove per league each year to honor the best of the best. Until last night, 2022 catcher Jose Trevino was the only Yankees player to take home the award. Now, they have two, as Bellinger was named the AL’s best defender, joining the truly outstanding Pete Crow-Armstrong, the NL winner and likely MVP. Congrats to Cody!

MLB.com | Bryan Hoch : Elite pitching, lackluster bats; such was the story of the 2026 Yankees. If the 2027 squad is to progress further into the postseason, offensive upgrades are a must. Here, Hoch examines a number of positions where the Bombers should seek more thump. To my eyes, the position that is most in need is also the spot with the most intriguing option – third base, where Ryan McMahon stunk it up this past year, and where NPB slugger Teruaki Sato is seeking to make a splash in free agency. It’s been a hot minute since the Yankees acquired NPB talent, so I’m not sure how seriously the Yankees will participate in the Sato sweepstakes (especially if they’re unsure about his glovework at the hot corner), but a blogger can hope.

FanGraphs | Eric Longenhagen : If you want more info on Teruaki Sato, Eric Longenhagen has you covered. The FanGraphs lead prospect analyst covered Sato as part of his overview on notable players from the NPB and KBO, had this to say about the 2026 Central League Triple Crown winner – “Though Sato isn’t a five-tool superstar – he’s going to strike out a lot and play below-average defense – he should hit for enough power to be a productive, heart-of-the-order power hitter with 25-30 home runs.”

Another potential upgrade for the Yankees, albeit at second base, is Shugo Maki of the Yokohama BayStars, who expressed his desire to play stateside last offseason, though he hasn’t been officially posted yet. Says Longenhagen: “Maki projects as an everyday second baseman in the Brandon Lowe mold, and you could make a pretty good argument that his hit-tool stability (Maki has a single-digit miss rate against fastballs compared to Sato’s whopping 32 percent) makes him a superior “prospect” when compared to Sato.”

NJ.com | Bob Klapisch : With yet another postseason run cut short, Yankees fans are understandably frustrated with Brian Cashman. However, former Athletics GM Billy Beane of “Moneyball” fame considers Cashman to be “one of the best that’s ever done this job. Period.” While your mileage may vary on Cashman’s maneuvering in recent years, one must acknowledge that his overall body of work remains very impressive. If you’re up in arms about Hal Steinbrenner handing Cashman another extension, maybe Beane’s words can offer you some reassurance. Or not.

Lastly, in case you missed Madison’s column , the Yankees are down a key member of the 2026 coaching staff. Tanner Swanson has gained a reputation since being hired away from the Twins in 2020 as one of the best catching instructors in baseball. As noted above Trevino became a Platinum Glove winner under his watch, and most impressively, he’s helped transform Austin Wells from a guy who might not stick behind the plate into a legitimately great defensive catcher who was a Gold Glove finalist himself in 2026. But his contract was up, and he’ll be heading to the Pacific Northwest because new Mariners skipper Jason Kanzler offered the Seattle native a job as his bench coach. Best of luck to him — and to the Yankees on finding his replacement.
