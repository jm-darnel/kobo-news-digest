New York NAACP Responds As Jeffries Secures Votes For House Speakership

Harlem World · November 18, 2026

In response to Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries securing the votes to become speaker of the U.S. House of Representatives for the 120th Congress, the NAACP New York State Conference issued the following statement:

“House Democrats took an important step forward in selecting New York’s own Hakeem Jeffries to serve as the next speaker of the U.S. House of Representatives. There’s still work to be done between now and January, but this preliminary vote and the shift in power in Congress, governorships, and legislatures across the country signal a renewed call from voters to tackle the issues that matter most to them.

“From lowering the cost of living and expanding affordable housing to strengthening public education and health care, Leader Jeffries and Congress have a robust agenda to deliver on behalf of New Yorkers and the American people. While most voters have these shared concerns, we know any successful approach to addressing those challenges must take into account the unique needs of Black New Yorkers and other communities.

“In that respect, we express full confidence in Leader Jeffries and expect his leadership to deliver tangible results for New Yorkers across the state.

“As Leader Jeffries prepares to formally take the gavel in January, we look forward to working alongside him to ensure the needs of Black New Yorkers are placed front and center. Even after this first step, our work is far from over. We will remain committed to staying engaged, organized, and united to hold Washington accountable and deliver economic opportunity for our communities in 2027 and beyond.”
