Delta delays flights ahead of Thanksgiving holiday over system failure

By Giulia Heyward · Gothamist · November 22, 2026

A tech snafu at one of the world’s busiest airports on Sunday is delaying thousands of Delta Airlines flights headed ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday.

An issue with Delta Airlines’ weight and balance system at Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport is responsible for the delay at one of the busiest airports in the country, the Federal Aviation Administration said in a statement posted on social media.

More than 3,550 flights traveling within, into or out of the U.S. have been delayed as of Sunday afternoon, according to Flight Aware.

“Delta is working to resolve issues with the airline’s weight and balance automation system,” the FAA statement said. “These delays are not related to any FAA air traffic systems.”

Delta said it is working limit congestion at its Atlanta hub.

“Delta teams are using manual workarounds to safely get flights departed while work continues to restore automation,” said Delta spokesperson Natalie Duggan. “We thank our customers for their patience and our people for their professionalism."

Other recent technological malfunctions have impacted other industries, including online banking systems and Microsoft Outlook.

Delta Airlines did not immediately respond to a request for comment from Gothamist.
