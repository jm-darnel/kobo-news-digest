What to know about diabetes during American Diabetes Month

By Rocco Vertuccio · NY1 · November 15, 2026

November is American Diabetes Month, a time to raise awareness about a disease affecting millions of people across the country.

More than 40 million Americans — about 12% of the population — are living with diabetes, according to the American Diabetes Association. The impact is especially significant among older adults, with nearly 29% of Americans age 65 and older diagnosed with the disease. An estimated 364,000 children and teens under 20 are also living with diagnosed diabetes, and about 1.5 million Americans are diagnosed each year.

Meanwhile, more than 115 million adults in the U.S. are living with prediabetes.

Amy Huelle, director of Diabetes Services and a certified diabetes care and education specialist at Maimonides Health, joined “Weekends On 1” to discuss diabetes prevention, management and the importance of awareness.
