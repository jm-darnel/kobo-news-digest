Family creates DIY Macy’s parade after postponement

By Emma Barnett · NY1 · November 26, 2026

“I know that this change of plans will cause inconveniences, yet safety has to come first,” Mayor Zohran Mamdani said.

Officials said the balloons will be prepared again ahead of Saturday’s rescheduled parade.

For many tourists who planned their Thanksgiving trips around the event, the postponement was a major disappointment.

“We came all the way from Corpus Christi, Texas, over 1,600 miles,” said one visitor. “As you can imagine, we were extremely upset that it had been postponed.”

Another visitor said the timing meant they would miss the parade entirely.

“I’m from Portland, Oregon,” the visitor said. “I was sad because we’re going to leave on Saturday.”

But not everyone let the postponement dampen their Thanksgiving spirit.

“I decided the parade still has to happen, even if it’s not Macy’s,” Joshua Androphy, a Manhattan resident, said.

“We have a marching band. We have our cheerleader. We have our Minion sitting on the bananas,” said Dina Mann, a Manhattan resident. “We just marched down. We sang. We played marching band music using our phone, just DIY. We invited people to come, but we kind of got a lot of side-eye.”

“We weren’t going to let the rain stop us,” Nat Androphy said. “We came here. We are at Macy’s Herald Square. Thanksgiving, Happy Thanksgiving. Eat some turkey. Gobble, gobble, gobble.”

NYPD Commissioner Jessica Tisch said more than 1,000 officers who were originally scheduled to be off Saturday will be called in to help facilitate the event.
