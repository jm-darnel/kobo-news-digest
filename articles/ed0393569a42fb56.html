Mamdani recovering after surgery to repair torn Achilles

By Jill Webb · Gothamist · November 28, 2026

Mayor Zohran Mamdani is recovering in Gracie Mansion after successfully undergoing surgery on Friday to repair an Achilles tendon injured on the soccer pitch last week, City Hall spokesperson said.

“The surgery was successful, and the mayor is expected to make a full recovery,” Calvello said in the statement. “He was discharged from the hospital and is now resting comfortably at Gracie Mansion.”

He added, “We are extremely grateful for the first-class care the mayor received from the medical staff today.”

Mamdani, 35, sustained the non-contact injury in a soccer match last week between City Hall staffers and members of the media. The injury is expected to limit the mayor's movement for months to come.

Mamdani arrived late to the Nov. 19 game at an indoor soccer field in the Financial District. He later told reporters he didn’t stretch before playing.

“I forgot that I was 35,” he said.

He said ahead of the procedure he expected to wear a protective boot for six to eight weeks and won’t be able to play soccer again for nine months.

First Deputy Mayor Dean Fuleihan is leading day-to-day operations today, Calvello said.
