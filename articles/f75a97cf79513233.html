Bronx vigil honors 15-year-old Javits Frimpong after fatal shooting

A vigil was held near East 171st Street and Crotona Place for 15-year-old Javits Frimpong, who police said was shot there last week while on his way to school and later died at a hospital.

A week after the shooting, classmates, clergy and members of the Ghanaian community gathered at the same intersection to remember the teenager, who immigrated from Ghana. People released balloons and called for justice.

“He was a sweet boy. Smart, very respectful, never bothered anyone,” Ms. K., Frimpong’ teacher, said.

One classmate addressed the loss during the vigil.

“You came into this world with a future of possibilities, but your journey ended far too soon,” the classmate said.

A witness to the shooting said he stayed with Javits in his final hours.

“I was glad I was able to be with him in his last hours,” the witness said. “I’m glad somebody was there to make him feel comfortable. And to let him know that he was not alone.”

Police have not announced any arrests.

At the vigil, those gathered chanted: “No justice. No peace.”

“Javits, you ran for your life, run, run, run with God,” one speaker said. “Just keep running until we get it right.”
