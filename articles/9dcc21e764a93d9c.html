Mamdani expands free produce programs for New Yorkers on SNAP

By Karen Yi · Gothamist · November 8, 2026

“Every New Yorker should be able to afford a well-balanced, healthy diet,” Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services Dr. Helen Arteaga said in a statement. “But rising grocery prices have put fresh fruits and vegetables out of reach for far too many families.”

Get the Good Stuff lets SNAP recipients get a dollar for dollar match for every dollar they spend on fresh or frozen fruits, vegetables and beans. The program offers up to $10 dollars cash back at participating grocery stores. The program is currently available at 21 supermarkets but city officials said they would fund another 12 grocery stores interested in participating. Stores can apply through Nov. 20.

“Fruits, vegetables, and beans are important to a balanced diet and help reduce the risk of diet-related diseases. But with the rise in grocery prices, they aren't always affordable,” said NYC Health Commissioner Dr. Alister F. Martin.

New Yorkers can look up participating grocery stores and find their nearest farmer’s market on the city’s health map.
