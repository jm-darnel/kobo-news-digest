Work from legendary artists on view for free at Christie's

By Roger Clark · NY1 · November 9, 2026

When the owner passed away, family members reached out to Christie’s auction house.

“It doesn’t often happen its kind of what all of us specialists dreamed of, you know, there’s a little bit of Indiana Jones in all of us, so to go to someone’s home and open a box and see an O’Keeffe that has been at the bottom of a closet for 40 years is just extraordinary,” Tylee Abbott, the international director of American art at Christie’s, said.

It’s just one of the stories for Fall Marquee Week at Christie’s in Rockefeller Center, with artwork from many of the greats on display for free through Nov. 17 in advance of some big auctions.

“There’s so much to learn here, the team is always excited to speak with people who love art whether or not you are a buyer or not, and even if you are just starting out, there really is something for everyone,” Vanessa Fusco, the head of the Impressionist and Modern Art Department at Christie’s New York, said.

“When you go up close and really get a chance to get a look at it and understand the brushwork and the color, he makes such incredible bold color choices. And so we really welcome visitors to come and get an up close look at this incredible work,” Fusco said.
