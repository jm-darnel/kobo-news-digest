Death toll reaches 8 after Brooklyn building collapse, officials say

By Spectrum News NY1 · NY1 · November 12, 2026

In a post on social media, Mamdani said rescue workers spent the night searching the site under extremely dangerous conditions for anyone who may still be trapped.

At least 19 people have been injured, including three firefighters, according to the FDNY. The department tallied a total of 27 total patients as of 8 a.m. including eight people pronounced dead, four taken to area hospitals in critical condition, five with serious but non-life-threatening injuries and 10 with minor injuries.

"There are no words for a loss of this magnitude," Mamdani wrote. “My heart is with the families and loved ones of those we have lost, those who are injured and everyone grieving today.”

Rescue operations remained ongoing Thursday morning. Mamdani urged people to stay clear of the area and said anyone unable to reach someone they believe may have been affected by the collapse should call 311.

At a news conference Wednesday night, FDNY Commissioner Lillian Bonsignore said operations were likely to continue for hours.

"We know that we are not finished here, and we will likely not be finished for quite some time," Bonsignore said.

Mamdani described the rescue efforts as "an incredibly challenging and fast-moving situation, with precarious wreckage to navigate and dangerous voids beneath the surface."

A service center is scheduled to open at 9 a.m. Thursday at the YMCA Flatbush, where residents will be able to connect with government and nonprofit agencies for additional assistance.
