Police found a deceased person with multiple stab wounds inside a building in Queens on Friday, officials said.

Emergency responders pronounced her dead at the scene.

There were no arrests as of Friday night, and police hadn't yet described any suspects. Police also had not identified the woman or provided an estimate of her age. Police said the incident was still under investigation.
