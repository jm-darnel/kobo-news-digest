Yankees to hire Brandon Hyde as bench coach

By Matt Ferenchick · Pinstripe Alley · November 25, 2026

Going into this offseason, the Yankees had some spots open not only on the roster, but also on the coaching staff. As of late Wednesday morning, it appears the Yankees have filled one of the coaching openings (though they still need a new hitting coach).

Former Orioles’ manager Brandon Hyde has reportedly been hired by the team as the new bench coach, taking the place of Brad Ausmus.

Hyde managed the Orioles from 2019-25, compiling a record of 421-492. The beginning of his tenure there coincided with Baltimore’s complete teardown, so a couple of his early seasons make that overall record a bit worse than it actually was. He was there as the Orioles returned to the postseason in 2023 and 2024, winning AL Manager of the Year honors for 102 wins in the breakout 2023 (Baltimore’s most since 1980).

However, Hyde was let go after a bad start in 2025, as the O’s have been unable to build on all the young talent they seemingly had during those two playoff campaigns. This past season, Hyde served as an advisor in the Rays’ organization during their run to the AL pennant.

In addition to it being notable that the Yankees have hired a former manager to the bench coach role, Hyde and the Yankees did have some previous awkward history. During a 2024 game, Hyde was visibly yelling at the Yankee dugout during a bench-clearing incident after the O’s Heston Kjerstad was hit with a pitch. By all accounts, the situation got calmed down the next day , but it’s always amusing when former enemies team up.

Hyde will be replacing the aforementioned Ausmus, who was hired by the Astros for their manager position earlier this offseason. He’ll be the fourth bench coach to serve under Boone, following Josh Bard, Carlos Mendoza, and Ausmus.
