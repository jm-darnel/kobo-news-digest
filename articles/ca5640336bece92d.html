A Buried Secret Ignites A Reckoning In “Reparations” At Harlem Stage

Harlem World · November 10, 2026

Harlem Stage presents James Sheldon’s two-time Best Play Award-winning drama, Reparations, in partnership with The Billie Holiday Theatre, directed by Harlem Stage CEO & Artistic Director Indira Etwaroo.

A night of passion between a White book editor and a younger Black writer takes a dangerous turn when he confronts her with a secret that could destroy her life, while setting him free. An explosive reckoning with race, privilege, betrayal, and the price of justice, Reparations poses unsettling questions that defy easy answers: Who determines what is owed? How does the past shape the choices of the present? And what happens when abstract arguments about justice become intensely personal?

Never miss new stories in Harlem

“At a moment when the distance between those who hold power and those whose lives are shaped by it has never felt wider, I cannot imagine a more urgent time to tell this story,” said Etwaroo. “Theater has always held a mirror up to society. Reparations asks what repair truly demands when the wound runs deeper than money and the privileges born of injustice endure. Harlem Stage is proud to be joined by The Billie Holiday Theatre in holding up that mirror.”

Harlem Stage is located at 150 Convent Avenue, New York, New York 10031. For more information and the full performance schedule, visit: www.HarlemStage.org.

Community Conversations

Hearing directly from the community is at the heart of this journey. Audiences will be invited into intimate conversations at Harlem Stage and The Billie Holiday Theatre, led by scholars, thought leaders, artists, and change makers. Together, these conversations ensure that Reparations is not only a performance, but also a platform where communities tell their own stories, connect across generations, and affirm that the fight for justice is shared and ongoing.

Student Access Initiative

Buy One. Give One: Every ticket purchased provides a NYC student with free access to a transformative live performance. Harlem Stage’s Student Matinee Program includes a teaching artist-led pre-show workshop, a free healthy lunch, post-show conversation with artists, and educational guides exploring the historical and sociocultural dimensions of each work, aligned with NYC’s Blueprint for Teaching and Learning in the Arts.

Harlem Stage

For more than forty-three years, Harlem Stage has been held up by many hands: the teacher who brought her students, the young dancer who found her first stage here, the artist who was told no everywhere else and heard yes in Harlem. We the People of Harlem, of New York, of the Global Majority created this STAGE as a deliberate act of vision and resistance. Harlem Stage stands with its community and insists that our stories endure.

We the People. We commission. We build. We present. We raise up. We send the work out into the world. Generations of artists, audiences, and students have come through these doors and, from inside these walls, redrawn the boundaries of dance, music, theater, film, and performance. This is what We the People can build when we are given a STAGE, and so long as this STAGE stands, our stories shall not perish from the earth.

In fall 2024, Dr. Indira Etwaroo assumed the role of CEO & Artistic Director of Harlem Stage, following the extraordinary 25-year tenure of Patricia Cruz. Etwaroo’s leadership is grounded in the conviction that artists must have the resources, agency, and institutional support to illuminate histories too often obscured, recover narratives excluded from the dominant cultural record, and expand our collective understanding of the national and global human experience. Central to this vision is an equally fundamental principle: access to art and culture is not a privilege conferred upon a select few, but a right that belongs to all people.
