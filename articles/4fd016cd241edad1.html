Valdez explains why she voted against Jeffries for House speaker

By Shannon Ryan · NY1 · November 20, 2026

U.S. House Democrats made history this week, nominating Hakeem Jeffries to become the first Black speaker of the House in an overwhelming 251–11 vote.

Among the 11 Democrats who broke with the majority were two incoming New York City congresswomen: Darializa Avila Chevalier and Claire Valdez.

Five of the seven signatories, including Avila Chevalier and Valdez, are members of the Democratic Socialists of America.

NY1’s Shannon Ryan interviewed Valdez about her decision.
