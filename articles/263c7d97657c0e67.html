For those who don’t know what Somos is, it’s a very overstimulating and overwhelming event that seems to get bigger every year. It’s a political junket. It’s a networking event. It’s a service opportunity and it’s a chance for a lot of people to go down in Puerto Rico, a place New York City has very close ties to.

Katie Honan sends an audio postcard recapping her reporting trip to Puerto Rico, while Alex Brook Lynn calls in from Paris to help explain the ongoing street protests there.

Plus Christina Greer and Harry Siegel talk about deadly lithium-ion battery fires, ICE’s push for migrant shelters records and much more right here in New York City.
