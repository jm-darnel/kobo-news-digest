Today on Pinstripe Alley – 11/25/26

By Jake Devin · Pinstripe Alley · November 25, 2026

Well, any questions about whether there’d be a pre-lockout feeding frenzy have been answered, and they’ve been answered in the affirmative. Tarik Skubal’s signing with the Mets appears to have opened up the floodgates; Freddy Peralta, Trevor Rogers, Kris Bubic, Teruaki Sato, and Kaima Taira all inked significant deals over the last two days. It was a fun flurry activity, one that saw a lot of cash splashed around by a number of teams. Does this really seem like a sport that should be heading into an owner-imposed lockout over economic concerns? Who can say.

On the site today, Josh will discuss some of the activity from this week and what it suggests about the chances of a salary cap being implemented, and he’ll also provide the grade on Ben Rice’s 2026 season. Later, as part of our Yankee Birthday series, we have a feature on one of the greats, as Kevin writes up the one and only Joe DiMaggio, with a focus on the Yankee Clipper’s salary holdout early in his career.

Questions/Prompts:
