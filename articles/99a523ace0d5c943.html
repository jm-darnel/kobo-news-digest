Red Sox Adding Shelley Duncan To Coaching Staff

Duncan, 47, played in parts of seven big league seasons from 2007-13, totaling 330 games and 997 plate appearances as a first baseman and corner outfielder. He’s had a long coaching career in the more than decade that has passed since he called it quits as a player.

Boston cleaned house on its coaching staff back in April, firing then-manager Alex Cora and five members of his staff on the heels of a terrible start to the season. Their own Triple-A skipper, Chad Tracy, was elevated to the big league manager’s role on an interim basis. After the Red Sox embarked on a remarkable turnaround under Tracy and made the postseason, Tracy was signed to a three-year contract as the Red Sox’ new skipper, shedding the “interim” label.
