Mamdani tears Achilles in soccer match against NYC press corps, will be out for the season

By Elizabeth Kim, Clayton Guse · Gothamist · November 20, 2026

Mayor Zohran Mamdani tore his Achilles tendon during a soccer game between his staffers and members of the city’s press corps Thursday night.

Mamdani arrived late to the 7 p.m. game at an indoor soccer field in the Financial District. He said he did not stretch before playing.

“I forgot that I was 35,” he said.

It’s a season-ending injury. He said he’ll be in a boot for six to eight weeks and won’t be able to play soccer again for nine months. Dora Pekec, a mayoral spokesperson, said it was “TBD” whether Mamdani would require surgery.

Mamdani — who was the captain of his high school soccer team during his senior year at Bronx Science — scored a goal shortly after entering the game. He logged an extended amount of minutes, while the members of the press team, which included multiple Gothamist journalists, adopted a strategy of frequent player substitution. The game was seven-against-seven on a short field.

It was a non-contact injury. Mamdani, a midfielder, raced after a ball and landed awkwardly on his right leg. He was helped off the field by his press secretary, Joe Calvello, and Gothamist reporter David Brand.

Brand netted a goal during the match. Gothamist transportation and infrastructure editor Clayton Guse tallied an assist on The Kickers’ first goal in a pass to Crain's reporter Jack Grieve. Guse said the assist was the product of “grit, but more importantly gumption.”

Gothamist reporters Liam Quigley and Walter Wuthmann also played valiantly. Wuthmann saved a goal with a header when his team’s keeper was out of the net.

It’s unclear how the injury will affect the mayor, who is known for his youthful energy and social media videos. He has often been seen criss-crossing around the city on a bicycle and getting into pick-up basketball games.

Many of the speakers wished the mayor a speedy recovery.

“I want to assure everyone it is not because of a budget fight with the Council,” joked City Council Speaker Julie Menin. “We really, really hope that you feel better.”
