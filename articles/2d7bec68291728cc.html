Teen arrested in killing of 15-year-old Javits Frimpong

By Spectrum News NY1 · NY1 · November 11, 2026

A 17-year-old has been arrested in connection with the fatal shooting of 15-year-old Javits Frimpong in the Bronx last month, police said.

Frimpong was shot in the stomach on Oct. 29 while on his way to school near East 171st Street and Crotona Place. He later died at a hospital.

Surveillance video showed Frimpong running from someone chasing him before collapsing on the sidewalk, according to police.

Frimpong came to the United States from Ghana last year with his father. His father told NY1 that his son was quiet and had told him he was being bullied at school just days before the shooting.

His mother traveled from Ghana to the United States to help plan his funeral after President Donald Trump granted an emergency visa at the request of Mayor Zohran Mamdani.
