Elected leaders hand out Thanksgiving meals in Brooklyn

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 25, 2026

A huge helping hand for many families in Brooklyn this Thanksgiving week.

State Sen. Kevin Parker and his staff gave away free Thanksgiving Day meals over the weekend in Brooklyn.

Dozens of residents lined up outside the Vanderveer Park United Methodist Church in Flatbush.

Parker, along with several community organizations, handed out bags containing turkey or chicken plus eggs and all the fixings that residents will need to prepare a wholesome meal this Thanksgiving.

"This year has been different than last year because the need is greater. There’s a lot more people requesting food, a lot more need in terms of organizations that are trying to serve people and even the price of things like turkeys is higher," he said. “And so it’s just been, you know, you’re getting less bang for the buck. And so it’s been really more important that we come together and pull our resources in order to kind of stand in the gap for the community.”

Now, organizers say they handed out about 1,000 turkeys and chickens
