AG Letitia James Announces Arrest Of Harlem Nursing Home Worker In Death Of Resident

By Ann Brown · Harlem World · November 25, 2026

A former respiratory therapist at a Harlem nursing home has been arrested and charged in connection with the death of a 71-year-old resident who allegedly was left without oxygen for approximately 30 minutes after being improperly connected to a ventilator.

New York Attorney General Letitia James announced the arrest of Anita Augustin, 62, of Elmsford, on charges stemming from the May 2025 incident at Henry J. Carter Specialty Hospital and Nursing Facility in Harlem. Augustin is accused of failing to ensure the resident was properly connected to a functioning ventilator before leaving her unattended.

An investigation by the Attorney General’s Medicaid Fraud Control Unit found that Augustin allegedly failed to follow basic safety procedures while transferring the resident from a portable mechanical ventilator to the bedside ventilator in her room.

According to investigators, the incident occurred on May 9, 2025, at approximately 4 p.m. Augustin allegedly left the room without properly connecting the resident to the bedside ventilator or conducting necessary checks to confirm the machine was functioning and the resident was breathing. The resident was reportedly deprived of oxygen for about 30 minutes and died 10 days later as a result of the alleged neglect.

“New Yorkers must be able to trust the nursing home staff who take their loved ones’ lives in their hands every day,” Attorney General James said. “There is no excuse for negligence that leads to tragic deaths in nursing homes.”

Augustin was arraigned in New York County Criminal Court before Judge Janice Chen. She faces charges of criminally negligent homicide and endangering the welfare of an incompetent or physically disabled person in the first degree, both felonies, along with falsifying business records in the second degree and willful violation of the health laws, both misdemeanors.

If convicted on the top charge, Augustin faces up to one and one-third to four years in state prison. She is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.

Residents, families and workers who suspect abuse or neglect can file confidential complaints with the Attorney General’s Office or call its Medicaid Fraud Control Unit hotline at 1-800-771-7755 or 212-417-5397.
