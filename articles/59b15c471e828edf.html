NYC overdose deaths fall to lowest level since 2019, Mamdani says

By Erica Brosnan · NY1 · November 13, 2026

Overdose deaths in New York City have fallen to their lowest levels since 2019, declining 58% from 2022, Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced Friday.

“Every overdose death is a life lost, and a reminder of the work we still have to do,” Mamdani said in a statement. “We know what saves lives: connecting people with care and making recovery services accessible to everyone who needs them.”

New Yorkers seeking mental health or substance use support can call or text 988. The city's NYC Mental Health Connect website can also help residents find substance-use recovery services.
