Man denied stabbing Brooklyn jogger but described being covered in blood, complaint says

By Brittany Kriegstein · Gothamist · November 9, 2026

Sayed Mohassib, a 27-year-old Department of Education employee with documented mental health issues, was arrested and charged with murder Saturday in connection with the killing of 28-year-old Katie Suarez.

Suarez was stabbed in the neck as she was running on the path on Shore Parkway near Bay 17th Street on Oct. 30, and died at a local hospital days later, officials have said.

NYPD detectives previously said they believed it was a random attack.

Despite denying stabbing anyone, Mohassib told police that he got rid of his bloody clothing and the knife, and tried to clean blood off his hands and off the walls of his apartment, the complaint states. Detectives who visited his apartment observed a reddish stain on the wall that later tested positive as blood, according to the complaint. Mohassib also identified himself in the surveillance video, the complaint states.

Besides the murder charge, Mohassib is also accused of tampering with physical evidence and criminal weapons possession, according to the documents. A Brooklyn judge ordered him held without bail as the case proceeds, and his attorney did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

Local lawmakers on Saturday demanded a full investigation into Mohassib’s hiring by the city’s Department of Education. They said he’d previously been discharged by the U.S. Marine Corps for psychiatric issues and had a brief stint at the NYPD before being hired by the city schools in 2024. City payroll records show he was working as a "substitute school aide.”

Mohassib is due back in court on Tuesday.
