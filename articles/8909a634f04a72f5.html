Craig Campanella: Nothing but nets

By Pat Kiernan, Chelsea Katz · NY1 · November 9, 2026

It’s a one-on-one game: Craig Campanella versus this basketball hoop in Hell’s Kitchen.

“How can I, you know, get the net tied in a way that it’ll stay up?” Campanella said, explaining his thought process while handing a basketball net. “And do it in a kind of an efficient and timely manner?”

“I’m not really a basketball player, but I do think that all basketball hoops need nets,” he said. “I think it’s more fun to play with the net.”

Campanella’s calling came a few years ago. He says he couldn’t resist stepping in when he saw a netless rim near his apartment in Harlem.

“I said to myself, ‘I have a few dollars, and I have a stepladder,’” he said.

Campanella moved to Queens a couple of years later and found the same problem. So he shot his shot, and then he didn’t stop.

“People reaching out to me and then sending me ‘thank you’ notes afterwards has been the best,” he said.

Once people contact Campanella on social media about a problem spot, he scopes out the situation. He verifies that the hoop needs some help and what kind of net it needs.

“The worst thing is to bring all my gear to a court that doesn’t need nets,” he said. “So scouting is a big part of what I do.”

Campanella fronts the money for the new netting himself, though he says that people and sports companies have been donating to the cause. The city’s Parks Department, which is in charge of overseeing the basketball courts, says they have no problem with a little maintenance teamwork.

“It’s easier to see if it goes in or not,” said Mike Christman, a basketball player who lives nearby. “It’s just, it’s just a nicer, nicer look, also just makes the sport a lot more enjoyable.”

Campanella installed his 100th net last month. And while it is an exciting milestone, Campanella says he’s just happy to do something simple for the community.

“There are more important problems in the world than putting nets on hoops,” Campanella said. “But it’s something I can do and something I like doing. So I do it.”

For coming in with the basketball net assist, Craig Campanella is our New Yorker of the Week.
