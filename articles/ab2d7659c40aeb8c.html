Wind, rain, maybe snowflakes: It's about to get chilly around NYC

By Giulia Heyward · Gothamist · November 11, 2026

New Yorkers should prepare for temperatures to drop later this week, with meteorologists forecasting gusty winds, moderate rain and the potential appearance of snowflakes in the coming days.

Parts of Long Island and Western Sound could also see minor coastal flooding during the daytime high tide cycles from Thursday into Saturday.

A low-pressure system passing through the area is responsible for this, as it is expected to bring cooler air from the north, with temperatures dropping to the 40s from Friday through Sunday, according to Brian Ciemnecki, a meteorologist with the National Weather Service.

That means New Yorkers will notice temperatures about 10 to 15 degrees colder than they’ve been for most of this week. Highs should remain about 60 through Thursday, before the sharp drop in temperatures begins.

Temperatures are expectedly to slightly increase into the 50s on Monday and Tuesday of next week.

Ciemnecki said this year has thus far been slightly warmer than last year, but New Yorkers shouldn’t assume they won’t get a repeat of last winter’s brutally cold weather.

“We’re not quite to wintertime yet, we’re still in fall,” he said. “Let’s see.”

Nearly two dozen people froze to death during last year’s wintry conditions, which saw record-breaking snowfall as temperatures plunged below freezing.
