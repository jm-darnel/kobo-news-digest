Building collapse closes streets, changes bus routes around site

By Noorulain Khawaja · NY1 · November 12, 2026

While there was heartache at the scene of Wednesday’s building collapse, there were also headaches Thursday for people trying to get around.

“It was hard because I literally had to turn around and come a whole other way then walk about two, three blocks to make a delivery,” Jeffrey Hooper said. “My prayers are for the families that had to go through this.”

Many understood that the city’s bus diversions and street closures were an effort to get more emergency personnel to the rescue operations.

“I was really surprised to see that nothing was running,” Bruce Barry said.

“I had no idea. I was on the bus and I saw that it took a detour, and I was like ‘Oh, where I am I going?’ and I got off the bus to kind of walk in the direction I needed to go and I found out by walking what was going on,” Barry said.

Although the FDNY announced Thursday afternoon that rescue efforts had officially concluded, residents should still be prepared for travel troubles in the area over the coming days.
