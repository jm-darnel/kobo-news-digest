Billion Oyster Project, NYC Ferry Launch Floating Classroom For Harlem Students And Beyond

Harlem World · November 9, 2026

Today, Billion Oyster Project, NYC Ferry, New York City Economic Development Corporation (NYCEDC), and Hornblower Group announced a new partnership and the launch of the Floating Classroom.

An ambitious new program that will turn the NYC Ferry vessels into interactive classrooms on New York Harbor and introduce thousands of students to marine science, the working waterfront, and careers on New York City’s waterways. As part of this new partnership, Billion Oyster Project will now serve as the Marine Restoration and Education Partner of NYC Ferry.

Announced at the DUMBO Ferry Dock in Brooklyn Bridge Park, the program will transform existing NYC Ferry vessels into science classrooms during off-peak weekday hours. Led by Billion Oyster Project educators, 90-minute voyages will introduce students to marine ecology, oyster restoration, climate resilience, and the people and careers that keep New York Harbor moving. The partnership aims to bring more than 10,000 public school students onto the Harbor annually.

For a city defined by its waterways, too many young New Yorkers have never had the chance to experience the Harbor firsthand. New York City is home to more than 1.1 million public school students, but getting a class onto the water has traditionally required resources that many schools simply do not have. By pairing NYC Ferry’s vessels and citywide network with Billion Oyster Project’s educators, the Floating Classroom makes the Harbor more accessible to students across New York City.

Each voyage will give students a new perspective on the city they call home. From its shipping lanes and shorelines to the marine habitats and restoration projects beneath the surface, New York Harbor will become a place where students can connect classroom learning to the natural systems and industries on New York City’s waterways.

“…ability to bring students on the water…”

“I’ve spent most of my life working on the water, and one of the things that still surprises me is how many New Yorkers have never had the chance to experience their own Harbor,” said Ann Fraioli, Director of Education at Billion Oyster Project. “You can teach a student about oysters, marine life, or water quality in a classroom, but there’s something different about being out there and seeing it for yourself. You begin to understand that New York is part of an extraordinary and rich ecosystem. We want every young New Yorker to have the chance to make that discovery.”

“…where thousands of our youngest New Yorkers…”

“Economic development is about creating opportunity and making sure every New Yorker has a chance to access all that the City has to offer,” said NYCEDC President & CEO Anthony Shorris. “This partnership with Billion Oyster Project puts that idea into practice, turning NYC Ferry vessels into floating classrooms where thousands of our youngest New Yorkers can explore marine science, learn about our working waterfront, and discover the maritime careers that will help shape New York’s future.”

“Hornblower is proud to build on and grow its success and maritime leadership of connecting New York City students to the opportunities of our shared waterways as we launch the Floating Classroom across New York Harbor,” said Timothy O’Brien, SVP of Ferries and Concessions at Hornblower Group. “With this program and our ongoing community engagement, Hornblower connects classrooms from across the city to educational experiences alongside cool and reliable careers in maritime, ensuring the future of NYC Ferry and maritime transportation in New York is inclusive, rewarding, and reflective of the city we serve.”

Schools interested in participating can learn more at the Floating Classroom.

About NYCEDC

New York City Economic Development Corporation is a mission-driven, nonprofit organization that works for a vibrant, inclusive, and globally competitive economy for all New Yorkers. We take a comprehensive approach, through four main strategies: strengthen confidence in NYC as a great place to do business; grow innovative sectors with a focus on equity, build neighborhoods as places to live, learn, work, and play; and deliver sustainable infrastructure for communities and the city’s future economy.

About Billion Oyster Project

Billion Oyster Project is a nonprofit restoring oyster reefs across New York Harbor and reconnecting New Yorkers with the water that defines their city. By pairing large-scale ecological restoration with place-based education, Billion Oyster Project is rebuilding the Harbor’s ecological foundation while preparing the next generation to sustain it. With a goal to restore one billion oysters and engage one million students by 2030, Billion Oyster Project is working to prove that cities can restore their native ecosystems, and that restoration lasts when people are part of it. For more information, visit billionoysterproject.org.
