Yankees news: Brian Cashman shopping Spencer Jones, Jasson Domínguez, Ryan McMahon

By Peter Brody · Pinstripe Alley · November 22, 2026

MLB.com : Brian Cashman revealed that he is fielding calls on Spencer Jones and Jasson Domínguez, with Jones in particular generating “a lot” of interest. Should the Yankees move both players this winter, it would only intensify the need to add a center fielder with only Cody Bellinger capable of fielding the position in anything more than an emergency role. It remains to be seen what type of return the Yankees could extract for Jones and Domínguez, both players showing clear deficiencies at the major league level. Jones acquitted himself well enough with a 106 wRC+ in 86 games but struggled defensively in center while Domínguez is untenable in the outfield and saw his production wither to a 76 wRC+ in 57 games, irrespective of a subsequent hot stretch in the Dominican Winter League.

Yahoo! Sports | TJ French : Speaking of Yankees with minimal trade value, the team would love to find a trade partner for Ryan McMahon this winter. This would be contingent upon acquiring a replacement infielder in either the same or a separate deal, though Cashman also declared that he would also be happy to have McMahon play out the final year of his contract in pinstripes. After posting a 78 wRC+, -3 DRS, and 0.5 fWAR in 124 games at third, the Yankees would likely need to attach a prospect or execute a bad contract swap to get the majority of McMahon’s $16 million off the books.

New York Daily News | Gary Phillips : David Bednar returned to the Yankees on the qualifying offer last week, but that did not bring an end to the negotiations between the two parties. Cashman confirmed that he had been in contact with Bednar’s representatives over the framework for a multi-year deal — teams have the option to negotiate an extension which would supersede the QO — but declined to reveal specifics. The motivation would be driven by a desire to reduce Bednar’s Competitive Balance Tax hit, the $23.15 million QO representing a record AAV for a reliever, albeit for just one year. Bednar went 35-for-39 in save opportunities with a 2.47 ERA this past season.
