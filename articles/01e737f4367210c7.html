$100M lawsuit follows mistaken NYPD arrest in Gowanus

By Louis Finley · NY1 · November 10, 2026

Timothy Brown is suing New York City and NYPD officers in federal court after detectives mistook him for a drug dealer’s associate during an arrest in Gowanus last April.

“I was in a state of shock. I didn’t know what was happening,” Brown said.

Brown’s $100 million lawsuit describes fractured ribs, a broken nose and permanent scars. He now walks with a cane.

“I’m still in pain in certain areas of my body,” he said.

In response to the lawsuit, the NYPD said it placed the detectives and their sergeant on modified duty and disbanded their team.

Brown’s attorneys said they want the detectives held accountable.

“They are still on the job. They are still getting a salary. They’re still building up their pensions,” Mina Malik, an attorney with the Cochran Firm, said.

“The leadership of the city is simply willing to pay hundreds of millions of dollars without making a change,” Derick Sells of the Cochran Firm said.

For Brown, the case is about accountability and preventing someone else from becoming the next case.
