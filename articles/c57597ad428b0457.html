Man charged with murder after woman found stabbed to death in Queens, NYPD says

By Phil Corso · Gothamist · November 10, 2026

A 54-year-old man has been charged with murder in connection with the fatal stabbing of a woman inside a building in Flushing, Queens, last week, police said Monday.

Police said officers responding to a 911 call around 4:30 p.m. Friday found an unidentified woman unconscious and unresponsive inside a building at 3617 164th St. The woman had multiple stab wounds and was pronounced dead at the scene by emergency medical workers, according to police.
