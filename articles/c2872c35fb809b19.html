NYPD charges homeless man with murder after woman, 62, was killed at Chinatown apartment

By Liam Quigley · Gothamist · November 21, 2026

Police say they charged a homeless man with murder after a woman was found dead at her apartment in the Chinatown area Friday night.

Police charged Alexander Florentino, 25, with murder in connection with the attack. They said a 66-year-old man was also found bleeding from a wound to his face after he was struck with a broken bottle.

NYPD officials said Florentino, whom they described as "undomiciled," was taken into custody near the scene Friday evening, but they didn’t formally charge him until Saturday.

In addition to murder, he's also facing charges of attempted murder, two counts of strangulation, assault, attempted assault and burglary.

Police haven’t shared the identity of the victim and said the medical examiner’s office was still determining the exact cause of her death Saturday.
