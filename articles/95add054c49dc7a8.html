Intoxicated Queens driver killed baby after running stop sign, indictment says

By Giulia Heyward · Gothamist · November 6, 2026

A Flushing man has been indicted on charges including manslaughter after he allegedly sped past a stop sign while on cocaine and THC, then struck and killed a 1-year-old boy in a stroller.

Elvin Rivera, 57, was driving in a residential area of Flushing around 8:35 a.m. on Oct. 11 when he ran through a stop sign on 32nd Avenue, traveling northbound on Murray Street, according to the indictment.

He then sped through the intersection and struck the stroller, which was being pushed by the 1-year-old’s nanny, authorities say.
