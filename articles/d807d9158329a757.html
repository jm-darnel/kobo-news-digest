Rikers detainee's death is at least the 9th in corrections department custody this year

By Ryan Kost · Gothamist · November 10, 2026

A 36-year-old man died on Rikers Island early Tuesday, according to the city’s Department of Correction.

A correction officer found the man in his bed at the Robert N. Davoren Center, according to the department. Staff called a medical emergency and he was pronounced dead just after 6 a.m. The department did not release a cause of death.

He is at least the ninth person to die in the department’s custody this year and at least the second man to die in that particular facility. He had pleaded not guilty to charges of attempted second-degree murder, attempted first-degree assault and third-degree assault.

Correction Commissioner Stanley Richards said in a statement that “this profound loss weighs heavily on us all.”
