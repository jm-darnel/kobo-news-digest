Los Angeles: Taye Diggs, Tisha Campbell, Paula Patton & More Celebrate Lifetime’s Love Of A Lifetime

Lifetime brought Hollywood together for an intimate and star-studded Love of a Lifetime Celebration on Wednesday, November 4, at White Rabbit Sky Lounge at The Valorian Hotel in West Hollywood, California.

Set against the backdrop of Los Angeles, the celebration highlighted Lifetime’s continued commitment to bringing compelling stories, unforgettable performances and meaningful entertainment to audiences everywhere.
