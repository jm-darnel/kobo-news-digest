Rep. Jeffries talks midterm wins, Pete Hegseth impeachment and speaker vote

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 25, 2026

A few weeks ago, after a sweeping Democratic victory in the midterms, NY1’s Errol Louis traveled to Washington, D.C., to sit down with Rep. Hakeem Jeffries. House Democrats overwhelmingly nominated Jeffries for speaker, putting him on track to become the first Black speaker in U.S. history.

Jeffries spoke about leading the largest Democratic House majority this century, the diversity of the incoming freshman class and what he saw while campaigning across the country. He also looked at how Democrats held every seat where an incumbent ran and picked up seats in Kentucky, Indiana, Florida and Guam.
