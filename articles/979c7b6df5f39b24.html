NYC panel set to vote on $8 billion Queens casino design Monday

By Giulia Heyward · Gothamist · November 24, 2026

A New York City panel is set to vote Monday on the proposed design of the Metropolitan Park and Hard Rock Hotel and Casino complex in Queens.

New renderings for the planned casino were released last month. The designs drew more than three hours of public testimony in October before the panel postponed its vote, citing a need for more information about the project's stormwater management plan.

Supporters praised the project's potential to create tens of thousands of union jobs, add green space and improve the nearby 7 train station. Opponents, including the group Queens Anti-Casino, argued the development would strain local infrastructure and raise environmental justice concerns.

Monday's meeting is scheduled for 2:30 p.m. at City Hall and will be livestreamed on the commission's YouTube channel. The public testimony period for the project has closed.

Metropolitan Park did not immediately respond to requests for comment from Gothamist.
