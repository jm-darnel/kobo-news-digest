NYBG Holiday Train Show celebrates 35 years

By Roger Clark · NY1 · November 13, 2026

As Johnny Cash once sang, "I hear the train a-coming. It's rolling 'round the bend."

In this case, that bend is inside the landmark Haupt Conservatory at the New York Botanical Garden in the Bronx, and the train is a model freight train.

It's the 35th year for the New York Botanical Garden's Holiday Train Show, which features those fun model trains, but also almost 200 replicas of New York City-area landmarks, all made from plant parts.

"It's hard to imagine 35 years ago. I expect that they did not know that this would turn into a beloved New York City holiday tradition, but it sure has," Jennifer Bernstein, CEO and president of the garden, said.

"Durable, dried plant material — it's foraged in the forest. It's foraged at the conservatories where we work. We also have certain individuals around the country who will harvest certain materials for us that are things that we love," said Laura Busse Dolan, who runs the company started by her father Paul.

Replicas include Coney Island's Wonder Wheel, the Brooklyn Bridge, JFK Airport, old Yankee Stadium and the garden's own Haupt Conservatory and Mertz Building.

There are the skyscrapers too, and new this year is Midtown, Manhattan's One Vanderbilt, which features the SUMMIT One Vanderbilt immersive observatory experience.

"It's an incredible honor, we are excited to be recognized amongst New York's City's most famous landmarks we have already marked an indelible place in the skyline of New York City and what an honor to be part of this institution, one of the most visited holiday experiences in New York City," said Robert Schiffer, executive vice president of S.L. Green Realty Corp.
