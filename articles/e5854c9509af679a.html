As their offense still takes shape, the Knicks lean on defense to secure wins

By JAIME C. HARRIS AmNews Sports Editor · Amsterdam News · November 19, 2026

Facing their Atlantic Division brethren on Sunday and again on Tuesday in Toronto, the Knicks notched their 14th and 15th consecutive wins, respectively, versus the Raptors by clamping down defensively to pull out late-game victories.

Although they have not yet collectively found stable footing offensively—ranking 25th in points per game at 110 when yesterday’s (Wednesday) slate of NBA games tipped-off—the Knicks were second in opponent points allowed going into Tuesday’s game at 104 and fourth in defensive team rating, giving up just 108.4 points per 100 possessions.

On Sunday, the Knicks overcame a 14-point fourth quarter deficit to push the game into overtime and ultimately emerge with a 112-108 victory. It was driven by center Karl-Anthony Towns’ 28 points and 17 rebounds, guard Jalen Brunson’s 28 points and forward OG Anunoby’s 21 points and nine rebounds against his former team.

Tuesday evening, it was Brunson’s four-point play that served as the separator. He was fouled by the Raptors’ Ja’Kobe Walter on a 25-foot three pointer with 1:25 remaining, nailing the jumper and ensuing free throw to give the Knicks a 106-102 lead. Walter was called for a flagrant one foul for stepping under Brunson’s landing space and the Knicks held the Raptors scoreless for the rest of the game. Brunson finished with 31 points and Towns had 16 points and 16 rebounds.

“We just play to the best of our ability in the last five minutes,” said Brunson via MSG Network after the win. “I know early in the season it hasn’t looked like it, but that’s where we’re most comfortable.”

If the first month of the season hasn’t revealed certainties, there are some definitives, one being the Eastern Conference is going to be highly competitive for the top-six automatic playoff spots (seeds 1-6) and the four play-in entries (seeds 7-10).

Prior to the start of last night’s games, the Philadelphia 76ers led the East at 12-3 followed by the Boston Celtics (11-3), and Detroit Pistons (9-4). The No. 4 seed Miami Heat, No. 5 Atlanta Hawks, and No. 6 Knicks were all 8-6. The Knicks are back at MSG on Saturday to play the Portland Trail Blazers and on Sunday take on the Indiana Pacers at the Garden.
