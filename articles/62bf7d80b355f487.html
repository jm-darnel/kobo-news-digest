Yankees Social Media Spotlight: Last week before the lockout

By John Griffin · Pinstripe Alley · November 22, 2026

It’s Sunday once more, and you know what that means — it’s time for our weekly social media roundup! The offseason is in full swing, as the Yankees have made their first moves of the winter, bringing back a trio of relievers to rebuild a bullpen that saw five guys hit free agency. And yet, as we know, this burst of energy and excitement will be a short one: as we enter this Thanksgiving week, we also enter the final full week of Thanksgiving — and thus, the final full week before the lockout begins. With that sad future coming up, let’s see what’s been going on this week.

Sleeping Under the Stars

For the 15th consecutive year, Brian Cashman participated in the Covenant House’s annual Sleep Out, an event raising money and awareness for homeless youth in this country. This time, though, the Yankees’ general manager wasn’t alone — the Yankees hosted an event at Yankee Stadium, as more than 500 participants slept on the field at the Stadium.

Reheating Lasagna

While Cashman and the Yankees slept under the stars, they were still making deals. This time, they brought back old friend Jonathan Loáisiga, who spent the 2026 season with the Arizona Diamondbacks. But before we knew for sure that he was back, we did get from the man himself a hint that he was back in the form of an Instagram story.

Congratulations, Suzyn

Yankee radio broadcaster Suzyn Waldman was honored by the Library of American Broadcasting Foundation at their annual luncheon gala in New York

Congratulations, Max!

Yankees utilityman Max Schuemann got married this week!

Photo Dump from Rice

Ben Rice returned to Instagram for the first time in quite a while to post about the last couple of weeks — including a trip to Vegas, his girl running the marathon, and a friend’s wedding. And, as always happens when the players who are hardly on social media post, Rice’s teammates were in the comments: Cam Schlittler told him to hop on GTA, George Lombard Jr. and Spencer Jones called him a well-traveled man, and Brendan Beck said that a busy couple of weeks was “nothing” for a guy like Rice.

Some Shots from 2026

Cody Charneski, a social and digital content producer for the Yankees, took to Instagram this week to not only share some of his favorite shots from this past season, but to explain the art that goes into them. If you love photography or cinematography, then this is a post for you.

Sir Didi

Former Yankees shortstop Didi Gregorius posted a video from his Yankees days “cause it’s Monday.”

Some Parting Thoughts from CC

We end this week’s social media roundup with some wisdom from Hall of Famer CC Sabathia.
