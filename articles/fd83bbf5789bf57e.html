1 person dead as FDNY rescues people trapped in collapsed Brooklyn building, NYPD says

By Atlan Hassard · NY1 · November 11, 2026

Police say one person is dead and another is critically injured following a building collapse in Brooklyn. Multiple others were taken to Kings County Hospital, the NYPD added.

DOB records indicate the collapsed building is 1321 Flatbush Avenue, a 3-story mixed-use building with four residential units and five total units.

This is a developing story. Stay with NY1 for the latest updates.
