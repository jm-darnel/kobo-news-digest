New York Landmarks Conservancy Honored Distinguished New Yorkers At 2026 Celebration

Harlem World · November 11, 2026

On November 4, 2026, the New York Landmarks Conservancy hosted its annual Living Landmarks Celebration at The Plaza.

This year’s event honored the Durst Family (Helena Durst and Nan A. Rothschild), Jamee and Peter Gregory, Jane Hartley and Ralph Schlosstein, LL COOL J, Crystal McCrary McGuire and Raymond J. McGuire, and Peter Marino. The event raised a record-setting $1.5 million for the nonprofit organization, with more than 400 guests in attendance.

“Our incredible honorees reflect the rich diversity and extraordinary talent of New York,” says Peg Breen, President of The New York Landmarks Conservancy. “Each has made significant contributions that have added to everyone’s enjoyment of the City.”

Guests who gathered to celebrate the year’s extraordinary honorees included: Bonnie Comely and Stewart Lane, Cindy Campbell and DJ Kool Herc, Marina Kellen French, Audrey and Martin Gruss, Marlene Hess and James Zirin, Wendy Lehman Lash, Sally Minard, Deborah Norville, Elizabeth Stribling and Guy Robinson, Barbara Tober, and Angela Vallot and James Basker.
