Veterans Day Parade returns on Wednesday

By Cheryl Wills · NY1 · November 9, 2026

Thousands will march down Fifth Avenue for the annual Veterans Day Parade, and celebrations will continue at the Intrepid Museum.

But this year, the conversations about how to better help military members after serving are changing.

Cushman and Wakefield is on the front lines of that battle, employing more than 1,000 veterans across the United States.

Bruce Mosler, who is a board member for the city’s Veterans Day Parade and the co-chairman of the Intrepid Museum, joined “New York Tonight” on Monday to talk about the program and the city’s Veterans Day celebrations.
