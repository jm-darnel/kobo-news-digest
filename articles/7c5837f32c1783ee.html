Mayor Mamdani to have surgery for torn Achilles tendon Friday

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 27, 2026

Mayor Zohran Mamdani is scheduled to have surgery for his torn Achilles tendon Friday.

As a result of the injury, Mamdani said that he will not be able to participate in Saturday morning’s rescheduled Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade.

“And very disappointed about I’ve been looking forward to attending this parade and now, as a result of the rescheduling, that I will not be able to make it because of the doctor’s orders,” he said Wednesday.

First Deputy Mayor Dean Fuleihan will act as mayor while Mamdani is under anesthesia.
