McGuinness Boulevard redesign complete after years of debate

By Erica Brosnan · NY1 · November 13, 2026

After years of debate and advocacy from neighborhood residents, the long-awaited redesign of Brooklyn’s McGuinness Boulevard is complete, Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced Friday.

Parking-protected bike lanes now stretch along the entire corridor, from Meeker Avenue to the Pulaski Bridge, the mayor said at a news conference.

McGuinness Boulevard serves more than 4,000 daily riders during the summer months, the mayor’s office said in a news release.

Just days after his Jan. 3 inauguration, Mamdani joined community leaders and street safety advocates to promise the project would be completed before the end of the year.

“We promised we would finish this work before winter arrived, and I am so proud that even though it is cold, we have delivered on that promise,” Mamdani said.

Mamdani also invoked a quote from urbanist Jane Jacobs that he has used during previous appearances on the boulevard: “Cities have the capability of providing something for everybody only when they are created by everybody.”

“This day, a day of enormous progress, safety, and change was created by everybody,” he said.
