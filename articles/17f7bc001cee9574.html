New York Pops conductor talks 'One Night Only' concert at Carnegie Hall

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 19, 2026

From the world of "The Gilded Age" to the stage at Carnegie Hall.

Denée Benton and Jordan Donica, who play love interests on the HBO series, are joining the New York Pops on Friday, Nov. 20 for an evening showcasing their musical talents.

Bringing it all together is a star in his own right: the orchestra’s music director and conductor Steven Reineke.

NY1's Stephanie Simon spoke with Reineke about what it means to have the two stars together in concert, and on their new holiday album.
