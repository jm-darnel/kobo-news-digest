Yankees To Promote Aaron Gershenfeld

By Charlie Wright · MLB Trade Rumors (Yankees) · November 10, 2026

Gershenfeld has been the organization’s run prevention and catching coordinator in recent seasons. He’s worked with backstops in both the majors and minors. Gershenfeld has worked in defensive-related roles with New York since 2020.

Seattle hired Swanson to be their bench coach last week. He’s been credited with the strong defensive work of Yankees catchers in recent years. The list includes current players Austin Wells and J.C. Escarra and former catchers Kyle Higashioka and Jose Trevino . Wells has posted a double-digit Fielding Run Value for three straight seasons. He was a Gold Glove Award finalist this year, though Dillon Dingler earned the honor.

It’s unclear what Gershenfeld’s exact title will be, but it’s fair to assume he’ll step into the defensive duties vacated by Swanson. The Yankees ranked 15th in FanGraphs’ Defensive Runs Above Average metric in 2026. The club got tremendous efforts from Wells and Cody Bellinger , who won the Platinum Glove Award as the AL’s top defender. There were holes elsewhere on the diamond. The departure of Jazz Chisholm Jr. , who posted -6 Defensive Runs Saved, could be addition by subtraction on the defensive side.

Defense has been a major issue for the Yankees in recent playoff runs. The club famously collapsed in the field during the 2024 World Series against the Dodgers. New York made four errors in an ugly loss against the Rays in Game 2 of the ALDS this year.
