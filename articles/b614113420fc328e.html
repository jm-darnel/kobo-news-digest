Thanksgiving In Harlem 2026: Where To Eat, Find Meals And Volunteer

Harlem World · November 17, 2026

By HWM

Thanksgiving in Harlem can mean a restaurant table, groceries for a family gathering, or time spent helping neighbors.

With Thanksgiving falling on Thursday, November 26, this guide brings together published holiday dining plans, an East Harlem turkey distribution, and ongoing food assistance. There is one important distinction: a program operating during Thanksgiving week is not necessarily open on Thanksgiving Day. Check dates, eligibility, reservations, and availability before leaving home.

Where To Eat On Thanksgiving

Red Rooster Harlem, at 310 Lenox Avenue, has announced Thanksgiving service from 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. Diners in the main dining room can choose the regular à la carte menu or a Turkey Feast prix fixe priced at $90 per person. The restaurant also lists a separate $15 entertainment cover per person.

Live performances upstairs include AC Lincoln from noon to 4 p.m. and Diva N’kenge from 7 to 10 p.m. Before reserving, ask which charges apply to your seating and confirm the complete cost, including applicable taxes, gratuities, and any additional purchases.

Downstairs, Ginny’s Supper Club offers another option: an $85 Thanksgiving buffet, plus a $20 entertainment cover per person. The published lineup features the Nate Lucas All Stars from 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. These are two dining experiences within the same Lenox Avenue location, rather than separate neighborhood addresses.

Parties of seven or more should contact the restaurant’s events team instead of booking online. Its Thanksgiving page lists large-party pricing of $105 per person, plus the $15 music cover, and directs inquiries to dina@redroosterharlem.com.

Find Free Food Before The Holiday

In East Harlem, Union Settlement plans its annual turkey drive for Thursday, November 19, from 3 to 6 p.m. at 237 East 104th Street. The announced goal is to provide 400 families with a turkey and nonperishable Thanksgiving staples.

However, the published announcement does not specify registration procedures, eligibility rules, or whether distribution is open to walk-ins. Contact Union Settlement before traveling; the stated goal of serving 400 families is not a guarantee that food will be available to everyone who arrives.

West Harlem residents can also check Food Bank For NYC’s Community Kitchen at 252 West 116th Street. Its published schedule lists free community meals for all ages on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays, from 4 to 6 p.m. Halal and vegetarian options are available upon request.

Crucially, the kitchen lists closures for Thanksgiving Day, November 26, and Friday, November 27. Do not treat its regular Friday meal schedule as confirmation of holiday service.

For other options, Food Bank’s Find Food directory covers nearly 800 pantries, soup kitchens, and mobile pantries citywide. Confirm each location’s holiday schedule directly.

Give Back With A Confirmed Assignment

Food Bank lists volunteer opportunities supporting meal service, its Free Farmers Market, and older adult activities. Meal-service volunteers must be at least 18; market volunteers can participate from age 14 with an adult chaperone. Registration and available shifts should be checked through its volunteer calendar. These listings do not establish that Thanksgiving Day assignments are available.

Residents unable to volunteer can explore the organization’s donation and fundraising options. For individual volunteer questions, contact volunteer@foodbanknyc.org.

Plan Before Heading Out

Keep three questions handy: Is this service operating on my chosen date? Do I need a reservation or registration? What will I need to pay or bring? Published dining prices, food distributions, and volunteer programs serve different purposes. Checking those details helps families make informed plans without confusing a holiday dinner, grocery pickup, or service shift.
