Breezy but seasonal trend ahead

By Spectrum News Weather Staff · NY1 · November 8, 2026

Breezy but seasonal Highs: Low 60s Lows: Upper 40s

Northwest winds will gust up to 25-30 mph today through Tuesday with seasonal conditions in the forecast. Afternoon temperatures peak in the low 60s today with partly to mostly sunny skies. It'll be slightly cooler tomorrow in the mid to upper 50s with spotty, light rain showers possible in the morning through early afternoon. Bright skies for Tuesday and Wednesday with highs around 60° ahead of a big weather system bringing widespread rain through Thursday with additional showers expected Friday. We're tracking the coldest conditions of the season so far with temperatures not getting out of the 40s Friday into the weekend. In fact, some upstate communities could see their first round of accumulating snow!
