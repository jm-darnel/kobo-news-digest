Some sun, but warm on Monday ahead of late-week storm

By Spectrum News Weather Staff · NY1 · November 23, 2026

Mostly cloudy Highs: Low 60s Lows: Upper 40s

Partly sunny skies and drier weather return Monday, allowing temperatures to rise into the mid- and upper 60s for a final day.

Expect a dry cold front to move through, dropping temperatures Tuesday and beyond. Highs will only be in the mid-40s to low 50s the rest of the holiday week.

A potential nor'easter storm system may arrive on Thanksgiving, with widespread rain developing and winds increasing, potentially impacting the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade.

As for now, the start of the parade looks dry and breezy, with increasing clouds, rain and wind throughout the day.
