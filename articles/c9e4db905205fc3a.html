Letitia James Beats Trump-Backed Rival For Third Term

New York Attorney General Letitia James, a Democrat, won a third term on Tuesday, Nov. 3.

She beat Republican Saritha Komatireddy, a former federal prosecutor whom President Donald Trump had endorsed, according to Associated Press projections reported by Fox 5 New York. With about 85% of ballots counted, James led by roughly 20 percentage points, The New York Times reported.

The result keeps one of Trump’s best-known legal opponents in office. The Times Union called it a blow to the president, who had wanted to unseat an incumbent who has brought cases against him and filed dozens of lawsuits challenging his administration’s policies.

Victory speech and concession

James declared victory at about 10 p.m. at the New York Hilton Midtown in Manhattan, where Democrats had gathered for election night. “My heart is full tonight,” she said, adding that “New Yorkers told us loud and clear, they want Tish James to finish the job,” according to The Journal News. She also said her office had “wielded the sword and shield of the law” to protect New Yorkers’ rights, the Times reported.

Komatireddy later conceded in a post on X. “I’m proud of the campaign we ran,” she wrote, Business Standard reported. She added that “public service means serving people, not celebrity or personal gain.”

A Trump-centered contest

Komatireddy built her campaign around public safety. She called for changes to the state’s bail laws and said public safety in New York had “totally deteriorated,” she told CBS6 Albany. James said her challenger was beholden to Trump, which Komatireddy denied. Komatireddy often avoided mentioning the endorsement, the Times Union reported. Trump had called her an “America First Patriot,” according to the Times.

The race became closer in its final weeks. Komatireddy gained ground while James faced criticism over Gov. Kathy Hochul’s decision to appoint her special prosecutor in the Cornell University sexual assault case, The Journal News reported. During the campaign, James pointed to her record of protecting New Yorkers from federal cuts and immigration crackdowns, WAMC reported.

James’s history with Trump

James was first elected in 2018. She was the first Black woman elected to statewide office in New York and the state’s first female attorney general, Syracuse.com noted. She became nationally known for her 2022 civil fraud lawsuit against Trump and his company. That case produced a $454 million judgment, which an appeals court threw out in 2025, ABC7 New York reported. Federal prosecutors charged James with mortgage fraud in 2025. That indictment was dismissed, and prosecutors have tried to indict her again, according to the Times.

Fellow Democrat Tom DiNapoli also won reelection as state comptroller, WGRZ reported.
