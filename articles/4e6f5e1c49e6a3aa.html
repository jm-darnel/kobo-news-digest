Today on Pinstripe Alley – 11/23/26

By Andrew Mearns · Pinstripe Alley · November 23, 2026

Cam Schlittler and Gerrit Cole are no longer the only pitchers in town who have a Cy Young on their résumé. The Mets made a huge move last night by reportedly signing the best free agent on the market: Tarik Skubal. A two-time Cy winner who spent his entire career with the Tigers before moving to the Dodgers at the 2026 Trade Deadline, Skubal is now also the owner of a nine-year, $450 million contract — the richest ever given to a pitcher. Holy moly.

Later, Peter will consider another free agent target: Seibu Lions ace Kaima Taira.
