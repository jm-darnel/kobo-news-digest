Woman found dead in Lower Manhattan building, NYPD says

By Giulia Heyward · Gothamist · November 20, 2026

A woman was found dead in a Lower Manhattan building on Friday afternoon, according to police.

Police said they found an unconscious and unresponsive woman at the scene, with signs of trauma to her neck.

Police said a person of interest has been taken into custody and the investigation is ongoing.

This is a developing story and was updated with new information.
