Why are airline systems facing so many disruptions?

By Spectrum News NY1 · NY1 · November 17, 2026

A system outage at United Airlines caused delays for departing flights across the country, with some planes held on the ground for more than 90 minutes.

United has not said which system was affected, but pilots told air traffic controllers the problem involved a system used to calculate an aircraft’s final weight. That information is required before a plane can take off.

David McCarthy, a Columbia University lecturer in enterprise risk management, FAA-certified pilot and experienced U.S. Coast Guard aviator, joined "Mornings On 1" to discuss the latest disruptions.
