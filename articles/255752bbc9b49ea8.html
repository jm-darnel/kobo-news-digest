NYPD investigating alleged sexual assault of NYU student near freshman dorm

By Giulia Heyward · Gothamist · November 18, 2026

Police are investigating the alleged sexual assault of a 19-year-old NYU student in Greenwich Village over the weekend.

Police confirmed reports that a man allegedly assaulted the woman around 9:58 p.m. Sunday near Bleecker and Mercer streets, where NYU's John A. Paulson Center also serves as a first-year residence hall.

NYU is not conducting its own investigation because the alleged assailant is not a member of the university community, according to the school. The university says it is cooperating with the NYPD and providing support to students.

“The safety and wellbeing of our students will always come first,” said Wiley Norvell, NYU's senior vice president for university relations and public relations, in a statement. “Our focus is supporting our community and assisting the NYPD in its investigation. We strictly protect the privacy of anyone who comes forward.”

No arrests have been made, according to the police.
