Miami: Jean Shafiroff Receives ĄHOLA!’s Latina de Corazón Award At Latina 100 Powerhouse Gala

Harlem World · November 11, 2026

Philanthropist, author and television and host Jean Shafiroff was honored with the prestigious Latina de Corazón Award at the second annual ¡HOLA!

Latina Powerhouse 100 Gala, held in November 2026 at the Ziff Ballet Opera House at the Adrienne Arsht Center for the Performing Arts in Miami. The award, presented by philanthropist and businesswoman Adrienne Arsht, recognized Jean Shafiroff’s longstanding dedication to supporting Hispanic communities, empowering women and advancing philanthropic initiatives. Jean was among the distinguished honorees celebrated during the evening, which brought together approximately 400 guests from the worlds of entertainment, fashion, business, philanthropy and the arts. Presented by ¡HOLA! Américas, the gala celebrated influential women whose achievements continue to shape Hispanic culture and leadership across the United States and Latin America.

“I am deeply honored to receive the Latina de Corazón Award from ¡HOLA! and especially grateful to Adrienne Arsht for presenting it to me,” said Jean Shafiroff. “Philanthropy has always been about bringing people together, regardless of their backgrounds, and creating opportunities for those who need them most. The Hispanic community has contributed so much to the cultural and social fabric of our country, and I am proud to support organizations that help women, children and families build better futures. This recognition is especially meaningful because it celebrates the power of giving and the importance of working together.”

Jean Shafiroff’s philanthropic commitments include support to many charities that assist the Latin community, her role on the board of Casita Maria Center for Arts & Education, the longstanding Bronx-based nonprofit founded in 1934 to serve New York’s growing Puerto Rican community. Today, the organization provides arts, cultural and educational programming for young people and families in the South Bronx. Her charitable involvement also extends to the Dominican Women’s Development Center where Jean has been a strong supporter and past honoree, The New York Women’s Foundation, New York City Mission Society and numerous organizations addressing education, health care, women’s rights, animal rights and opportunities for underserved communities.

Hosted by former Miss Universe winners Dayanara Torres and Andrea Meza alongside Venezuelan-born television personality Boris Izaguirre, the celebration also featured musical performances by prominent Latin artists, including Natalia Jiménez, Camila Fernández and Leslie Grace. Held under the theme “The Hispanic Legacy: Inspiring America’s Future,” the gala highlighted the growing influence of Hispanic women across industries while recognizing those who have helped advance cultural understanding and community development.

For Jean Shafiroff, whose philanthropic efforts have earned recognition from numerous charitable organizations and civic institutions, the Miami honor adds another chapter to a career devoted to encouraging greater participation in charitable giving. Through her work as a fundraiser, board member, author and television host, she continues to advocate for philanthropy as a means of strengthening communities and improving lives.

Jean Shafiroff

Jean Shafiroff is a philanthropist, humanitarian, TV host, writer, and the author of the book “Successful Philanthropy: How to Make a Life By What You Give“. Jean works extensively as a volunteer fundraiser and leader in the philanthropic world. She serves on the boards of 8 charitable organizations and each year chairs upwards of eight to ten different charity galas. She also hosts and underwrites many large parties in her homes for different non-profits. Among the many causes she champions are those involved in women’s rights, rights of underserved populations, health care, and animal welfare. In addition to hosting her own TV show, entitled Successful Philanthropy, Jean Shafiroff often appears on TV shows, podcasts and then in the press about her fashion and philanthropy. She is an international leader who strives to motivate people of all ages and backgrounds to get involved in the philanthropic process.

Jean Shafiroff serves on the boards of the French Heritage Society, Southampton Hospital Foundation, NYC Mission Society, Couture Council of the Museum at FIT, New York Women’s Foundation, Casita Maria, Global Strays, and Southampton Animal Shelter Foundation Honorary Board. In addition, she is a member of the Advisory Board of the Historical Society of Palm Beach County. A Catholic, Jean served on the board of the Jewish Board for 28 years and now is one of their honorary trustees. Jean Shafiroff has been honored numerous times by a variety of charitable organizations for her work and is regarded Jean as being among the leading ladies of philanthropy.

About ¡HOLA! Américas:

¡HOLA! Américas is a leading bilingual entertainment and lifestyle media platform serving audiences across the United States and Latin America. Building on the international legacy of ¡HOLA! magazine, founded in Spain in 1944, the brand launched ¡HOLA! USA in 2016 before expanding into ¡HOLA! Américas, offering celebrity interviews, entertainment news and coverage of fashion, beauty, culture, society and prominent public figures in English and Spanish. The publication celebrates Hispanic culture and its influence across the Americas through editorial features, digital storytelling and special events.
