Yankees news: The trouble with third base

By Joshua Diemert · Pinstripe Alley · November 24, 2026

MLB.com | Brian Murphy : Everyone has some kind of weakness, even the mighty Dodgers came up short in 2026. The Yankees’ big weakness in this offseason is the complete lack of any value at third base, with Ryan McMahon unable to be even a league-average hitter and also starting to fail defensively. Teruaki Sato is a possible upgrade as he comes over from Japan and I’ll have more on him tomorrow*, but even if the Yankees miss on the NPB MVP, an upgrade at the hot corner is needed.

* Update : Sato is signing with the Cubs to play the outfield, so.. so much for that.

NJ.com | Adam Zagoria : The Mets seem to have this habit of picking up all the Yankees’ dropped toys, signing Clay Holmes, Devin Williams, Luke Weaver and of course Juan Soto over the past couple of years. That trend continued on Monday, with veteran slugger Paul Goldschmidt going across town after two seasons in the Bronx. Goldy agreed to a one year, $8 million deal that will see him most likely play in a strict platoon role, as the former MVP can still demolish left handed pitching — .967 OPS against southpaws vs. .590. Happy trails, Paul.

New York Post | Erich Richter : You know by now that the Yankees added bullpen depth yesterday, inking lefty Brent Suter to a one-year deal with a club option for 2028. Suter won’t blow the ball by you, but manages contact and deals a ton of ground balls, and boasts elite level extension that the Yankees’ pitching lab will do their best to further optimize.
