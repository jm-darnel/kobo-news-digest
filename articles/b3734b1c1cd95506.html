How to play in-5 Trivia on the SB Nation app

By Kris Willis · Pinstripe Alley · November 9, 2026

You all have mastered the art of commenting but can you name a Yankees player faster than your fellow commenters? That’s the challenge with in-5, our daily trivia game, and today we’re announcing the debut of the game in the SB Nation app (in-5 is available on the web too, you’ve probably seen it at the top of the site).

It’s a good opportunity to see if you can beat the commenter we all know you want to beat. Maybe you get it in the coveted first clue, maybe it takes four. But share your score in the comments, whatever it is.

How to play : In-5 is a daily trivia game. You get five clues to guess a player’s name. The clues go from broadest to most specific.

Play other teams and sports : We’ve got four broader sport games for MLB, NBA, NFL, MMA, and pro wrestling, and we have 25 team-specific games across pro baseball, basketball, and football.

Yankees fans, you can also play New York Giants and Mets trivia by joining them on the app. To play the general MLB game, follow our “MLB” community on the SB Nation app .
