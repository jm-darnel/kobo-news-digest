How Mamdani wants to change the way New York handles protests

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 20, 2026

This week, Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced he was disbanding an NYPD unit known sometimes for its clashes with protesters. The Strategic Response Group’s role at demonstrations will be taken over by a civilian unit in the Office of Community Safety working with the NYPD’s new First Amendment Support Team (FAST). The move has the backing of Police Commissioner Jessica Tisch. NY1 political reporter Bobby Cuza, statehouse reporter Bernadette Hogan and political director Bob Hardt look at what the dissolution of the SRG really means.

After that, what’s next in the blossoming partnership between the mayor and Gov. Kathy Hochul? Could it lead to new taxes on the wealthy — a top priority for Mamdani and his supporters on the left? The "Off Topic" team discusses how Albany and City Hall will work together in 2027.
