Driver charged with killing Brooklyn nurse crossing Third Avenue

By Liam Quigley, Ramsey Khalifeh · Gothamist · November 25, 2026

Staten Island resident Justin Mercado allegedly blew a red light on Aug. 29 and plowed into 28-year-old nurse Jiayi Zheng as he flew down Third Avenue at 55 mph, more than double the speed limit, authorities said. Officials said that Zheng, who had just finished a shift at nearby NYU Langone Hospital, was rushed back to the same hospital, where she died.

Authorities said Zheng was walking in a marked crosswalk when she was hit. In 2020, another nurse, Clara Kang, was killed by a motorcyclist while riding her bike home on the same stretch after an overnight shift.

City transportation department data shows there have been 17 deaths on the roadway in the past decade.

He pleaded not guilty to manslaughter, criminally negligent homicide, leaving the scene of a crash, assault and reckless endangerment.

Mercado’s 2014 Cadillac, which had Pennsylvania plates, was captured flying through Brooklyn on the day of the crash. On the morning of her death, the sedan was clocked speeding through a school zone in Lower Manhattan just 30 minutes before he plowed into Zheng, data shows.

Weeks before the crash, the car received a school zone speeding violation on Staten Island.

Mayor Zohran Mamdani restarted plans to make Third Avenue safer following Zheng’s death. Traffic lanes in both directions will be replaced over a 2.1-mile stretch beneath the Brooklyn-Queens Expressway with bike lanes, and the street’s pedestrian crossings will be shortened.

A call to Mercado’s lawyer was not immediately returned.
