Today on Pinstripe Alley – 11/22/26

By Jake Devin · Pinstripe Alley · November 22, 2026

We’re just about a week out from MLB owners locking out the players, and that means we’ve had a pretty active weekend of hot stove rumors and action. The Mets surprised by locking up young outfielder AJ Ewing on a nine-year extension yesterday, and we also heard some interesting buzz, such as the rumors surrounding the Giants shopping long-time ace Logan Webb. The Yankees were quiet after inking Jonathan Loáisiga, but it has to be said that they’ve been aggressive in addressing their bullpen, with Brian Cashman already having signed three relievers this offseason.

On the site today, you can catch an important feature in our Yankees Birthday series: Matt’s writeup of Yankee legend Ricky Ledée. And in the afternoon, John will spin through this week’s social media update, which sees players gearing up for a lockout.
