Displaced Sunset Park families seek help after fatal fire

By Emma Barnett · NY1 · November 6, 2026

Residents and neighbors impacted by a fatal fire in Sunset Park — which killed six people and left two hospitalized — are still scattered, confused and trying to find help.

On a block where many people speak Mandarin, one displaced neighbor, Lizhu Xie, said through a translation app that she lives with her three children and lost many belongings.

“We have lost a lot of things inside. Who will compensate for those losses?” Xie said.

Asked whether she knew where she would be sleeping that night, she said: “I have no place to sleep. Can you arrange a place for me to sleep?”

Jean-Thomas Candio, community disaster program manager for the Brooklyn Red Cross, said families affected by the fire were registered with the American Red Cross and receiving assistance.

“As far as we know at the Red Cross, all the family at that dwelling, at 651, they are registered with the American Red Cross, and they’ve been lodged,” Candio said.

Asked whether that meant they were getting free housing at a hotel, Candio said, “That is correct.”

Candio said three families impacted by the fire are being taken care of by the Red Cross.

Joyce Xie, a district leader and newly elected New York State Assembly member, was with a person whose wife was killed in the fire.

She said the loss has left him feeling helpless.

“I know it’s a very sad moment,” Joyce Xie said. “When you’re coming back home, your home is gone. Your family member is gone. So he feels very helpless. So that’s why here, we said, we’re going to walk to you step by step.”

Xie said the man and his wife had recently immigrated.

“I think it’s a very sad moment because they saw this as a new life and achieved a new dream here,” she said. “But this thing has happened to the family. It’s very bad.”
