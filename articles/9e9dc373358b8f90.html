Central Park rape suspect appears in court after Florida arrest

By Carly Haynes · NY1 · November 18, 2026

A man accused of raping several women in Central Park since 2021 will remain in jail without bail following his arraignment in a Manhattan courtroom on Wednesday.

Dereck Rivera-Deleon appeared before a judge with cuffs around his wrists and ankles.

Prosecutors charged him with two counts of predatory sexual assault in the rapes of two women in Central Park. He did not enter a plea.

Rivera-Deleon was extradited to New York late Tuesday night from Florida. He’s charged in connection with a reported rape in Central Park in October, where the NYPD said a woman was held at knifepoint and sexually assaulted.

Police said he is also facing a charge in a rape case from 2021.

According to prosecutors, he was identified in a photo array as the suspect in both cases.

Authorities are still working to track down a possible third victim connected to a reported rape in 2023, also in Central Park.

Prosecutors said Rivera-Deleon fled to Florida when he saw his photo in the news as a wanted suspect. His public defender claimed there’s currently no evidence to support that.

His next court appearance is scheduled for Monday, according to officials.
