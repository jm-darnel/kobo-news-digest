MTA Announces Grand Central Terminal Dining And Retail Expansion

Harlem World · November 18, 2026

“Businesses of all kinds are drawn to Grand Central for a reason,” said MTA Chair and CEO Janno Lieber. “The Terminal is back and better than ever.”

“We’re constantly looking for new ways to diversify our dining and retail offerings to boost Grand Central Terminal’s already robust options,” said MTA Chief Real Estate Transactions and Operations Officer David Florio. “And with the holiday season upon us, what better way to reconnect with friends or find unique gifts for loved ones at one of the most iconic buildings in New York City?”

“We are thrilled to partner again with Vizz Group to bring celebration and community to Grand Central, one of my favorite places in New York,” said David Rockwell, founder and President, Rockwell Group. “Centrale was inspired by an Italian spritz garden: We wanted to transport busy commuters to an uplifting space where lingering, connecting, and catching up isn’t just encouraged, it’s embedded in the culture. Our design is defined by a colorful palette of amber, cherry red, and capri blue accentuated by bold, swirling patterns and textured materials like ribbed glass, porcelain tile, and leather upholstery. Meaningful details connect the restaurant and market to the history of New York and the Terminal itself, grounding visitors in the surrounding city.”

“Grand Central Terminal is one of New York City’s most iconic destinations, and we’re thrilled to bring Toys“R”Us to the heart of Manhattan just in time for the holidays,” said Jamie Uitdenhowen, Executive Vice President of Toys“R”Us at WHP Global. “This new location gives commuters, New Yorkers and visitors another place to discover the latest toys and gifts while experiencing the joy of Toys“R”Us.”

Located inside the former Vineyard Vines shop at 89 East 42nd Street, the new Toys“R”Us store is operated by Go! Retail Group, the brand’s U.S. retail partner. The store brings the beloved toy brand to one of New York City’s most iconic destinations, offering commuters, New Yorkers and visitors a convenient place to shop for the season’s hottest toys and holiday gifts. The store features a curated assortment of toys, games, collectibles and must-have gifts from popular brands and entertainment franchises, including LEGO, Barbie, Hot Wheels, Pokémon, KPop Demon Hunters and more. The opening comes just in time for the holiday shopping season, with a special grand opening celebration planned for Friday, Nov. 20, featuring Geoffrey the Giraffe.

Accessible via the ramp in the southwest corner of the Main Concourse Level, the 8,455 square feet dual-concept Osteria Centrale and Mercato Centrale is the latest restaurant to open at Grand Central Terminal. Inspired by an Italian spritz garden, the 250-seat Osteria Centrale restaurant serves lunch, small appetizers, dinner, and late-night bites inspired by Italian cuisine every day from 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 a.m. Patrons can also sit at the 50-seat oblong bar to enjoy a meal or savor a cocktail, glass of wine or beer. Osteria Centrale is also available for private party bookings for up to 200 guests.

Located alongside Osteria Centrale, Mercato Centrale offers over 1,000 gourmet items, including sandwiches, pastries, salads, wraps, soft drinks, kombucha, alcoholic beverages and other grab and go items for commuters looking for a quick bite or snack, in addition to offering $1 La Colombe self-serve coffee every morning from 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. Mercato Centrale also features a self-checkout, giving customers additional convenience while on the go.

Both Osteria Centrale and Mercato Centrale were designed by the Rockwell Group, which also designed the French-inspired Grand Brasserie restaurant, also located inside Vanderbilt Hall.

These openings bring the total Grand Central Terminal occupancy rate to 91% and complement the more than 85 dining and retail locations that operate at Grand Central Terminal year-round. This also comes as Grand Central Terminal recently celebrated the opening of the annual Holiday Fair, which offers unique handmade items from more than 35 local small businesses. The Holiday Fair runs until Christmas Eve.

About Grand Central Terminal

Grand Central Terminal is one of America’s greatest transportation hubs and one of New York City’s most iconic buildings. Grand Central Terminal is the terminus of Metro-North Railroad, and home to a number of boutique restaurants, cocktail lounges, a gourmet market, and many specialty shops. In 2023, Grand Central’s historic footprint was expanded with the opening of Grand Central Madison, a 700,000-square-foot terminal and concourse designed to service the Long Island Rail Road (LIRR). LIRR access into Manhattan’s east side was the most transformative expansion of LIRR service in over a century. For more on Grand Central Madison, visit https://new.mta.info/grandcentralmadison. For more on Grand Central visit https://www.grandcentralterminal.com.

About Rockwell Group

Rockwell Group is a multidisciplinary architecture and design firm that balances creativity, innovation, and thought leadership with every undertaking. Headquartered in New York City, with offices in Los Angeles and Madrid, the firm was founded in 1984 by David Rockwell, FAIA, and merges performance and architecture to create extraordinary experiences and built environments across the globe. Projects include W Hotels (Hollywood, New York-Union Square, and Naples); Nobu hotels and restaurants worldwide; COTE 550 and Bar Chimera at 550 Madison (New York); Skywalker Grill at the Lucas Museum of Narrative Art (Los Angeles); Grand Brasserie (New York); and Palladino’s at Grand Central Terminal (New York).
