Forecast improves for Saturday's postponed Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade

By Rosemary Misdary · Gothamist · November 27, 2026

Weather for the rest of the holiday weekend and Saturday’s postponed Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will be a breeze compared to Thursday’s mayhem.

Clear and dry weather is expected on Saturday, with sustained winds reaching up to 15 mph and gusts up to 25 mph, according to the weather service, below the threshold that parade requires to proceed with its iconic giant balloons.

“Conditions are much better for a parade on Saturday,” said National Weather Service meteorologist David Stark. “The winds won't be anywhere near as strong.”

Central Park recorded 2.18 inches of rain, topping the previous record of 1.91 inches for Nov. 26, set in 1996, according to the weather service. The Rockaways remained under a flood advisory into Friday morning.

More than 100,000 homes in the Garden State were still without power early Friday, down from 179,000 on Thanksgiving Day.

More than 15,000 Con Ed customers in New York lost power during the nor'easter, according to Con Ed. In Westchester County, more than 9,000 households lost power, and many still were still waiting for service to be restored.

On Long Island, at the height of the storm, more than 40,000 homes lost power and the total was 11,000 in Orange and Rockland. Only a few thousand customers were still without service Friday, according to the utility officials.

Only general flight delays were reported at the region's airports.
