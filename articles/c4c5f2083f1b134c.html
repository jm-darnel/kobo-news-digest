Harlem Lights Up For The Holidays At Columbia’s Annual Manhattanville Tree Lighting

Harlem World · November 10, 2026

Join the “The Plaza” for Columbia University’s annual Manhattanville winter tree lighting ceremony.

Enjoy refreshments and live music with your neighbors before we begin the lights countdown promptly at 5:30 pm. Festive dress is optional, but encouraged!

Harlem Holiday Lights is a FREE, family-friendly event that encourages everyone to take advantage of:
