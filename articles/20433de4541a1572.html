Today on Pinstripe Alley – 11/20/26

By Madison Pavich · Pinstripe Alley · November 20, 2026

We’re less than a week away from Thanksgiving now, and the Yankees have started to get a bit active before the holidays kick in. After locking down David Bednar’s contract for 2027, there were overnight musings that Jonathan Loáisiga reportedly is coming back to New York after a year away in Arizona. His 2026 season was excellent, and it was also the first healthy season he’d had in a while, but up until now it seemed like there was more interest from the Dodgers or Mets than the Yankees. If they managed to get a hometown discount for their former reliever then they might’ve gotten away with a bit of a steal, but we’ll have to wait and see what the actual details of this potential deal are.

We’ve got a few things in store for you today, beginning with John giving us his thoughts on MLB’s early PR propaganda laying the ground for their side of the upcoming lockout. Jonathan hands out our latest report card to the rookie George Lombard Jr. after his whirlwind second half with the big league team, Kevin remembers the legacy of one of baseball’s early titans in Clark Griffith, and then in the afternoon I’ll be back to answer your latest mailbag questions.
