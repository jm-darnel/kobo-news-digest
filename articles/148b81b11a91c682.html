4-year-old boy dies, 2 others injured in NJ house fire

By Phil Corso · Gothamist · November 25, 2026

A 4-year-old boy died and two other people were injured in a house fire Tuesday afternoon in Union Township, New Jersey, near Newark, fire officials said.

Firefighters responding to a two-alarm blaze at a single-family home at 1129 Liberty Ave. encountered heavy flames coming from the front of the house, according to Union Fire Department official Raul Malave III.

Residents outside the home alerted firefighters that a child was missing. Crews searched the house “under extreme heat and advancing flames” and found the boy in a rear bedroom, Malave said.

A 12-year-old boy and a 39-year-old woman were also taken to hospitals with injuries that were not considered life-threatening, Malave said. No firefighters were injured.

Authorities said an investigation was underway to determine the cause of the blaze.

It wasn't the only major fire in Union Township on Tuesday, authorities said. Mayor Patricia Guerra-Fraizer said an earlier fire on Stuyvesant Avenue near Axton Avenue damaged at least seven businesses, including Here's the Story Bookstore and Conca D'Oro Bakery.
