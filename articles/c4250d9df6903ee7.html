Mamdani can’t run for president. So he’s trying to pick who does.

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 13, 2026

Mayor Zohran Mamdani displayed his growing national influence this week as he pushed to put working-class priorities at the center of the 2028 Democratic presidential race. NY1 political reporters Bobby Cuza and Kelly Mena and statehouse reporter Bernadette Hogan examine how the mayor could shape the field, even though his birth in Uganda makes him ineligible to run for president himself. They discuss his endorsement record and role in discouraging a primary challenge to House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries.

Then, following Democrats’ sweeping election victories, the team examines backlash from the left against Jeffries and Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer. With disagreements over Israel fueling much of the criticism, they discuss why Schumer may be more vulnerable to a challenge and what these divisions could mean for the Democratic Party.
