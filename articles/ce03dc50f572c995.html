Today on Pinstripe Alley – 11/24/26

By Madison Pavich · Pinstripe Alley · November 24, 2026

There were a couple moves of note on Monday, both related to the Yankees. First they lost a member of the 2026 team as Paul Goldschmidt agreed to a one-year deal with the Mets, but then they gained a new part of the 2027 team by signing Brent Suter to a one-year deal (with an option). Suter now joins Tim Hill and Brent Headrick as the lefties in a bullpen that is nearing completion, with just one or two more additions to make. All eyes are on Adrián Morejón now as the team has been eyeing adding him ever since the deadline, but he’ll command a hefty contract on the free agent market that might end up being too pricy for a team that still needs to address a couple parts of their lineup as well. If they do continue to bid for him though, they’ll have the chance to build a super bullpen for the first time in a while and enter 2027 with arguably the best relief corps in the league.
