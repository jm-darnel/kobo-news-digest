Today on Pinstripe Alley – 11/28/26

By Jake Devin · Pinstripe Alley · November 28, 2026

We’re easing towards the end of the Thanksgiving break, and closer and closer to a lockout-shaped break imposed by MLB owners. We’ll see if the league has one last offseason frenzy in it before things go dark. We saw a flurry ahead of Thanksgiving last week, and with the CBA set to expire on December 1st, it’s fair to wonder if we’ll get another surge to the finish line. Such a surge may have begun last night, the Blue Jays firing the hot stove up again last night to ink Jazz Chisholm Jr. to a one-year deal worth $20 million.

That marks the official end of Chisholm’s Yankee tenure, though it had been assumed for weeks that he’d be departing. The Yankees themselves have been quiet the last few days, though they seem poised to potentially make a major move. The Adrian Morejon sweepstakes appear ready to resolve, and ideally we’ll get a clearer picture of the shape of the Yankees’ offseason plan before the lights go off.
