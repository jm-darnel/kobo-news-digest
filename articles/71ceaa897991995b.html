Multiple Teams Showing Interest In Tyler Austin

By Darragh McDonald · MLB Trade Rumors (Yankees) · November 24, 2026

Free agent first baseman Tyler Austin is generating interest from multiple clubs, reports Joel Sherman of The New York Post . He lists the Mets, Yankees, Cubs, Blue Jays and Rays as the teams in the mix.

Austin, 35, would be a buy-low addition for any club and Sherman notes he would almost certainly have to settle for a minor league deal. He played in the majors from 2016 to 2019. He showed off some power in that time but struck out in 36.9% of his plate appearances.

He then went overseas and revitalized his career with the Yokohama DeNA BayStars of Japan’s Nippon Professional Baseball. He hit 48 home runs over the 2020 and 2021 seasons. The next two campaigns were largely wiped out by injuries but he got back on track in 2024, hitting another 25 home runs. Right knee issues limited him to 65 games in 2025 but he was able to add another 11 homers. Over that six-year stretch from 2020 to 2025, he had a .293/.377/.568 line for the BayStars with a manageable 22.8% strikeout rate.

Though clubs may be intrigued by his big power showing in Japan, his age and injury track record will limit him to minor league offers. For the Cubs, they still have Busch at first base and also added another big lefty bat today by agreeing to a deal with Teruaki Sato . If they can reunite with Austin on a minor league deal, they could get a look at him in spring training. If he’s healthy and playing well, he could perhaps earn a bench spot and help the Cubs play matchups.

For the Mets, they just added righty-swinging Paul Goldschmidt to platoon with lefty Jared Young . Sherman suggests the Goldschmidt signing could push Mark Vientos , another righty bat, off the roster. Bringing Austin aboard on a minor league deal would allow the Mets to effectively replace Vientos without using a roster spot, giving them cover for the event of a Goldschmidt injury.
