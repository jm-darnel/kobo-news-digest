Councilman Riley holds annual Uptown Thanksgiving Giveback

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 25, 2026

Councilman Kevin Riley held his annual Uptown Thanksgiving Giveback in Co-Op City on Monday.

Riley, along with dozens of volunteers, handed out free Thanksgiving meals for residents to take home to prepare. Hundreds of people lined up to take free turkeys, chicken, oxtail and all the fixings.

Neighbors say they’re thankful.

Various community organizations also helped out.

Spectrum, the parent company of NY1, was a sponsor of the event.
