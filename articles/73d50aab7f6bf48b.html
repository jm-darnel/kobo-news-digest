NYPD searches for suspect who police say stabbed a woman at random in Riverside Park

By Brittany Kriegstein · Gothamist · November 24, 2026

Police are looking for a man who approached a woman in a Manhattan park Monday night and randomly stabbed her, leaving her hospitalized, according to officials.

Officers came to Riverside Park near West 96th Street around 7:40 p.m., after the victim called 911 herself to report the stabbing, police said. The 26-year-old woman had been stabbed in her torso, and police said she was taken to Mount Sinai Morningside Hospital in stable condition.

According to the NYPD, the woman was walking in the park when the suspect approached her. He did not say anything to her before stabbing her and running away, police said.

Police said the suspect was wearing all black at the time.
