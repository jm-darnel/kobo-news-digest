Central Park rape suspect taken into custody at Miami Burger King, NYPD says

By Charles Lane · Gothamist · November 10, 2026

A man accused of raping a woman in Central Park last month was taken into custody Tuesday while working at a Burger King in Miami, police said, adding that he will also be charged in two earlier rapes in the park.

In a social media post, police identified the man as Dereck Rivera-Deleon and said the earlier attacks happened in 2021 and 2023.

NYPD detectives assigned to a U.S. Marshals regional fugitive task force took Rivera-Deleon into custody with help from the U.S. Marshals Service, officials said on X.

Police did not say what charges he faces in the October attack or when he will be brought back to New York.

It was not immediately clear whether Rivera-Deleon has a lawyer.
