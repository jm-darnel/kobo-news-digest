Federal government claims control of Newark Housing Authority

By Michael Sol Warren · Gothamist · November 20, 2026

There was no public announcement of the move outside of the letter and federal officials did not respond to questions Thursday night.

According to the letter dated Thursday from HUD Assistant Secretary Benjamin Hobbs to the leaders of the Newark Housing Authority, federal authorities had issued a final determination of substantial default for breaching the terms of a 2024 agreement between HUD and the authority.

HUD wrote that it warned the authority of its findings in two separate letters, dated Sept. 22 and Nov 5. While the local authority responded, the federal agency said the responses were insufficient.

“NHA has not taken good faith efforts to substantially improve,” Hobbs wrote in Thursday’s letter. “HUD considered NHA's reported progress and claimed corrective efforts before determining whether further action is warranted. Those efforts do not outweigh the continued failure to meet benchmarks that NHA itself agreed to over two years ago.”

Neither HUD nor Mayor Ras Baraka’s office immediately responded to requests for comment.

As part of the takeover, HUD dismissed the authority's entire board of commissioners. The Newark Housing Authority’s website had been updated on Thursday night to show that responsibilities of the board had been transferred to two officials in HUD’s Office of Public and Indian Housing: Deputy Assistant Secretary Shylon Ferry and Associate Deputy Assistant Secretary Daniel Sherrod.

A Newark Housing Authority board meeting scheduled for Friday has been cancelled, according to a notice posted on the authority’s website.

“NHA's day-to-day operations and resident services will continue as usual under HUD oversight,” the notice read.
