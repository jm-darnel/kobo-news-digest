South Carolina State defeats Delaware State 26-16 to claim outright MEAC championship

By Associated Press · Amsterdam News · November 22, 2026

ORANGEBURG, S.C. (AP) — Keely Watson threw for 211 yards and South Carolina State wrapped up another Mid-Eastern Athletic Conference championship with a 26-16 victory over Delaware State on Saturday.

South Carolina State (10-2, 5-0) has won or shared the MEAC championship in all three seasons under coach Chennis Berry and has 21 conference championships overall.

Watson’s 17-yard touchdown pass to Troy Hillman gave the Bulldogs a 6-0 lead late in the first quarter but Delaware State blocked the PAT and Geovanny Fabian returned it for a defensive 2-point conversion.

Jackson connected on a 37-yard field goal early in the fourth and Josh Shaw’s 1-yard touchdown run made it 26-16 with four minutes left.

Noah Brannock had 227 yards passing for Delaware State (6-6, 2-3).

Up next

South Carolina State will represent the MEAC in the Celebration Bowl on Dec. 12 in Atlanta.
