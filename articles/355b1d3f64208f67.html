Today on Pinstripe Alley – 11/27/26

By Madison Pavich · Pinstripe Alley · November 27, 2026

Thanksgiving has come and gone, and if you were one of many watching the football games on display you were treated to some instant classics with Bears-Lions and Eagles-Cowboys giving us some wild comebacks and last-second theatrics (Chiefs-Bills came down to the wire too). Setting our sights back on baseball, the sport continues to approach the end of the CBA and the beginning of what looks to be a nasty lockout, with just four days left before it all goes down. The Yankees are in hot pursuit of one big bullpen signing to wrap up their relief corps retooling before that happens, and we’ll be on high alert if they manage to land Adrián Morejón over the weekend before the buzzer sounds.

We’ve got a busy day as we get back into the swing of things, starting with Jonathan giving out a report card to Carlos Rodón for an abbreviated season. Peter considers Seiya Suzuki’s potential fit on the Yankees’ roster with a fairly similar comp to Randy Arozarena, Matt takes us back to the days of the first Yankees championship for Bullet Joe Bush’s birthday, and John has some fun examining the context of the Percy Jackson series’ choice to use a Yankees cap as a magic item.
