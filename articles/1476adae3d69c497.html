Yankees news: You can put those ‘pen worries to Bed-nar

By Kento Mizuno · Pinstripe Alley · November 18, 2026

MLB Trade Rumors | Charlie Wright : With the Rule 5 draft fast approaching, the Yankees selected Carlos Lagrange’s contract, adding him to the 40-man roster to prevent him from being poached by other teams. While Lagrange never did get his cup of coffee in 2026, he showcased elite stuff in Triple-A and remains one of the Yankees’ top prospects, with a 2027 debut all but certain. Protecting him from the Rule 5 draft was a no-brainer. The Yankees also added LHP Kyle Carr and OF Garrett Martin to the 40-man. Carr is currently rated as a 40FV prospect by FanGraphs and posted a 4.58 ERA (4.82 FIP) in 21 games across Double-A and Triple-A last year. Meanwhile, the 26-year-old Martin isn’t a top prospect by any means, but he did hit .270/.337/.570 in the high minors in 2026.

Sports Info Solutions | Mark Simon : There’s been some talk of this year’s HOF ballot being weaker than previous years. One thing is for certain; it’s grittier than ever before, thanks to the inclusion of one Brett Gardner. This piece by Simon highlights Gardner’s defensive value, which accounts for a big chunk of his impressive career bWAR total of 44 (FanGraphs has him at 37.3). Gardner isn’t a Hall of Famer, and he’s likely to be a one-and-done case, but he was a very good player for a pretty long time, and we should take this chance to celebrate his career.
