Terms and Deliverables

Final edited photographs will be delivered through a private digital key within 5–7 business days. Each shoot includes a curated selection of the strongest images; final image quantities vary according to event duration, guest flow, access, lighting, and program activity.

Clients receive a non-exclusive license to use delivered images for event promotion, social media, websites, email, press outreach, and internal marketing. Harlem World Magazine retains copyright to all photographs and may use images for its editorial, promotional, portfolio, and archival purposes.

A 50 percent non-refundable retainer secures the event date. The remaining balance is due before or on the day of the event. Please provide at least 30 days’ notice for bookings. Short-notice requests may be accommodated based on availability and can be subject to rush-booking rates. Clients may request one rescheduling with at least 14 days’ written notice, subject to photographer availability. Cancellations within 14 days of the event may require full payment.

Clients are responsible for photographer access, credentials, appropriate venue entry, safe working conditions, a designated event contact, and a clear photo area for red-carpet coverage. Additional coverage must be approved by the client or authorized event contact and is billed at $200 per hour.

For availability, package details, and a customized quote, contact advertising@harlemworldmagazine.com. Please include your event date, venue, anticipated guest count, requested coverage hours, step-and-repeat details, and desired image-delivery timeline.
