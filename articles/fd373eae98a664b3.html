Harlem Legionnaires’ Task Force Town Hall & Anniversary

Harlem World · November 16, 2026

On Saturday, November 14th, the Harlem Legionnaires’ Task Force (HLTF) hosted a Town Hall and One Year Anniversary at Williams Institutional CME Church.

Speaker Dr. Alister Martin, Commissioner of the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene, outlined how DOHMH is working with Mayor Mamdani’s Office on a five-step approach to improve the city’s communication, prevention, and response time. He emphasized being better prepared ahead of future Legionnaires’ Disease outbreaks and other health crises.

Deputy Manhattan Borough President, Miesha Smith, encouraged tenants to organize and recommended raising awareness among landlords, enabling them to comply with regulations and keep their tenants safe from Legionnaires’ Disease and other water-borne illnesses.

Epidemiologist Dr. Victoria Lynch explained how Legionella bacteria gets into the lungs and environmental factors that impact the spread. She called for increased research funding to study Legionella bacteria and other pathogens.

Elected officials Dr. Yusef Salaam (NYC Council Member, District 9), Jordan Wright (NYS Assemblyman, District 70) and closing speaker, Cordell Cleare (NYS Senator, District 30), all spoke about the devastation that Legionnaires’ Disease has had on their constituents. They called for transparency and accountability from DOHMH and landlords.

A panel of leaders from Manhattan Community Boards 8, 9, and 10 spoke about how they rallied around their communities while dealing with Legionnaires’ Disease in their districts.

Maria Freeman, a founding member of the Harlem Legionnaires’ Task Force, informed attendees about how they support passing Legionnaires’ Disease legislation in the City Council and Capitol. HLTF was instrumental in passing NYC Local Law 159 this year, which requires more frequent testing and regulation of cooling towers.
