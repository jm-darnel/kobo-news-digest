Newly elected New York House members attend orientation in Washington

By Shannon Ryan · NY1 · November 9, 2026

More than 100 newly elected House members are in Washington for orientation, ahead of their swearing-in ceremony in January.

“Everything’s new,” said Representative-elect Micah Lasher of Manhattan.

Six of the more than 100 first term-members are from New York, with five Democrats joining the state’s congressional delegation. The sole Republican — North Country Representative-elect Anthony Constantino.

Representatives-elect Claire Valdez and Darializa Avila Chevalier will join Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez as New York City Democratic Socialists of America members in Congress.

“[It’s] something I’m still thinking about,” said Avila Chevalier, who will soon represent Upper Manhattan and the Bronx.

Valdez, who represents Brooklyn and Queens, similarly stated, “I remain uncommitted for the moment.” She explained, “[Congressional progressives] want to see Medicare for All be advanced. We want to see the federal government be a force for good in working people’s lives. And that can include affordable housing.”

Representative-elect Brad Lander, who earned Mayor Zohran Mamdani’s endorsement in his fight to represent Manhattan and Brooklyn, broke with Valdez and Avila Chevalier.

“I will be supporting Hakeem Jeffries for speaker. I’m excited to vote for the first Black and first Brooklyn Speaker of the House,” he stated.

Lower Hudson Valley Representative-elect Cait Conley, who flipped a red district to earn her seat, told Spectrum News, “I think zooming out, right? What the American people showed us, what voters in my district showed me, was that they are sick of the political dysfunction. They’re sick of the infighting. They’re sick of the partisanship, and so what I don’t think Democrats should do is allow hyper-partisanship and infighting of our own party to be what we lead with.”
