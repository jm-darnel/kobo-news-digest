Councilwoman De La Rosa hosts annual Thanksgiving food giveaway

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 25, 2026

A huge Thanksgiving food giveaway took over the United Palace Theater in Washington Heights earlier this week.

Lines were wrapped around the historic building.

Organizers say more than 1,000 people showed up. People got to take home free turkeys, fresh vegetables, canned goods and soft drinks.

Various other community associations pitched in.

Spectrum, the parent company of NY1, was a sponsor of the event.
