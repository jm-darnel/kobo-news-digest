Man arrested in Brooklyn runner's killing worked at nearby school

By Spectrum News NY1 · NY1 · November 9, 2026

A 27-year-old man accused of fatally stabbing Brooklyn runner Katie Suarez is facing murder charges, as new details from court records raise questions about how he was hired to work as a teacher's aide.

According to court documents, security camera footage placed Mohassib near the scene before and after the attack, and showed him running away.

Court documents detail that Mohassib told investigators he went to the path with a knife because “people were going to come and kill his family.”

He told police he sat on a bench and “became covered in blood,” but had not stabbed anyone. Prosecutors say he told investigators he ran home and got rid of his bloody clothes and the knife.

Now, questions remain about how Mohassib was hired to work at P.S. 200 in Bath Beach, just a few blocks from where Suarez was attacked.

At the time of his arrest, Mohassib was working as a teacher's aide. Before joining the Department of Education, he worked as an NYPD officer for a little more than a month in 2022 before being granted extended military leave. He also had a brief stint with the Marine Corps before being dismissed for being mentally unfit. He later applied for reinstatement with the NYPD but was denied.

NY1 spoke with a man who knows Mohassib and his family through the Islamic center around the corner from the school where he worked. He said Mohassib and his father both have mental health problems.

“His father is also with the same mental problems and he's in the, uh, mental facility and this guy also has the mental problems. I told my friend, how is he going to give the education to the kids if he is mental,” the man said.

Mohassib is being held without bail after being arraigned on murder charges this weekend. He is due back in court tomorrow.
