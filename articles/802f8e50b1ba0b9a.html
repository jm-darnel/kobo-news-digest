Yankees news: Manfred and the owners strike again

By Andrés Chávez · Pinstripe Alley · November 19, 2026

New York Post | Joel Sherman : ($) Sherman reports that the Yankees are interested in former Orioles manager Brandon Hyde as their new bench coach and that Hyde may sit down for a formal interview in the coming days. Brad Ausmus held the position for three seasons before departing last week to take the Astros managerial job. Hyde became the manager in Baltimore in 2019, earning AL Manager of the Year honors in 2023 after leading the O’s to a 101-61 record, but was fired early in the 2025 season after Baltimore got off to a slow start.

MLB.com | Mark Feinsand : According to Feinsand, the Yankees remain ‘a team to watch’ in the Adrián Morejón sweepstakes even after closer David Bednar accepted their qualifying offer. “New York has spent big on setup men in the past, and adding Morejon to the bullpen would give them one of the best combos since, well, Morejon and Miller in San Diego the past couple years.” The left-hander has been on the Yanks’ radar for years, and would be a superb, albeit expensive, addition. He posted a 2.83 ERA in 86 innings this year with the Padres, with 107 strikeouts.
