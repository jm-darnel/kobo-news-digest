Yankees To Sign Jonathan Loaisiga

By Charlie Wright · MLB Trade Rumors (Yankees) · November 25, 2026

Nov. 25: Loaisiga is actually guaranteed $19MM on the deal, rather than $18MM, reports Jorge Castillo of ESPN. The 2029 club option is valued at $9.5MM and comes with a $500K buyout. Loaisiga can earn an additional $750K worth of incentives per season. He will make even $9MM salaries in the two guaranteed years, per Jon Heyman of The New York Post .

Nov. 20: Reliever Jonathan Loaisiga is heading to the Yankees on a two-year deal. It’s an $18MM agreement that includes a club option for 2029. New York makes yet another addition to its bullpen by bringing Loaisiga back to the Bronx. Loaisiga is a client of the Ballengee Group.

Loaisiga left the Yankees in free agency after struggling with health and performance. He had to settle for a minor league deal with the Diamondbacks last winter. The veteran broke camp with the team and worked his way into a high-leverage role in Arizona. He rebuilt his value enough to earn a multi-year agreement from the Yankees.

Elbow injuries plagued Loaisiga’s final years in New York. His average fastball velocity had dipped to 96.7 mph in 2025. That number was back up to 97.6 this past year. Loaisiga posted modest strikeout numbers and a mediocre 10.6% swinging-strike rate, but getting a tick back on his heater was a positive development.

After a brief stint in the Giants’ organization, Loaisiga signed a minor league deal with the Yankees in 2016. He quickly moved through the system and established himself as one of the more intriguing young arms in the minors in the late 2010s. Loaisiga had been strictly a starter in the minors, but took on a multi-faceted role when he debuted with the Yankees in 2018.

Loaisiga struggled to find a consistent role in New York over his first three MLB seasons. He broke out in 2021, delivering a 2.17 ERA over a career-high 70 2/3 innings. The Yankees ditched any plans to use him as a starter, keeping him in the bullpen on a full-time basis. Loaisiga tied for the team lead with 18 holds. He also earned the first five saves of his career.

Shoulder and elbow issues limited Loaisiga to 51 1/3 innings from 2023 to 2025. He went to free agency as little more than a reclamation project. Arizona adjusted his approach, having Loaisiga rely more on his four-seamer and changeup instead of being so sinker-heavy. He still leaned on the sinker more than any other pitch, but it had just a 35.1% usage rate, his lowest since the shortened 2020 season. The result was elite contact numbers, minimal walks, and tons of groundballs. Loaisiga ranked in the 92nd percentile or better in hard-hit rate, groundball rate, barrel rate, and walk rate.

General manager Brian Cashman said one of the primary focuses of the offseason was the bullpen. He’s certainly made it a priority so far. New York re-signed Tim Hill and retained closer David Bednar , who accepted a qualifying offer. Loaisiga is the third addition to the unit in the past week.

With Hill and Bednar in the fold, the Yankees have nearly every piece back from last year’s bullpen, which ranked second in ERA. The final missing piece is free agent Paul Blackburn , who has been connected to the club. Loaisiga could potentially slot into the role vacated by Blackburn if there isn’t a reunion there.

Nicaraguan broadcaster Levi Luna was the first to report Loaisiga’s signing. Jorge Castillo of ESPN reported the club option for 2029. Mark Feinsand of MLB.com reported the value of the contract.
