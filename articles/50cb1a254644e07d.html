Senate Majority Leader Andrea Stewart-Cousins dies at 76

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 27, 2026

State Senate Majority Leader Andrea Stewart-Cousins has died, according to a statement.

"It is with profound sadness that I announce the passing of New York State Senate Temporary President and Majority Leader Andrea Stewart-Cousins, who died on November 27, 2026, at the age of 76," Senate Majority Communications Director Mike Murphy wrote in a statement.

Stewart-Cousins was the longest-serving majority leader in state history. She was also the first woman to serve in that role.

She is survived by her three children and six grandchildren.
