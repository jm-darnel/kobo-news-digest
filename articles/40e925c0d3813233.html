The National Arts Club Celebrates The Holiday Season With Carols On The Stoop And Broadway Favorites

Harlem World · November 17, 2026

Highlights include the Club’s traditional holiday concert on its stoop with the Gramercy Brass Orchestra, free and open to the public, and an evening of tap and swing with two-time Bessie Award winner Caleb Teicher.

Featured Programs, all free and open to the public.

Holiday Songs from the Stoop – Monday, December 7, 6 p.m. The Principal Quintet of the Gramercy Brass Orchestra performs favorite songs and carols on the stoop of the Club’s historic landmark, under the direction of trumpeter John Henry Lambert Jr., who grew up in the Gramercy Park neighborhood and founded the ensemble. The orchestra has won numerous awards, including First Place at the North American Brass Band Competition.

Swing Out and Tap into the Holidays! – Tuesday, December 8, 6:30 p.m. Two-time Bessie Award winner Caleb Teicher presents an interdisciplinary performance blending tap, jazz, and swing dance with wintertime classics, followed by an intimate Q&A with the artist.

Songs of the Season – From Broadway and Beyond – Monday, December 14, 6:30 p.m. Broadway performers including Micah Young, Nicolas King, Ali Ewoldt, and Alyssa Melder perform songs from shows and films that speak to the season.

Additional Public Programs

• Dec. 1, 5 p.m.: Author Meghan O’Gieblyn discusses her new memoir, Will and Attention, in conversation with editor and author Gerald Howard

• Dec. 2, 6:30 p.m.: A Gulf Coast Christmas with Michael Harold and Quinn Peeper, New Orleans hosts and authors of the new hospitality book Coastal Shindig

Exhibitions

CYFEST 17 | Hybrid Ecologies: Natura Naturans (Lower Galleries, November 30 – January 1, 2027) brings together artists and collectives working in painting, drawing, installation, film and video, photography, and new media. The exhibition reimagines nature as natura naturans, nature that creates, through algorithmic systems, light, and sound. CYFEST 17 is part of a series of traveling exhibitions organized by CYLAND Foundation in collaboration with the Kolodzei Art Foundation and Leonardo, with past editions in Thessaloniki, Greece, and Venice, Italy.

Galleries are free and open to the public Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., and Saturday and Sunday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. (subject to change).

Club News

About the National Arts Club

Founded in 1898, the National Arts Club is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit with a mission to stimulate, foster, and promote public interest in the arts and to educate people in the fine arts. Annually, the Club offers more than 150 free programs to the public, including exhibitions, theatrical and musical performances, and lectures and readings, attracting an audience of more than 25,000 members of the general public. Featured programs focus on all disciplines of the arts. For a complete list of events or to learn more, please visit nacnyc.org.
