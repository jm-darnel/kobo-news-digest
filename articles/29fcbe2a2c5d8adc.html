Alleged Central Park rapist charged with 2 counts of rape, NYPD says

By Brittany Kriegstein · Gothamist · November 18, 2026

Dereck Rivera-Deleon, 30, arrived in New York City on Tuesday — a week after he was taken into custody at a Burger King in Miami by NYPD detectives assigned to the U.S. Marshals Regional Fugitive Task Force, according to the department.

Police said he attacked a 39-year-old woman near East 67th Street and East Drive around 8:30 p.m. on Oct. 24. Officials said he would also be charged with two earlier rapes in the park, in 2023 and 2021.

Rivera-Deleon is scheduled to be arraigned in Manhattan Criminal Court. Information for his lawyer is not yet available.

Police said his most recent New York address was in the Wakefield neighborhood of the Bronx.
