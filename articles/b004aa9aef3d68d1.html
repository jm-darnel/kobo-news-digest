NYPD: 2 Co-Op City peace officers shot, suspect dead

By Spectrum News Staff, Louis Finley · NY1 · November 16, 2026

Two Co-Op City peace officers were shot during a confrontation just before noon Wednesday at the Bronx development, according to the NYPD.

NYPD officers responded to a request for assistance from the Co-Op City Department of Public Safety during a wellness check around 11:45 a.m. Upon arrival, they found that the suspect had discharged a gun at the peace officers and barricaded himself in an apartment.

Police said they recovered a firearm at the scene, and the investigation is ongoing.

Neighbors told NY1 that they're still trying to wrap their heads around the incident.

“You got these cops for Co-Op City, and they respond quick. And they’re always around, so it’s safe,” Juan Quinones, a neighbor, said.

“I’ve been here [for] 25ish years. It’s been mostly elderly families, children. We’ve all grown up seeing each other. You know here and there, just like any neighborhood, there’s something that’s bound to happen,” Imani Martinez, a neighbor, said.

Co-Op City is the largest co-op development in the country, with up to 50,000 residents in five sections consisting of 35 high-rise buildings and some townhouses.
