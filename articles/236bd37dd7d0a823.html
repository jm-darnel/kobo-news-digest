Thanksgiving food giveaway at the South Jamaica Houses in Queens

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 25, 2026

A special donation for hundreds of families in Queens this Thanksgiving.

Councilman Ty Hankerson and his staff held a Thanksgiving food giveaway at the South Jamaica Houses.

Residents of the NYCHA public housing complex lined up for free turkey, chicken and oxtail.

Hankerson says it’s important to give back to the community and help families in need this holiday.

“It’s been difficult in this country the last few years, and so we wanted to make sure as local electeds that we were providing some relief,” he said. “This means everything to me. My mother is also out here volunteering with us at different locations, and my heart for service really came from her. She taught me that you have to serve your community, and so this is a result of that.”

Hankerson also handed out food at the nearby Baisley Houses.

Spectrum, the parent company of NY1, was a sponsor of the event.
