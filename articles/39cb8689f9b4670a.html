Ask Pinstripe Alley: Yankees mailbag questions request

By Madison Pavich · Pinstripe Alley · November 16, 2026

There’s still plenty of time to figure out where the team wants to make those improvements, but if they want to avoid a free-for-all before the start of the year time is running out. There’s a couple of weeks left before December 1st, when the CBA is set to expire and the league is expected to enter a lockout. How long it’ll lastand if games will be lost is unclear, but the transaction freeze that will follow is expected to last for at least a large chunk of the normal offseason cycle. Should the team try to jump out and get themselves on the board with an upgrade beforehand? How will the lockout affect the posting system, with plenty of eyes on Japanese standout Teruaki Sato looking to come stateside? If you have questions like these, or anything else on your mind, send ‘em in for a chance to be featured in our Yankees mailbag.

Answers will run on Friday afternoon. All questions received by the night of November 19th will be considered. You can leave your submissions in the comment section below or by e-mail to pinstripealleyblog [at] gmail [dot] com.
