College Football’s Surprise Contenders: Who Could Shake Up The 2026-27 Race?

Harlem World · November 16, 2026

College football is moving beyond preseason predictions, with Utah and UCLA unbeaten as other contenders enter the playoff race. Conference standings and remaining games will shape their chances.

Utah Has the Strongest Case to Break Through

Utah is 9-0 and leads the Big 12 at 6-0. The Utes beat Arkansas 43-10, then shut out Utah State 33-0, and most recently defeated BYU 28-20. The committee ranks Utah No. 2, behind Georgia and ahead of UCLA.

UCLA’s Undefeated Run Faces Its Hardest Stretch

UCLA is 9-0, 6-0 in Big Ten play, and No. 4 in the CFP rankings. Its 17-15 road win at Oregon is a key ranked victory. Wins over Wisconsin and Minnesota followed. The Bruins last started 9-0 back in 1998.

Illinois visits the Rose Bowl November 13. UCLA then travels to Michigan before hosting USC. An undefeated finish would simplify the committee’s evaluation, while a loss could tighten the at-large race.

Texas Tech Can Turn Position Into Leverage

Texas Tech is 8-1, 5-1 in the Big 12, and No. 12 in the CFP rankings. The Red Raiders remain in contention, but their selection is not guaranteed. Winning the Big 12 would provide a direct path into the 12-team field, with Utah leading the conference at 6-0.

Texas Tech visits No. 25 Oklahoma State (6-3) on November 14, followed by a road game against Baylor and a home matchup with TCU. Winning out would strengthen its record and conference standing, while a loss could hurt its title hopes and CFP position.

Wake Forest Needs the ACC Route

Wake Forest is 8-1 and 5-1 in the ACC, with its only loss against Miami. The Demon Deacons are No. 13 in the CFP rankings. They beat Virginia 51-7 and FCS opponent Merrimack 77-0, a less demanding test of playoff readiness.

SMU is 7-2 and No. 25 in the AP poll, making the matchup a chance for Wake to strengthen its résumé. A win could shift weekly college football trends by improving the Demon Deacons’ position in the playoff discussion. Georgia Tech and Duke remain on the schedule. An ACC title would give Wake a direct path into the 12-team field.
