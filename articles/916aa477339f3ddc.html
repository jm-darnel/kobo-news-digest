By Jake Devin · Pinstripe Alley · November 19, 2026

We had our first blockbuster, or something resembling one, yesterday, the Royals sending 2025 All-Star Maikel Garcia to Seattle for right-hander Bryce Miller. Maybe it’s not the most explosive deal, but it’s an interesting one, the two teams swapping talented young players that better suit their needs. Some fans were hoping that the Yankees and Royals could perhaps link up on a deal involving Garcia, with the Yankees in need of an infielder and the Royals looking for controllable pitching, but that ship has sailed.
