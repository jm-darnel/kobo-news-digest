Macy's presents new floats for 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade

By Roger Clark · NY1 · November 18, 2026

It’s the kind of place where anything seems possible, with old and new friends preparing to be seen by the world.

“There's Macy’s colleagues who are working in this building, carpenters, welders, designers, production specialists, that are making this parade come to life,” Kathleen Wright, the director of production operations at Macy’s Studios, said.

Macy’s gave a sneak preview of seven new floats that will be part of the parade, including "Dashing All The Way" from DoorDash, "Hogwarts Holiday" from HBO, "Mr. Peanut Power Plant" from Planters and Purina’s "Salute to Service Dogs."

Another one is a tribute to puppeteer and illustrator Tony Sarg, who had a big role in the parade’s history.

“In 1928, he had the idea to turn marionette puppets upside down and fill them with helium, and now you see your beloved favorite characters like Snoopy or Mickey Mouse, that are going to be flying down the parade route,” Wright said.

There is a "Parade All Stars" float featuring characters like Popeye and Olive Oil, Sonic the Hedgehog and Bullwinkle.

“We’ve given everything we got," Will Coss, the executive producer and vice president at Macy’s Studios, said.

"We're going bigger and better than we've ever gone before, we have over 100 inflatables, we have more floats, brand new floats than we have ever had, more marching bands, performance groups. It’s going to be the biggest and best parade," Coss added.

Coss said it all starts in the studio, where everything is done by hand, ready to be delivered to fans on Thanksgiving morning.
