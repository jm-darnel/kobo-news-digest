Yankees news: MLB continues to frolic with private equity

By Joshua Diemert · Pinstripe Alley · November 20, 2026

Front Office Sports | Ben Horney : After the Yankees reached an investment deal with Apollo Global Management over the summer, the rapacious claws of private equity continue to sink into what was once the great American pastime. Swiss firm Seidler Equity Partners bought out an as-yet-undisclosed stake in Rawlings, the official baseball supplier to MLB, that the league has owned since 2018. MLB did say they would maintain “meaningful ownership” of the company, but it seems Seidler is the controlling partner. Expect offense to rise once the ghouls learn rubber is cheaper than leather.

NJ.com | Adam Zagoria : The Yankees have reportedly been in contact with Randy Arozerena’s camp, as the mercurial outfielder courts offers in his first true taste of free agency. The Yankees’ struggle from the right side of the plate was a story from the moment Aaron Judge went down, and in those dog days of summer where it felt like they faced a left-handed starter four out of five days, right-handed power would be an excellent add to the roster, and Randy’s never had a bad year at the plate. His defense and baserunning though…

Yahoo! Sports | Jackson Kruse : The Yankees made a small splash yesterday, inking switch-hitting outfielder Allan Castro to a minor league deal. Castro, just 23, became a minor-league free agent after playing in the Red Sox system since 2019, was a little underwhelming with an 88 wRC+ in 119 games for Triple-A Pawtucket, but per FanGraphs boasts above average power both raw and in-game. If the Yankees can build some kind of contact profile, he might end up a surprising depth piece going forward.
