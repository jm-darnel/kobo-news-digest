Yankees news: Brian Cashman taps bullpen as top priority

By Nick Power · Pinstripe Alley · November 14, 2026

MLB.com | Bryan Hoch : After the conclusion of the annual GM meetings this week, the Yankees’ offseason priorities are beginning to come into focus. Brian Cashman acknowledged in an interview that rebuilding his bullpen tops the list. “Two of the three pitchers going into our eighth and ninth inning are gone; (Paul) Blackburn and (David) Bednar right now are free agents, so we’ve got our work to do,” the Yankees GM said. “And because of that, I am casting a wide net.”

Options include reunions with free agents Bednar, Blackburn, Tim Hill, and Jonathan Loáisiga as well as Adrian Morejon, who was linked to the Yankees while with the Padres this past season. Other potential areas for improvement include third base, second base, and catcher.

FanGraphs | Matthew Roberson : After another disappointing October, an overarching look at where the Yankees go from here. Roberson recaps some of the problems at hand — an aging roster, thin farm system, and depleted coaching staff — before pinpointing what he believes to be the core issue. Since signing DJ LeMahieu before the 2019 season, the most impactful external free agent position player Cashman has brought in is Paul Goldschmidt, the aging future Hall of Famer who’s been a helpful part-time player but is no longer the type of impact bat the Yankees may need to get over the top. If Cashman eschews a major addition to the lineup this offseason, Roberson predicts one more year of World Series drought.
