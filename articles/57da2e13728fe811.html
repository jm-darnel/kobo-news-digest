Students, neighbors question Columbia over locked campus entrances

By Louis Finley · NY1 · November 18, 2026

Columbia University students and neighbors questioned a university official Wednesday night over restricted access to College Walk, which is a Morningside Heights passage that connects Broadway and Amsterdam Avenue at 116th Street.

“I used to be a student here, and they were always open. It was super convenient,” one person said outside the school.

Another student told NY1 that campus buildings should remain secured, but College Walk should be more accessible.

“I do think that the buildings inside should probably still be badge access, but that’s it,” the student said.

Last week, a judge ruled the public has no right to cross College Walk. Residents appealed Tuesday.

Columbia has said that despite the favorable ruling, it is looking at ways to expand access to the public.

“I think we all look forward to that committee being able to make a series of concrete recommendations in short order about how we can become more open,” Loftin Flowers of Columbia University said.

State Assemblymember-elect Eli Northrop said the university and the neighborhood are linked.

“Columbia is successful when Morningside Heights is successful and when the relationship between the neighborhood and the university is good,” Northrop said.

Lenna Nepomnyaschy, a neighbor, said students are also affected by the restricted access.

“They’re being deprived of a life here in New York City, which is what they came for,” Nepomnyaschy said. “They didn’t go to Wisconsin. They came to New York City because this is what they wanted.”
