United Airlines says system outage affecting departing flights is resolved

By Phil Corso · Gothamist · November 16, 2026

United Airlines said it has resolved a widespread technology outage that affected some departing flights Monday evening.

"We're aware of the widespread system outage, and our teams are working hard to get things back up and running as soon as possible," the first post said.

In an update shortly after 6 p.m., it said the issue had been resolved but did not say what caused it or how many flights were affected.

It also comes as customers of several major banks reported trouble accessing their online accounts Monday. There is no indication the outages are connected.

United advised passengers to check their flight status through its app or website.

This is a developing story and may be updated.
