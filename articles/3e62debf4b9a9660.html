Yankees Claim Chase Hampton

By AJ Eustace · MLB Trade Rumors (Yankees) · November 17, 2026

When Aaron Judge returned from the 60-day IL in August, New York designated Hampton for assignment to clear the necessary 40-man roster space. The Nationals claimed, then optioned Hampton on September 12, though he only stuck around for two weeks and did not get into game action for Double-A Harrisburg. The Cardinals claimed Hampton on September 26, followed by the Orioles on November 2. In the end, the waiver carousel brings Hampton back home to New York.

New York’s current crop of low-leverage relievers includes right-handers Yerry de los Santos and Kervin Castro , along with former starters Luis Gil and Clarke Schmidt , if they are not traded or non-tendered. Taking another flier on Hampton isn’t a bad idea to fill that kind of middle relief role. Before the TJS, Hampton’s fastball sat in the 92-94 mph range and touched 97 mph, while his slider was viewed as a plus pitch. Granted, some of the prospect shine has worn off, but there’s no harm in seeing if Hampton can regain his feel for his pitches with an additional year under his belt post-surgery.

So far in the offseason, the Yankees are seen as players for the top free agent relievers on the market. They are reportedly interested in lefty Adrián Morejón , who turned down the Padres’ qualifying offer this morning. They have also been linked with old friend Jonathan Loaisiga , and they would obviously like to retain Bednar. Brent Headrick and Hill are the two incumbents who played meaningful relief roles in 2026.
