Woman dies after tumbling from a moving SUV on the BQE near the Brooklyn Bridge

By Michael Sol Warren · Gothamist · November 8, 2026

A 37-year-old woman died after falling out of a moving car on the Brooklyn-Queens Expressway early Sunday morning.

Police said the woman opened the rear door of a Toyota Highlander and tumbled out of the car at around 2:30 a.m. The vehicle was travelling westbound on the BQE and approaching exit 28 near the Brooklyn Bridge.

An NYPD spokesperson said the woman suffered severe trauma from the collision, and first responders declared her dead at the scene.

No arrests have been made. Police said the investigation is ongoing.
