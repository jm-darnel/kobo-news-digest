Yankees news: Randy Arozarena on the team’s radar

By Madison Pavich · Pinstripe Alley · November 17, 2026

MLB Trade Rumors | Anthony Franco: The Yankees are apparently interested in adding Randy Arozarena to complete their outfield. Arozarena, coming off of a career-high 5.5 fWAR season with Seattle, has reportedly declined his qualifying offer and will become a free agent with the accompanying draft pick penalties should a team like the Yankees sign him. He isn’t a natural fit in their outfield with Cody Bellinger and Aaron Judge already entrenched at the corners, and the team’s DH situation is… complicated, but adding a bat like Arozarena is definitely worth considering at the very least.

NY Daily News | Gary Phillips: Speaking of the outfield, that center field gap could be filled in house if the Yankees are willing to trust Spencer Jones with a bigger role after his late season surge. However, the team appears to be less than confident that he could handle the job. Brian Cashman is on record saying that Jones is “capable” in center but “probably profiles better on [a] corner,” leading inquiring minds to wonder what the team will do with the outfielder over the offseason. If the team is willing to compromise on defense for a quality bat, it probably makes more sense to prefer going with the known quantity in Arozarena rather than Jones, but the team has stuck through with keeping him around after years of trade speculation.

AMNY | Joe Pantorno: The Yankees brought back one member of last year’s bullpen over the weekend when they agreed to a deal with Tim Hill, but another reunion with a recent Yankee might be out of the cards. The Yankees showed some initial interest in old friend Jonathan Loáisiga, who left for Arizona with a string of injury-riddled years following his 2021 breakout. Loáisiga dealt for the Diamondbacks this season, pitching to a 2.42 ERA in 67 innings, and that has attracted the attention of several suitors with the Mets, Dodgers, Blue Jays, and Phillies as the frontrunners. The Yankees have built their bullpen largely on picking up strays, so if the money is piling up on the table it males sense that he’ll wind up elsewhere.

MLB.com | Brian Murphy: The Hall of Fame ballot is officially out, and while it is a relatively weak year for candidates (Buster Posey seems like the only likely candidate to get above the 75 percent threshold) there are a number of former Yankees on the ballot including Brett Gardner’s first time as a candidate. Gardy doesn’t have the strongest case, but his incredible value as a defender might let him stick above the five percent threshold needed to stay on the ballot for future consideration.

FanGraphs | Jay Jaffe: The other Hall of Fame consideration going on is the Era Committee vote, and Jaffe’s next case to consider is Brian Sabean. The architect of the 2010s Giants got his start working for the Yankees’ scouting department playing a part in building the Core Four era, giving him an expansive list of accomplishments. He faces competition on the ballot from some of his former managers in Dusty Baker and Bruce Bochy though, and to what extent each of them will siphon votes from the others remains to be seen.
