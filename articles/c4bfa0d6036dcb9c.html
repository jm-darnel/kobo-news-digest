Immigration advocates urge City Council to pass new bills to restrict ICE

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 19, 2026

Advocates are calling on the City Council to pass new legislation that would further restrict the city’s cooperation with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement.

This all comes after a man was shot by a federal immigration officer last month in the Marble Hill neighborhood of Manhattan while in a car with his 5-year-old son.

Rosa Cohen-Cruz, the director of immigration policy at the Bronx Defenders, and Dave Basnet, the senior immigration strategist at the New York Civil Liberties Union, joined NY1's Bobby Cuza on “Inside City Hall” to discuss more.
