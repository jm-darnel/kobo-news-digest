After the blue wave: What’s next for New York?

It was a dominant election night for New York Democrats, led by Gov. Kathy Hochul’s roughly 20-point victory over Republican Bruce Blakeman. Attorney General Letitia James and Comptroller Tom DiNapoli also easily won their respective races, while Democrats flipped New York’s 17th Congressional District as incumbent Rep. Mike Lawler conceded to Kate Conley despite a major ballot-printing error in Westchester. Meanwhile, longtime Brooklynites Chuck Schumer and Hakeem Jeffries are poised to lead the Senate and House. NY1 political reporters Bobby Cuza, Dan Rivoli and Kelly Mena broke down a historic night for Democrats in New York.

After that, the “Off Topic” team examined Mayor Zohran Mamdani’s relationship with Hochul and what it could mean for upcoming budget negotiations, along with the New York City ballot questions, all of which passed with more than 60% support.
