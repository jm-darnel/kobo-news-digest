NYC, They’re Back! Join The Daddy Victory Club For Their Next Daddy & Me Walk In Harlem

Harlem World · November 10, 2026

Hello Dads and Families, Harlem, we’re coming back!

Following our first Daddy & Me Walk in October, Daddy Victory Club is excited to return to Harlem for another morning of fatherhood, family, and community!

Join us Saturday, November 14, at 10:00 a.m. as we bring Black fathers and our children together for a walk through Central Park.

Never miss new stories in Harlem

Our first Harlem walk was just the beginning. We’re continuing to build a community where fathers can connect, support one another, and create meaningful memories with our children.

Event Details

Date: Saturday, November 14, 2026 Time: 10:00–11:00 a.m. ET Meeting Location: Charles A. Dana Discovery Center, Central Park, at 110th Street between Fifth and Lenox Avenues, Harlem, NYC

Event Details: https://www.daddyvictoryclub.org/events/dvc-harlem-8pfm8

Whether you joined us in October or this will be your first DVC experience, we’d love to see you. Bring your children, invite another dad, and help us continue building something special in Harlem.

You don’t have to have fatherhood figured out. Just come walk with us!

For questions, please contact our NYC Ambassador, Douglas Gavin, at doug@daddyvictoryclub.org.

See you Saturday!
