How to help the victims of the Brooklyn building collapse

By Karen Yi · Gothamist · November 13, 2026

New Yorkers are stepping up to help the families affected by the fatal building collapse in Flatbush, which left eight people dead and 18 others injured.

Several organizations, including the YMCA and local elected leaders, are asking for donations, gift cards and new toiletries to help the families displaced by the disaster.

“These families lost everything,” Councilmember Farah Louis, who represents Flatbush, said during a press conference Friday. She said her office was standing with families to “make sure you get the shelter you need, the relief you need and the resources you need to build back again.”

Here’s a list of ways to help.

New toiletries

Organizations are asking for new and unopened donations of toiletries. That includes toothpaste, toothbrushes, deodorant, soap, body wipes, tissues, tampons and pads, hairbrushes and baby wipes. Those donations can be dropped off at several locations:

• Flatbush YMCA at 1401 Flatbush Ave.

• Brooklyn Borough Hall at 209 Joralemon St. (enter via Adams Street)

• Councilmember Farah Louis’ office at 2253 Nostrand Ave.

• The organization D.R.U.M. at 1468 Flatbush Ave.

Gift cards

New Yorkers can also donate gift cards in $25, $50 or $100 denominations that will go directly to impacted families to use for groceries, clothing and other household essentials. Gift cards can be for food, phone service or cash. Those donations can be dropped off at several locations:

• Flatbush YMCA at 1401 Flatbush Ave.

• Brooklyn Borough Hall at 209 Joralemon St. (enter via Adams Street)

• Councilmember Farah Louis’ office at 2253 Nostrand Ave.

• The organization D.R.U.M. at 1468 Flatbush Ave.

Monetary donations

Anderson Velez, 35, whose wife and two children fell into the rubble and survived, also launched a fundraising page as his 9-year-old recovers in the hospital. The family had just moved out of a homeless shelter and moved into their apartment eight days before the collapse.

Clothing

Mark Appel, the group’s founder, said the organization is accepting new or gently used clothing in good condition. In particular, they are looking for winter gear such as gloves, scarves and sweaters, as well as underwear. People are encouraged to drop off sizes medium to large, as well as kids’ sizes.

Toys

Community Board 17 is hosting an annual holiday party on Dec. 18 and will invite the families affected by the collapse. New Yorkers can contribute new toys or children’s gifts for the celebration, which can be dropped off at the Community Board 17 offices at 4112 Farragut Rd.

New and unused bedding

Sheets, pillows, pillowcases and blankets are being accepted at the following locations:

• Community Board 17 at 4112 Farragut Rd.

• Clarendon Road Church at 3304 Clarendon Rd.

• Lenox Road Baptist Church at 1356 Nostrand Ave.
