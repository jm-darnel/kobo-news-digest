Woman arrested after climbing outside of a Manhattan skyscraper's 91st floor, NYPD says

By Charles Lane · Gothamist · November 17, 2026

An 18-year-old woman from Portland, Oregon, was pulled off the outside of a Midtown luxury residential tower around its 91st floor Monday evening, police said.

Vivian Warnock was arrested after people called police to report someone climbing the exterior of the Steinway Tower at 111 West 57th St., according to the NYPD. Officers responded just before 5 p.m. and saw her climbing the building's exterior, police said. FDNY firefighters pulled her down, and she was arrested at 5:30 p.m., police said. No injuries were reported.

Police charged Warnock with burglary, a felony, but Manhattan prosecutors reduced the charge to criminal trespassing, a misdemeanor. She pleaded not guilty Tuesday and was released on her own recognizance, according to prosecutors. Court records did not yet include her lawyer information.
