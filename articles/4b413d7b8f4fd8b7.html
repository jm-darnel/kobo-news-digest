Latest On Adrian Morejon’s Market

By Anthony Franco · MLB Trade Rumors (Yankees) · November 25, 2026

With Trevor Rogers coming off the board last night, Adrian Morejon is the only unsigned pitcher who is still attached to the qualifying offer. The star setup man reportedly plans to remain in the bullpen despite getting calls from teams interested in building him up as a starter.

There hasn’t been a ton of reporting about the teams involved in Morejon’s bidding. The Marlins were linked to the Cuban-born southpaw a couple weeks ago but would be a very surprising landing spot for a pitcher who could command a $75-100MM guarantee. The Yankees were also tied to Morejon a few weeks ago, yet that preceded David Bednar surprisingly accepting the $23.15MM qualifying offer. New York has also agreed to a two-year, $18MM deal with Jonathan Loáisiga while finalizing cheaper one-year contracts for southpaws Tim Hill and Brent Suter .

Another AL East club is also in the mix. Mitch Bannon of The Athletic reported on Tuesday that the Blue Jays were engaged on Morejon. That report preceded their agreement with right-hander Kaima Taira on a five-year, $80MM deal this evening. Perhaps that reduces Toronto’s urgency to spend big on another pitcher given their need for a bat, but Taira is surely going to get an opportunity to start. The Jays have a couple high-end righty relievers at the back of the bullpen in Louis Varland and Tyler Rogers . They’re much lighter from the left side, where Mason Fluharty (a pure lefty specialist) is their only reliable option.

Toronto and New York both exceeded the luxury tax threshold this year. They’d both forfeit their second- and fifth-highest draft choices, plus $1MM from their international bonus pools, to sign a qualified free agent.

There is at least one big-market Eastern division team that doesn’t appear to be strongly involved. Matt Gelb and Charlotte Varnes of The Athletic write that the Phillies are not believed to be serious suitors for Morejon. Philadelphia is light on lefty relief and could use another setup option to pair with Jonathan Bowlan in front of star closer Jhoan Duran . They arguably have bigger needs in the corner outfield and at third base than splurging on a reliever, however.
