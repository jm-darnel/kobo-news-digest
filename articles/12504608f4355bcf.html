Yankees news: Sato signs with Cubs; Peralta joins O’s

By Nick Power · Pinstripe Alley · November 25, 2026

MLB Trade Rumors | Charlie Wright : The Cubs came to terms with Teruaki Sato on a six-year, $120 million deal Tuesday. While the 27-year-old, who won the 2026 NPB Triple Crown, is expected to play the outfield for Chicago, he was primarily a third baseman in Japan. The Yankees, in search of help at the hot corner, had been linked to Sato, and this signing takes a potential solution off the board. The Cubs also signed starter Trevor Rogers away from the Orioles Monday night while Baltimore replaced him Tuesday with Freddy Peralta in a flurry of free agent activity.

New York Post | Dylan Svoboda : The Yankees inked 22-year-old Kosei Hiroike to a minor-league deal Tuesday. The right-hander chose to bypass the NPB draft, signing with the Yankees directly out of college. Hiroike, whose fastball has been clocked at 97 mph, registered a 2.66 ERA with 51 strikeouts in 44 innings while pitching for Keio University this season. Should he make it to the bigs, Hirioke would be the first Japanese-born player to don the pinstripes since Masahiro Tanaka in 2020.

Reuters : The Yankees made official two previously announced acquisitions Tuesday, inking one-year deals with Brent Suter and Tim Hill. The two profile similarly as veteran lefty sinkerballers who rely on high groundball rates to offset limited velocity. The two signings, along with closer David Bednar accepting his qualifying offer, help augment a bullpen corps that GM Brian Cashman identified as his top priority this offseason.

New York Post | Joel Sherman : At 35, Tyler Austin is plotting a return to MLB. The former Yankee has received interest from his former squad for a reunion on a minor-league deal. The Mets, Blue Jays, Cubs, and Rays are reportedly also in the mix. Austin spent six successful seasons with the Yokohama DeNA BayStars of the NPB before signing a minor-league deal with the Cubs last year. His comeback to the States was put on hold, though, as knee issues forced him to miss a full season. Now purportedly healthy, Austin would add the potential for right-handed pop with no playing time commitment.
