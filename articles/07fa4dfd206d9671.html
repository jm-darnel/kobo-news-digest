NYPD says man murdered his elderly aunt at their Bronx home

By Catalina Gonella · Gothamist · November 16, 2026

A 35-year-old man was arrested and charged with murder Sunday after police say his aunt was found beaten to death in the Bronx home the two shared.

Police said officers responded to a call about an assault in progress at 754 Manida Street just after midnight Saturday morning.

When police arrived they found 75-year-old Carmen Nunez unconscious and unresponsive, according to the NYPD. EMS rushed her to Lincoln Hospital, where she was pronounced dead, officials said.

Officers took Joshua Nunez into custody at the scene on Saturday, and charged him with murder, manslaughter, and assault on Sunday morning.

Records show they both lived at the Bronx address where the assault was reported, but police did not provide information about what prompted the attack, or if anyone else was present.

Nunez's attorney information was not immediately available.

There have been three murders in the precinct, which includes Hunts Point and Longwood, so far this year; the same rate as 2025, according to NYPD data. Felony assaults in the precinct have declined by roughly 10% so far this year over last.
