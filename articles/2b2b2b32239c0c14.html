MSNBC Is Now MS NOW: Where To Find The Live Stream

Harlem World · November 12, 2026

What changed with the rebrand, who is on the schedule, and how to watch without cable

For nearly three decades, MSNBC was one of the most recognizable names in cable news.

Then, in late 2025, the network quietly became MS NOW. The logo changed, the ownership changed, and so did the way many people tune in. Plenty of viewers still search for the MSNBC live stream out of habit, so it helps to understand what actually happened and where the channel lives today.

How MS NOW Approaches Streaming

One of the clearest changes after the split is how aggressively the network has moved into streaming. In September 2026, MS NOW launched a direct-to-consumer membership that includes a 24/7 live stream of the full channel, a library of on-demand shows, and member-only content, available through its website and mobile app. Live audio streaming is available too.

Where to Watch the Live Stream

Here are the main ways to watch today:

• MS NOW Membership: The standard price is $7.99 per month or $79.99 per year, and it includes the 24/7 live feed plus full episodes on demand.

• Connected TV apps: The live feed is available on Roku, Apple TV, Amazon Fire TV, Samsung, and Google TV, in addition to the website and mobile app.

• Provider sign-in: If you already pay for TV through a participating provider, you can sign in to the MS NOW app and watch live coverage with those credentials.

• Livenewsof.com: For a quick online option, the site has a dedicated page where you can follow the channel from your browser. Viewers who like to compare coverage across networks can explore news live category of the site to keep several channels within reach. This can be handy on big news days when you want to see how different outlets cover the same story.

• Live TV streaming services: Several cable-free bundles carry the channel alongside other news networks, which makes sense if you want one subscription for everything. Check the current channel lineup before signing up, since packages and prices change.

Choosing the Right Option

A Few Practical Tips

• Update your app before major events, since older versions can cause login or playback issues.

• Check your device support, because not every older TV or streaming box runs the latest app.

• Watch for promotions. The network has offered limited-time discounts on annual plans, so read the checkout terms and renewal price carefully.

• Stick to official sources. Unofficial “free” streams are often unreliable and can expose your device to malware.

MSNBC may be gone as a name, but the channel, many of its familiar faces, and its live coverage are very much alive as MS NOW. The rebrand brought new ownership, a reshaped schedule, and, most importantly for cord-cutters, an official way to stream the live channel without cable. As of November 2026, that means a simple membership, wide device support, and more features on the way. Whether you call it MSNBC or MS NOW, finding the live stream has never been easier.
