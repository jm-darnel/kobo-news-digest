Skeletal remains found in backyard of West Village building, NYPD says

Construction workers discovered part of a 19th-century human skeleton behind a building in the West Village on Thursday, according to the NYPD.

Officials said a construction crew excavating the backyard of 79 Christopher St. called 911 around 11 a.m. after finding several bones and part of a skull, buried 4 to 7 feet below the ground.

Police said no foul play was suspected, and that it was not immediately clear if the remains belonged to a woman or a man.
