2 peace officers shot at Co-op City in the Bronx, person barricaded, NYPD says

By Charles Lane · Gothamist · November 16, 2026

Two Co-op City peace officers were shot in the Bronx Monday morning, police said.

Police did not immediately describe the circumstances or anyone's condition.

Co-op City, a 15,000-unit development, is a self-governed, private residential housing cooperative with its own Department of Public Safety.

According to the department, its officers are state-certified peace officers who are authorized to carry firearms and can make arrests.

Co-op City officials did not answer phone calls seeking comment.

This is a developing story and has been updated based on new information from police.
