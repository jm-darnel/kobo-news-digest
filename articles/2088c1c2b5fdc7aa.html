Building next to deadly Brooklyn collapse site must be demolished, officials say

By Alyssa Paolicelli · NY1 · November 19, 2026

For more than a week, the city's Department of Buildings has been working to determine the cause of the fatal collapse in Flatbush, which also injured more than a dozen people.

In a statement, DOB Commissioner Ahmed Tigani said allowing workers inside to install the heavy-duty shoring needed to stabilize the building would put them at risk.

“Given the dangerous condition of the building and the imperative to make sure no one else gets hurt, ordering for the controlled safe demolition of the building is the only available option,” he said in part.

A fenced-off collapse zone has been established outside the building to protect the public ahead of the planned demolition.

Because of the structural stability of the floors and asbestos contamination of items remaining inside, residents will not be allowed back in to retrieve any of their belongings.

“It’s terrible. It’s crazy how it just fell down, and then because of it, that other place, they have to get rid of that," Gus Gretzinger, a young Brooklyn resident on his way to school, said Thursday.

Christina Fontanarosa, another Brooklyn resident, described the damage as surreal.

“It’s almost as if you are looking at a Barbie doll house, and it doesn’t even appear real, but you know that real lives and real people lost everything,” Fontanarosa said.

Vacate orders remain in effect at 1325 and 1327 Flatbush Ave.
