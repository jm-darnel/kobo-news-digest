Goodbye Kitty: Preparation underway for a windy Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in NYC

By Rosemary Misdary · Gothamist · November 24, 2026

It could be Goodbye Kitty for Macy’s Centennial Thanksgiving Day Parade.

“It’s going to be a stormy Thanksgiving Day,” National Weather Service meteorologist Bryan Ramsey said.

While a few inches of precipitation could dampen the parade starting in the morning, rain hasn’t stopped crowds in past years. The serious issue is the wind.

”With the high wind watch in effect, it's not a warning yet, but that means damage is likely and strong winds,” Ramsey said.

While marching bands, dancers, performers and floats can go on rain or shine, the weather conditions could be bad news for Joe Cool and Woodstock, and the many other character inflatables. As a safety precaution, if winds exceed 23 mph or wind gusts are more than 34 mph, giant balloons are grounded or flown lower.
