Tornado watch in effect for 11 NJ counties, flash flood warning in Queens

By Giulia Heyward · Gothamist · November 12, 2026

A tornado watch was in effect for 11 New Jersey counties, and parts of Queens were under a flash flood warning until early Thursday evening.

A tornado warning had also previously been in effect for Southern Queens but expired after 2:30 p.m. However, the area was under a flash flood warning until 5:15 p.m. due to heavy rainfall in the area, according to the agency.

“We issued the warning as a potential heads up in case there are conditions that end up materializing with the heavy rain that is materializing over Queens right now, “ said David Stark, a meteorologist with the National Weather Service headquartered in New York.

Stark said that there wasn't a flash flood warning in effect for other parts of the city.
