Non-stop Christmas music begins on 106.7 Lite FM

By AmNews Staff Reports · Amsterdam News · November 20, 2026

Just in time to get you into the holiday spirit, WLTW 106.7 Lite FM flipped the switch to its annual all-holiday music format on Friday becoming “New York’s Christmas Station.”

To celebrate the official flip to holiday music, 106.7 LITE FM morning show hosts Cubby and Christine, with special guest singer and songwriter Ejae, broadcast live from Friday evening at the Christmas Spectacular Starring the Radio City Rockettes® at Radio City Music Hall.

Lite FM can be heard over the air on 106.7 FM in the New York-metro area, online at litefm.iheart.com or on the iHeartRadio app on Apple and Android devices along with several other platforms.
