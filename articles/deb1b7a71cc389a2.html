NAACP New York State Conference Statement On Disbanding NYPD’s Strategic Response Group

Harlem World · November 16, 2026

In response to Mayor Mamdani and Police Commissioner Tisch’s decision to disband NYPD’s Strategic Response Group, the NAACP New York State Conference issued the following statement:

“The decision to disband the NYPD’s Strategic Response Group is a step in the right direction to ensure New Yorkers First Amendment rights are protected. For years, advocates and community members across our city have made clear that peaceful protests should never be met with aggressive tactics. By disbanding the SRG and separating counterterrorism from protest response, the administration is rightly prioritizing a community-first approach rooted in dialogue and de-escalation.

“While we welcome the creation of the civilian-led Community Dialogue and De-escalation Division and the new restrictions on crowd control tools, the NAACP New York State Conference urges the Mayor and the NYPD to implement these reforms transparently and with real accountability measures.
