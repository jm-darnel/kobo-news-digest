Cybersecurity expert weighs in on recent tech disruptions

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 17, 2026

A series of technological disruptions affecting major companies, airlines, government agencies and banks over the past several days has some New Yorkers feeling anxious and frustrated.

On Tuesday, some Apple customers reported problems using Apple Pay and downloading apps. The National Security Administration also reported issues with its internal communication system.

It comes days after flights were grounded nationwide because of a technical issue. That was followed by a system outage at Untied Airlines. And Monday, multiple banks including Chase and Wells Fargo reported technical outages for customers online and at some branches.

Tech expert Paul Powers, whose company, Physna, develops technology to protect against cyber attacks, joined "The Rush Hour" on Tuesday to discuss the outages.
