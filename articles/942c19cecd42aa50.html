New Yorkers on edge after outages disrupt flights, services nationwide

By Alyssa Paolicelli · NY1 · November 17, 2026

A series of reportedly unrelated technological disruptions affecting major companies, airlines, government agencies and banks over several days has some New Yorkers not only frustrated, but anxious about what's going on.

Microsoft said a combination of a hidden software defect, increased load and reduced server capacity cascaded into a multi-day global outage that began last Thursday.

"It seems to be everything's getting overloaded somehow, so that's the issue that's going on probably," New Yorker Joe Valentine said. "They need to get a handle on this quick before it gets worse.”

Flight-related disruptions continued Monday, when United Airlines paused flights across the country for an hour and a half. The airline said the system that weighs planes before takeoff went down.

“The natural inclination is to think there is some sort of conspiracy, but it could be all coincidence," New Yorker Borris Schlossberg said. "But it is very hard to say. We'll see what happens when they investigate all of them."

Also Monday, some major banks across the country reported problems with account access, slowdowns and transactions going through.

JPMorgan Chase said it was a software glitch affecting Zelle payments and other financial institutions.

All the companies and government agencies involved in the incidents have so far ruled out a cyberattack or anything nefarious.

“I don't think it is a coincidence. I think we should pay attention to it, but I don't think it is a doomsday scenario,” New Yorker Arthur Cohen said.
