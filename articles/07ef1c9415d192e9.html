Tribeca: Legendary Photographer Patrick McMullan New Exhibition So Famous At One Art Space

Harlem World · November 23, 2026

Photographer Patrick McMullan, known for documenting the people, parties and personalities that made New York an international center of fashion, art and nightlife launched his new exhibition, “So Famous,” at One Art Space in Tribeca.

“With SO FAMOUS, we invite guests to step inside Patrick McMullan’s world – flashing cameras, red carpets, and unforgettable faces. His photographs capture more than celebrity; they preserve an extraordinary era in New York’s cultural history. The glamour, the energy, the flash – a moment in time, captured.” – MaryAnn Giella McCulloh

“When I look back at these photographs, I don’t just see famous faces. I remember the parties, the friendships and the incredible energy of New York at a time when art, fashion, music and nightlife all came together. I was fortunate to be in the right places with my camera, capturing people who would go on to become legends, although many were already larger than life. What makes ‘So Famous’ special to me is the opportunity to revisit those moments and share them with a new generation. Before social media, there was a certain magic to being in the room, and I hope these photographs capture some of that excitement.” – Patrick McMullan

SO FAMOUS celebrates Patrick McMullan’s photographic legacy. Curated by MaryAnn Giella McCulloh at One Art Space in Tribeca, the exhibition captures a defining era of New York’s art, fashion, and cultural history through the lens of one of its most celebrated photographers. With its growing reputation for presenting celebrated artists and compelling exhibitions, One Art Space is becoming one of Tribeca’s most recognized galleries. Despite the pouring rain, New York’s art collectors, fashion insiders, and society crowd came out to celebrate the opening of SO FAMOUS. Notable attendees at the launch reception for the exhibition included: Patrick McMullan, MaryAnn Giella McCulloh, Mei Fung, Candace Bushnell, Nicole Miller, Tommy Silverman, Carmen D’Alessio, Mark Benecke, Michael Fredo, Anthony Hayden Guest, Dustin Pittman, Christophe von Hohenberg, Donna D’Cruz, Katie Ford, Patricia Velasquez, Susan Blond, George Whipple, Ric Pipino, Cal Hoffman, Victoria Leacock Hoffman, Bara de Cabrol, Roger de Cabrol, Terry Quinn, Ann Dexter-Jones, Fern Mallis, Frederick Andersen, Yogmata Keiko Aikawa, Keiko Aoki, Consuelo Vanderbilt Costin and Jean Shafiroff.

Patrick McMullan

Patrick McMullan is an internationally recognized photographer, author, publisher and chronicler of celebrity, fashion and New York society. Born in Brooklyn and raised in Huntington, Long Island, he studied at New York University before establishing himself as a prominent nightlife photographer in the 1980s, encouraged by his friendship with Andy Warhol. Over more than four decades, McMullan has photographed leading figures in entertainment, fashion, politics and the arts, capturing defining moments in New York’s cultural and social history.

His photographs have appeared in publications including Vogue, Vanity Fair, Interview, Harper’s Bazaar and The New York Times Magazine. McMullan is also the founder of Patrick McMullan Company, a Manhattan-based photography agency whose extensive archive documents decades of celebrity culture, society events and nightlife. His publishing projects include Glamour Girls, Kiss Kiss and so8os: A Photographic Diary of a Decade, while his photography has been exhibited in galleries and cultural institutions internationally. His work continues to provide a visual record of the personalities and events that have shaped contemporary popular culture. For more information, please visit: www.patrickmcmullan.com

One Art Space

One Art Space opened in May 2011 and for more than 16 years has been a cultural destination in the heart of Tribeca, presenting museum caliber exhibitions by established and emerging artists from New York and internationally. Led by co-owners and women gallerists MaryAnn Giella McCulloh and Mei Fung, the gallery blends art and curatorial skill with business expertise located at 23 Warren Street in New York City. MaryAnn’s background in creativity stems from her father, Joe Giella, a renowned Batman illustrator hired by Stan Lee.

For more than a decade, One Art Space has championed women artists through its annual Women’s History Month exhibitions. MaryAnn Giella McCulloh is also an elected member of the Executive Board of NAWA. 2026 projects include a sold-out curated presentation of Shepard Fairey at the Hamptons Fine Art Fair, as well as exhibitions featuring Hunt Slonem, Don Hershman, Al Diaz (SAMO©), Chuck Connelly and Purvis Young. Our mission is to create a place where the giants of art history and the visionaries of art’s future come together in One Art Space. For more information, please visit: www.oneartspace.com
