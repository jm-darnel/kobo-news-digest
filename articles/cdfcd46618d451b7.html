Battle to reach HBCU Celebration Bowl is down to final weekend

By JAIME C. HARRIS AmNews Sports Editor · Amsterdam News · November 19, 2026

Over the past five seasons, either Jackson State in the Southwestern Athletic Conference (SWAC) or South Carolina State from the Mid-Eastern Athletic Conference (MEAC) or both have appeared in the Celebration Bowl, the Historically Black College and University (HBCU) national championship game. They met up in 2021, with South Carolina winning 31-10, and in 2024, when JSU earned the title 28-7.

Who will represent the SWAC on December 12 at Mercedes Benz Stadium in Atlanta, Ga., in this year’s iteration of the Cricket Celebration Bowl is yet to be determined, but the SC State Bulldogs ticket has already been punched. They clinched their third consecutive trip by defeating North Carolina Central 38-24 on Saturday at home to elevate to 9-2 overall and an undefeated 4-0 in conference play.

Led by head coach Chennis Berry, the Bulldogs are aiming for their second straight 10-win season and consecutive national titles. South Carolina State outlasted Prairie View A&M 40-38 in last year’s Celebration Bowl in a four-overtime classic. Berry came to South Carolina State in 2024 from Benedict, where he won two Division II Black national championships (2022 and 2023).

As for the SWAC, four teams are still mathematically in the hunt: Alabama A&M, Jackson State, Prairie View A&M, and Southern. The Alabama A&M Bulldogs and Prairie View A&M Panthers can decide their own path to the SWAC championship game, scheduled for December 5. A win by the Bulldogs (8-3, 5-2) over Mississippi Valley State (2-5, 3-8) on the road this Saturday will guarantee them a seat at the table out of the SWAC East division.

A Panthers (6-5, 5-2) victory at Alabama State (6-4, 5-3) assures them an invite out of the SWAC West division. Losses by both Alabama A&M and Prairie View A&M would force a tiebreaker to determine who will meet in the conference championship game.
