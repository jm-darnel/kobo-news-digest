Watch: MTA bus video captures moment of deadly Brooklyn building collapse

By Charles Lane · Gothamist · November 19, 2026

A new video released by the MTA provides a rare look at the moment of last week's building collapse in Brooklyn, which killed eight people and injured 18 others.

In the video, the building’s northeast corner tips into the excavation site next door, shearing the front of the building in two. It takes about seven seconds for the building to fall.

Gothamist reported this week the city had shut down at least five projects tied to a pair of engineers in the wake of the collapse and is inspecting more than 160 construction sites tied to companies and people involved in the development project. At least one tenant is suing the landlord of the building, after reporting cracks in the building’s foundation that the owner never addressed.

But more than a week after the collapse, officials have not said exactly what caused the building to fall.

Workers had been excavating the empty lot at 2625 Bedford Ave. for a new apartment building earlier that day, officials said.

On Wednesday, the Department of Buildings said it will demolish 1323 Flatbush Ave., a neighboring building damaged in the collapse, after engineers determined it is too unstable to repair safely.
