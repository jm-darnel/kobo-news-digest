80-year-old man dies in Hamilton Heights fire, FDNY says

By Louis Finley · NY1 · November 9, 2026

An 80-year-old man died after a fire broke out early Monday morning in Hamilton Heights, officials said.

According to the FDNY, there was a heavy clutter inside the building, and a blocked hydrant outside made fighting the fire harder.

Fire officials said the fire started on the third floor around 1:30 a.m. Crews worked for nearly four hours to bring it under control.

Two other residents and two firefighters suffered minor injuries, according to officials.

“You see, the char on the building. The flames were coming all the way out, up the building. It was outrageous,” witness Mason Garrity said.

City Council is seeking answers from the FDNY. The department received a letter Sunday evening requesting its appearance later this month to discuss the increase.

Separately, lithium-ion battery fire investigations have already surpassed last year’s total by 43%.
