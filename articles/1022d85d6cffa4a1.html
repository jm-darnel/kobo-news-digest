Teens from Jack and Jill of America’s Brooklyn Chapter lead Thanksgiving food giveaway

By Contributed Press Release · Amsterdam News · November 25, 2026

On Saturday, Nov. 21, more than 30 teens from the Brooklyn Chapter of Jack and Jill of America turned gratitude into action, helping provide Thanksgiving turkeys and trimmings to 150–200 local families at Cornerstone Baptist Church in Brooklyn.

As New Yorkers grapple with rising costs and food insecurity, the annual Thanksgiving giveaway brought together teens, mothers and community members to help ensure Brooklyn families have food on their tables this holiday season.
