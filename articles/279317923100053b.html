NYC postpones Thanksgiving parade to Saturday as wind gusts threaten disaster

By Rosemary Misdary, Kaya Laterman · Gothamist · November 25, 2026

City officials announced Wednesday that the iconic parade, which is marking its 100th edition this year, will be postponed until Saturday as a powerful storm barrels toward New York City. Both New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani and NYPD Commissioner Jessica Tisch said there were no known specific or credible threats to the parade at this time.

“This will be a heavy storm,” Mamdani said at a Wednesday night press conference. “There is no destination worth risking your safety.”

“Overall, it’s going to be a very windy day tomorrow,” said Joe Pollina, a National Weather Service meteorologist, on Wednesday.

In a statement, Macy’s said the parade is expected to kick off at 8:30 a.m. on Saturday.

Will Coss, the vice president and executive producer of Macy’s Studios, said organizers were “heartbroken that our parade was postponed.”

Coss said the balloons will be deflated on Wednesday night, taken back to a facility in New Jersey and brought back out to be inflated before Saturday's parade. There will not be another traditional public balloon inflation event on Friday, he added.

Coss described getting the balloons ready again as a “weeks-long process that’s going to get condensed to about 24 hours.”

“It’s the only inflation we support here in New York City,” Mamdani said.

Thousands of police officers will be deployed Saturday, with an increased police presence along the parade route, officials said.

Tisch said more than 1,000 officers who were scheduled to be off on Saturday will now have to work.

Mamdani said sustained winds of 30 to 40 mph were expected on Thanksgiving Day, with gusts potentially reaching 65 mph. The NWS’s high wind warning goes into effect at 10 a.m., when the parade would have been in full swing.

Because the mayor is scheduled to have surgery to repair his torn Achilles tendon on Friday, he said he will not attend the rescheduled parade.

Tisch said the parade will start from the corner of West 77th Street and Central Park West. The route will head south on Central Park West, turn left onto Central Park South and then turn right onto Sixth Avenue. The parade will end on 34th Street in front of Macy’s Herald Square.

Street closures will begin Friday and continue into Saturday, Tisch said. They include Central Park West from West 88th to West 59th streets, restrictions on Central Park transverse roads and closures along Sixth Avenue and surrounding streets down to 34th Street.

There will be no crosstown vehicle traffic through the parade route, and streets will reopen in phases throughout the afternoon, she said.
