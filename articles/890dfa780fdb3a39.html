Mamdani disbands controversial NYPD unit responsible for protest response

By Erica Brosnan · NY1 · November 16, 2026

Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced Monday that the NYPD's Strategic Response Group will be disbanded and replaced with a new civilian-led division that will continue to work with the NYPD, with the backing of Police Commissioner Jessica Tisch.

In a prerecorded video released by City Hall, Mamdani said the SRG will no longer be responsible for crowd control at demonstrations. Instead, a group of civilians will work alongside NYPD officers to engage with protesters and de-escalate tensions.

Mamdani made the announcement alongside City Councilman Chi Ossé, who has been among the more vocal critics of the SRG and has called for changes to the unit.

“This will not be a uniformed police unit. It'll be made up of civilians without weapons or arrest powers,” Mamdani said.

According to the mayor's office, the new Community Dialogue and De-escalation Division, or CDDD, will be housed within the Mayor's Office of Community Safety. A separate team of 30 uniformed officers, known as the First Amendment Support Team, or FAST, will be created within the NYPD to engage in dialogue and de-escalation.

“I would never support changes that diminish the NYPD’s ability to protect New Yorkers, or that put our officers in a position where they lack the resources necessary to confront a serious threat,” Police Commissioner Jessica Tisch said in a statement. “These changes will make us a stronger, more effective department. I thank the men and women of the NYPD for their noble service to this city.”

Pepper spray will no longer be used as a general crowd-management tool, and long-range acoustic devices will be limited to 90 decibels, according to the city.
