Yankees news: Black Friday Shopping

By John Griffin · Pinstripe Alley · November 28, 2026

MLB Trade Rumors | Mark Polishuk : Ever since the trade deadline, the Yankees have made it very clear that they have wanted to add to their catching contingent, looking for a right-handed bat to go alongside the lefty-swinging Austin Wells. With San Diego looking to clear a path to playing time for top prospect Ethan Salas, two names that the Yankees have been looking into are Luis Campusano and Freddy Fermin. While both are right-handed hitters, their profiles are remarkably different: Fermin would give the Yankees one of the best defensive tandems in the game, and Campusano would provide some power behind the dish the team has desperately needed in recent years. For what it’s worth, not only have the two teams made some high-profile moves in recent years, the situation very much mimics the 2023-2024 winter: the Padres need rotation help, the Yankees have an excess of starters, and the Padres have exactly what the Yankees need.

ESPN | Jorge Castillo : As Andrew detailed for us yesterday , former Yankees second baseman Jazz Chisholm Jr. agreed to a one-year contract with the Toronto Blue Jays that contains both a player and a team option for 2028 and which guarantees him $34.5 million. The move brings to a close a dramatic Yankees career for the 28-year-old infielder, which saw him provide a spark to a scuffling 2024 squad and become a key contributor at the hot corner as the team went to the World Series for the first time since 2009, fill a critical role in the middle of the order in 2025, before scuffling in his contract year while courting controversy on and off the field.

MLB Trade Rumors | Darragh McDonald : As Friday came to a close, we learned that another multi-year Yankee who hit free agency would not be returning in 2027. Steady long man Ryan Yarbrough is signing with the Cubs, per Robert Murray of FanSided (who also broke the Chisholm news). The Yankees’ decision to sign Brent Suter essentially rendered Yarbrough’s possible future with the team redundant, as Suter has a similar profile and better stuff. Between Suter, Brent Headrick, and Tim Hill, the bullpen was getting fairly left-handed regardless.
