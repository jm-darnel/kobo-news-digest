New York Blood Center Urges More Blood Drives To Prevent Holiday Shortages

Harlem World · November 9, 2026

New York Blood Center (NYBC) is calling on local businesses, schools, places of worship, community groups and other organizations to host blood drives during the upcoming holiday season, particularly between December 21 and January 10.

NYBC needs approximately 5,500 additional blood donations, equivalent to nearly 140 blood drives, to help meet hospital demand during the critical winter months.

Each month, more than 500 blood drives are made possible by organizations that open their doors and provide space for their employees, members, neighbors, and communities to donate. These partnerships are essential to maintaining the blood supply for area hospitals. But during the holidays, when schools close, businesses reduce operations, families travel, and seasonal illnesses increase, opportunities to donate blood decline dramatically.

This year, NYBC is asking the community to help in a different way: not simply by donating blood, but by providing a place for others to donate.

“While the holidays are a time of celebration for many New Yorkers, they can also be one of the most challenging times of year for our blood supply,” said Jeannie Mascolino, Vice President of Operations at New York Blood Center. “We saw firsthand last winter how quickly donations can decline during the holidays. We’re asking organizations to help us get ahead of that challenge by scheduling blood drives now, especially surrounding Christmas and New Year’s. Every blood drive helps ensure patients have access to the lifesaving blood products they need.”

Hosting a blood drive is easy, and NYBC provides support every step of the way. Organizations interested in hosting a blood drive can complete a brief interest form at nybc.org/host-a-blood-drive/ and will be connected with an NYBC account manager to help coordinate and plan their drive.

Blood donors can give every 56 days, and recent updates to FDA guidance mean more people may now be eligible to donate. To make an appointment or view current eligibility guidelines, call 1-800-933-2566 or visit nybc.org.

About New York Blood Center (NYBC)

Founded in 1964, New York Blood Center (NYBC) has served the tri-state area for more than 60 years, delivering approximately 500,000 lifesaving blood products annually to nearly 200 hospitals, EMS and healthcare partners. NYBC is part of New York Blood Center Enterprises (NYBCe), which operates in 16 states and delivers more than one million blood products to nearly 550 U.S. hospitals annually. NYBCe additionally delivers cellular therapies, specialty pharmacy, and medical services to 200+ research, academic and biopharmaceutical organizations. NYBCe’s Lindsley F. Kimball Research Institute is a leader in hematology and transfusion medicine research, dedicated to the study, prevention, treatment and cure of bloodborne and blood-related diseases. NYBC serves as a vital community lifeline dedicated to helping patients and advancing global public health. To learn more, visit nybc.org. Connect with us on Facebook, X, Instagram, and LinkedIn.
