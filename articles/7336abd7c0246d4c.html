Gov. Hochul calls on Duffy to resign after FAA meltdown grounds nation's flights

By Ryan Kost · Gothamist · November 14, 2026

Gov. Kathy Hochul called on U.S. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy to resign or be fired after a communications failure grounded all flights across the country Friday, leading to thousands of flight cancellations and delays that continued well into Saturday.

“Sean Duffy must resign, or President Trump must remove him immediately,” the governor said in a statement Saturday morning.

“From chronic air traffic controller shortages to repeated communications breakdowns, serious lapses in our aviation system have become a pattern on Sean Duffy’s watch.”

Hochul and the MTA have been at odds with President Donald Trump’s transportation department for much of the past two years, largely over congestion pricing. Duffy has repeatedly moved to kill Manhattan’s toll program, but has been outflanked by the state in court.

New Jersey Gov. Mikie Sherrill was still stranded in Denver, Colo. Saturday morning after her Friday night flight was grounded.

At a Friday briefing, Duffy blamed aging equipment. He said the analog system’s switches and routers got overloaded with data. “It’s the old hardware that went down,” he said. Officials haven’t released a final determination of the cause.

Hochul called for a congressional investigation into the causes before the start of the holiday travel rush.
