Examining the growing political influence of NYC-DSA

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 19, 2026

At a recent convention, the New York City Democratic Socialists of America laid out their priorities for local and national activities.

Their priorities include a possible primary challenge to Gov. Kathy Hochul and a push to draft Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez for a presidential run in 2028.

Peter Sterne, the news and opinion editor at City & State New York and closely follows developments within the DSA, joined NY1's Bobby Cuza on “Inside City Hall” Thursday to break this down.
