What stormy weather could mean for Macy’s parade balloons

By Carly Haynes · NY1 · November 23, 2026

This year’s event will include a record 100 inflatable balloons, along with marching bands, dancers, pop stars and casts from nine Broadway musicals.

“We spent four years as a marching band family, and we always loved the Macy’s Day Parade, so we’re excited to see the rehearsal, even if we don’t get to see the real thing,” Kristen Butera said.

“It feels weird that, like, my parents are back in Denver, and I take a picture and say. ‘You’re seeing this on Thursday.’ But I’m seeing this a few blocks from my house,” Melanie Royston said.

Organizers are also preparing for possible severe weather. An East Coast storm system could move in on Thanksgiving Day with strong winds and heavy rain.

City regulations allow balloons to fly only if sustained winds are below 23 mph and gusts are up to 34 mph.

“This parade has gone on since 1924, rain or shine,” Cross said. “We’re prepared for all the elements.”

Organizers said anemometers will be placed along the route to monitor wind speeds. According to Cross, the parade is prepared to continue in the rain.

Additional events are scheduled before Thanksgiving morning, including another rehearsal Tuesday night for remaining artists and “inflation day” Wednesday, when spectators can head to Central Park to watch staff blow up the balloons that’ll be featured during the parade.
