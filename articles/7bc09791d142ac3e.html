Sen. Majority Leader Andrea Stewart-Cousins discusses education and tax increases

By Susan Arbetter · NY1 · November 16, 2026

Last week, SED Commissioner Betty Rosa informed the state the transition would need more money to implement.

In an exclusive interview with Capital Tonight, Senate Majority Leader Andrea Stewart-Cousins told host Susan Arbetter she is concerned about those rising costs.

Stewart-Cousins, who in January will preside over the largest Democratic Senate majority in state history, also discussed the possibility of raising taxes to fund priorities.

This January, a new cap on outside income will kick in for lawmakers; when asked if she was considering a pay raise for legislators, Stewart-Cousins said she had not discussed it with her conference.
