Active pattern continues around Thanksgiving across U.S.

By Spectrum News Weather Staff · NY1 · November 24, 2026

It has been a busy pattern since the start of November with El Nino in full swing. Unfortunately for travelers, this active pattern is set to continue with another impactful East Coast system.

Below we will go over what to expect each day this week across the U.S. to better help make travel plans.

A more detailed forecast for each day can be found below.

If you are traveling on Thanksgiving Day, most of the country thankfully looks quiet and uneventful. The Pacific NW system will be pushing inland bringing snow across the northern Mountain West. Additionally, the lake-effect machine will continue in the Great Lakes.

Black Friday shoppers may need to deal with some winter weather from the Northern Plains to Great Lakes. Temperatures will be much colder behind the early week front setting this one up.

Wet weather is likely across the Deep South and Mississippi River Valley Saturday with up to an inch or two of rainfall possible. Further north, some winter weather and snow is posssible across the Great Lakes and Ohio Valley.

As a low pressure system and cold front move eastward, more rain is possible across the Deep South and Southeast. Rain should stretch up the East Coast with winter weather and snow possible in parts of the Northeast and New England.
