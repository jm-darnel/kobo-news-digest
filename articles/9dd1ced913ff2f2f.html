FAA meltdown strands NJ Gov. Sherrill at Dem conference in Denver

By Ryan Kost, Michael Sol Warren · Gothamist · November 14, 2026

New Jersey Gov. Mikie Sherrill is among the thousands of travelers stranded after an air traffic control breakdown that grounded all flights nationwide for more than five hours Friday.

Sherrill left New Jersey late Thursday and was expected to return Friday evening, a spokesperson said. She’s now expected back sometime Saturday morning.

More than a thousand flights at Newark, JFK and Laguardia were canceled as of Saturday morning and passengers were still facing long delays, according to FlightAware, a national flight tracking site.

Lt. Gov. Shirley Emehelu, who was only just appointed on Monday, has been serving as acting governor while Sherrill is away. Emehelu replaced Dale Caldwell, who resigned in September, after an investigation substantiated a sexual harassment complaint against him and other allegations surfaced that he had made women on his staff uncomfortable.
