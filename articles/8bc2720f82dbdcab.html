New York Times’ Metropolitan Diary celebrates 50 years

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 11, 2026

For 50 years, the New York Times’ Metropolitan Diary has captured the everyday moments that make New York City unique.

To mark the anniversary, the Times is looking back at its extensive archive of entries. One favorite dates to July 4, 1984, when a traffic jam in Corona, Queens, came to a halt as a driver stopped for a cup of lemon ice.

Ed Shanahan, editor of Metropolitan Diary, joined “Mornings On 1” to celebrate 50 years of New Yorkers telling their stories.
