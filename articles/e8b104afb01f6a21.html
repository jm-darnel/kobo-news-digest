The McGuinness Boulevard Redesign Is Finally Complete

By Katie Honan · The City · November 13, 2026

A long-awaited Brooklyn street redesign was finally completed Friday, after years of starts and stops and a supporting role in the bribery case of a former top aide to Mayor Eric Adams.

Mayor Zohran Mamdani and other elected officials celebrated the changes along a one-mile stretch of McGuinness Boulevard by cutting a ceremonial ribbon under the Pulaski Bridge in Greenpoint to cheers.

Mamdani vowed to restart the project three days into his term as mayor, and on Friday he thanked the advocates – many who lost loved ones to crashes – who kept pushing for it.

“Even as the long pattern of wealthy interests trumping those of working people in this neighborhood repeated itself over and over, today that belief is finally vindicated,” he said. “My friends, we have completed the redesign of McGuinness Boulevard.”
