'Close Rikers czar' details plan to close Rikers by 2032

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 20, 2026

Mayor Zohran Mamdani shared a blueprint Friday to shut down the troubled jail complex on Rikers Island and replace it with four smaller borough-based jails by 2032.

Under current law, the city is required to close Rikers by next August, but that deadline will be missed.
