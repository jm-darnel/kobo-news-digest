CUNY professor breaks down how New Yorkers voted across the five boroughs

John Mollenkopf, a professor of political science and sociology at the CUNY Graduate Center, joined “Inside City Hall” to discuss voting trends across the city following Tuesday’s election. Mollenkopf is also director of the CUNY Graduate Center’s Center for Urban Research, which puts together an interactive map every election cycle.
