Man charged with murder in shooting at NJ vigil that left 1 dead, 4 injured

By Giulia Heyward · Gothamist · November 17, 2026

A New Jersey man is facing murder charges in connection with a shooting at a candlelight vigil in Elizabeth last month that left one person dead and four others injured, prosecutors announced Monday.

Travon Holmes, 29, is charged with first-degree murder, conspiracy to commit murder and four counts of attempted murder, according to the Union County Prosecutor's Office.

Five people were shot, including 26-year-old Steve Scott of Edison, who was pronounced dead at the scene. The four other victims were taken to a local hospital for treatment, officials said.

Holmes was arrested Friday, Nov. 13, in Elizabeth and is being held at the Essex County Jail, prosecutors said.
