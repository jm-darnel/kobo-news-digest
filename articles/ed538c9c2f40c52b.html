Ready for Thanksgiving? Harlem restaurants have you covered!

By KYSHA HARRIS Food Editor, @SCHOPnyc · Amsterdam News · November 19, 2026

Thanksgiving is coming, and I am not prepared. Are you?

Truthfully, I’m still thinking about the smoked turkey I enjoyed at a Thanksgiving dinner in July. Yes, July! Back in 2023, I attended a turkey cooking class and dinner featuring birds from the Diestel Family Ranch , a fourth-generation family farm in California. I wrote about the experience in my 2024 column, “Thanksgiving in July,” and I’ve been thinking about that turkey ever since.

Unfortunately, my favorite, their Naturally Pecan Smoked Whole Turkey , is already sold out for the season! However, Diestel offers a wonderful selection of other birds, including organic, pasture-raised, and heritage varieties. The last day to order online for Thanksgiving is November 20, so don’t delay. You can also check for Diestel products at local retailers.

Here’s the thing: While I’m certainly looking forward to Thanksgiving dinner, I’m not necessarily looking forward to all the shopping, chopping, cooking, and — most importantly — cleaning up.

If you’re like me and would rather enjoy the meal than spend days preparing it, Harlem has options. From traditional soul food feasts to elegant prix fixe dinners, live music, and complete holiday meals to take home, neighborhood restaurants are ready to do the heavy lifting.

Here are six Harlem restaurants offering Thanksgiving dining and takeout for Thursday, Nov. 26.

Settepani

196 Malcolm X Boulevard 917-492-4806

Settepani is serving Thanksgiving à la carte, featuring an organic roasted turkey entrée for $42 with a choice of stuffing and seasonal sides, plus other special entrées. Thanksgiving catering is also available for pickup, with meals serving eight to 10 people for $519. Orders must be placed by Monday, Nov. 23, at 4 p.m. Let’s not forget that Settepani began as a bakery on Lenox Avenue 27 years ago! They’re offering 10 varieties of holiday pies, including apple crumb, pumpkin, and cannoli, with three varieties available by special order only.

Red Rooster Harlem and Ginny’s Supper Club

310 Lenox Avenue 212-792-9001

Red Rooster is making Thanksgiving a celebration with two dining experiences and live entertainment throughout the day. In the main dining room, guests can enjoy a Turkey Feast prix fixe for $90 per person or order from the regular à la carte menu. A $15 entertainment cover applies.

Downstairs, Ginny’s Supper Club will host a Thanksgiving buffet for $85 per person, with live music and an additional $20 entertainment cover. Both venues will celebrate from 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., making this a particularly good choice for anyone who wants dinner, drinks, and a little music with their turkey. Reservations are recommended.

Melba’s

300 West 114th Street 212-864-7777

Melba’s brings its signature Harlem hospitality to Thanksgiving with a three-course prix fixe dinner for $69 per person, plus tax and gratuity. The menu features traditional oven-roasted turkey with sausage stuffing, BBQ-braised short ribs, herb-crusted salmon, pineapple-glazed ham, and a vegan entrée.

Guests can select two sides, including mac and cheese or collard greens, and finish with a holiday dessert. Seatings are scheduled for noon, 4 p.m., and 7:30 p.m. Reservations are available through OpenTable .

Sylvia’s Restaurant

328 Malcolm X Boulevard 212-996-0660

At Sylvia’s, Thanksgiving favorites aren’t reserved for the fourth Thursday in November. The Harlem institution serves up soul food classics like collard greens, mac and cheese, candied yams, and cornbread year-round.

This Thanksgiving, enjoy the familiar comforts of Sylvia’s dining room or bring the celebration home with ready-to-eat takeout. After all, there’s something to be said for a holiday meal that tastes like home without having to cook it yourself. Thanksgiving service runs from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.

233 West 125th Street, fifth floor 332-266-8727

Located above the historic Victoria Theater in the Renaissance New York Harlem Hotel, the Victoria is bringing a little elegance to Thanksgiving with an advertised $89 three-course prix fixe dining experience featuring Moët & Chandon Brut Impérial.

It’s an appealing choice for anyone looking to dress up, enjoy some Champagne, and celebrate Thanksgiving with a contemporary twist. The experience is for guests 21 and older. Reservations are available through Resy .

PB Brasserie Steak House

60 West 125th Street 866-740-4464

PB Brasserie brings a French-inspired touch to Thanksgiving with a three-course menu featuring crab croquettes with celery rémoulade, traditional turkey with bread stuffing, sweet potato purée and cranberry jam, and alternatives like truffle mushroom risotto, salmon Béarnaise, and filet mignon.

Finish with pumpkin pie, cheesecake, or chocolate mousse for a holiday meal that offers something for traditionalists and adventurous diners alike. Thanksgiving service runs from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. Visit the restaurant’s website for reservations.

Finally, to my OGs who came here looking for my annual turkey recipe, I owe you one! I went digging through the Amsterdam News archives and discovered that I haven ’t shared my turkey recipe in these pages since 2019. Yes, 2019! We all know what happened after that, so perhaps a little grace is in order.

Seven years later, I’m happy to bring it back. Here’s my “Talking SCHOP! An Early Bird Start” column, complete with my turkey recipe. Use your discretion with any updates, and I trust you’ll make it your own.

Whether you’re dining out, bringing dinner home or rolling up your sleeves to cook the whole meal yourself, here’s to a Thanksgiving filled with good food, good company, and ideally, somebody else doing the dishes.

Thanks for reading and Happy Thanksgiving!
