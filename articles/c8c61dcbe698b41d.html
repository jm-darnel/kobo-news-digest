Mamdani tests new strategy to punish bad NYC landlords in latest lawsuit

By David Brand · Gothamist · November 18, 2026

Mayor Zohran Mamdani says the city has a new tool in its fight against bad landlords: a legal maneuver typically used to punish fraud.

Mamdani said the city’s usual tactics for holding landlords accountable — violations and fines — has not worked in the case of Singer, and other chronically negligent owners.

“When the same old tools fail to deliver results, we will use new strategies to hold noncompliant landlords accountable and win justice for tenants,” the mayor said in a statement. “Every New Yorker deserves a safe, well-maintained home, and we will use every tool at our disposal to make that a reality across the five boroughs.”

“This is a new playbook,” said Department of Housing Preservation and Development Commissioner Dina Levy in an interview. “This has extreme consequences financially and we are going to keep innovating to finally, at long lost, make them compliant or get out of the real estate business.”

She said the tactic aligns with Mayor Zohran Mamdani’s housing plan, which features a section called “Fix The City” with strategies for punishing “persistently negligent landlords.”

But the city’s legal argument in the new case against Singer remains untested.

In a written statement, Corporation Counsel Steven Banks said he was confident the False Claims Act can serve as a “powerful new tool to seek relief for tenants and hold landlords accountable for the public nuisances they have created in our communities.”
