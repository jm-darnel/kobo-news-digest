Scouting the needs of the AL East

By Joshua Diemert · Pinstripe Alley · November 18, 2026

Nothing operates in a vacuum, certainly not a baseball team’s offseason. Every trade idea needs a partner willing to deal, every free agent is taking someone else’s call after yours. We have a pretty good idea of where the Yankees need to improve (GOD get a third baseman), and who they stand to lose, but let’s check in on the four teams they have to compete with for the AL East division title.

Red Sox

Blue Jays

I know we’re probably all tired of the Blue Jays being the also-ran in big free agent pursuits — see Shohei Ohtani and Juan Soto — and I promise you Toronto is too. Still, I think they’re a true contender for Tarik Skubal. On the offense, they need to figure out Vlad Guerrero Jr. and really that’s all there is to it, everything on that half of the game depends on him being a 140 wRC+ hitter and not a 95. You figure that out internally, and then get Skubal to pair with Cease atop that rotation… that’s an intimidating roster.

Orioles

What the Orioles need is a vision, more than any one player or even focus on a position group. The tanking failed, it failed as the team filled Top 100 prospect lists but always left those future wins on the table. It failed when instead of ploughing back into a roster that won 101 games in 2023 the club decided they had arrived and no changes were necessary. It failed when they traded Adley Rutschman, the whole reason they tanked in the first place!

They need to rebuild a pitching staff and maybe the signing of Pete Alonso last winter gives O’s fans hope that the team (and newish ownership) will spend away, but a chequebook without a direction often leads to disaster.

Rays

I have no idea what Tampa Bay will ever do. You’d think that ground breaking on a new ballpark and additional money coming in from such a deep playoff run might make them a little interested in the free agent market, but more to the point I’d expect them to dabble in the trade market and try and keep that AL pennant winning team on top. As always, to MLB GMs, if the Rays call you do not pick up the phone.
