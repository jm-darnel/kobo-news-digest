Assemblyman-elect Eon Huntley lays out his priorities

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 9, 2026

Last week’s elections resulted in historic wins for Democrats in states across the country, including in New York.

State Democrats regained a supermajority in the Senate, and the supermajority widened in the state Assembly, marking the largest majority in both houses of the legislature’s history.

Voters also elected the largest-ever bloc of democratic socialists to the statehouse, and my next guest is part of that 14-member bloc.

Eon Huntley won his race for state Assembly to represent New York’s 56th Assembly District, which includes parts of Bedford-Stuyvesant and Crown Heights in Brooklyn.

He joined “Inside City Hall” to discuss more.
