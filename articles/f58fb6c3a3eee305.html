Meet state Sen.-elect Aber Kawas, the first Palestinian American elected to the N.Y. state legislature

By Susan Arbetter · NY1 · November 11, 2026

Aber Kawas is the newly elected state senator representing Queens District 12, the same seat that former state Senate Deputy Majority Leader Mike Gianaris held from 2011 to 2026.

Gianaris resigned to work for Metropolitan Park in August.

Kawas is a member of the Democratic Socialists of America and ran on a platform of healthcare for all, improved public transportation and affordable housing.

She spoke with Capital Tonight host Susan Arbetter about her background, the issues that inform her politics, and the state’s efforts to push back against ICE.

At one point in the interview, the “New York for All Act” was discussed. While elements of the original bill were folded into a compromise package, the full act did not pass.

Bill sponsor state Sen. Andrew Gounardes plans to reintroduce the legislation in the next session, which starts in January.
