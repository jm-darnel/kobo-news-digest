From Harlem To Hollywood, We Honor Our Veterans

Harlem World · November 11, 2026

By HWM

This Veterans Day, we honor the family members, friends, and neighbors who have served our great nation—and whose example calls us toward its greater potential.

From Harlem to Hollywood, our gratitude begins close to home: with our fathers and mothers, brothers and sisters, extended family, dear friends, neighbors, and colleagues who have answered the call to serve.

They hold a place not only in our nation’s story, but in our own. They are the familiar faces in family photographs, the friends gathered around our tables, and the neighbors who help make a community feel like home. Through them, the meaning of service becomes personal.

Today, we honor their courage, their commitment, and the sacrifices they have made in wartime and in peace. We recognize, too, those who stood beside them—the partners, parents, children, siblings, and friends whose love helped sustain them through separation, uncertainty, and homecoming.

Even if you never fought in a war, you may have faced your own battles at home as family members and friends served far away. You lived with the worry, waited for a call, kept a household going, or supported someone through their return. Those experiences are distinct from military service, but the love, endurance, and sacrifice behind them deserve recognition, too.

We remember those who never returned and hold close the families and friends who carry their memory. We also honor those living with the visible and unseen burdens of service, acknowledging that a homecoming does not always mark the end of sacrifice.

As we celebrate our 250th anniversary, their service asks something of us in return: to help our great nation realize its greater potential. That means nurturing communities like Harlem where dignity, opportunity, and belonging extend to everyone—and where veterans and their families receive care and support long after their service ends.

To all who have served, and to everyone who has stood beside them: thank you.

From Harlem to Hollywood, may our gratitude be more than words—and our commitment to our veterans last well beyond today.
