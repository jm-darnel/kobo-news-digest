73-year-old woman pushed onto tracks at Queens station, officials say

By Noorulain Khawaja · NY1 · November 20, 2026

A 73-year-old woman was shoved onto subway tracks in Queens Friday morning, officials said.

According to police, the woman was pushed while she was standing on the platform, waiting for a southbound N train around 10 a.m. at the Astoria Boulevard Station.

Police said the attack was unprovoked.

“Very surprised because I do take the train here every day to Manhattan,” Julia Maneliak, a subway rider, said.

“It’s bad, bad, bad, very bad,” Abba Guernah, another subway rider, said.

Authorities said that Good Samaritans jumped into action-to help get the woman up off the tracks before the next train pulled in.

“You know, it’s funny because everybody says New Yorkers don’t care about anything, but when something happens, New Yorkers pull together,” Brandon Arroyo of Astoria, who regularly takes the subway, said.

“Whoever helped her, you know, I wish him well, and he’s the best one what he did. I wish her [the 73-year-old woman] well. And to those guys too-because they risk their life, too,” Guernah said.

“New Yorkers are the best,” said Mikko James, another subway rider.

Authorities have not yet identified a suspect.

Some New Yorkers that NY1 spoke with said that Friday’s incident is a reminder to them to be more vigilant.

“Anytime when I’m here, I’m like I need to be in the middle because I’m really afraid. It’s terrifying,” James said.

“I never had a problem. I find it very safe, but these things happen. It happened to my neighbor at the beginning of the pandemic in 2020. He got pushed into the subway tracks, these things happen. He did survive. He did survive,” Arroyo said.

No arrests have been made, and the investigation is ongoing.
