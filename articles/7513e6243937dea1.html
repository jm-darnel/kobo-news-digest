Citymeals on Wheels CEO talks about higher food prices and more

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 19, 2026

New Yorkers are facing higher prices at the grocery store amid rising fuel costs, inflation, tariffs and more extreme weather effecting harvests.

Beth Shapiro, the CEO of Citymeals on Wheels, joined NY1's Bobby Cuza on “Inside City Hall” Thursday to discuss more.

Citymeals on Wheels is a nonprofit organization that delivers food year-round to older New Yorkers in need.
