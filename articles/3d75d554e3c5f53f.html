French Heritage Society Le Bal Imperial In New York

Harlem World · November 16, 2026

On Wednesday, November 11th, 2026, French Heritage Society (FHS) hosted their annual Gala Dinner Dance – Le Bal Impérial – at a private club in Manhattan.

Brett Sherlock, International Consultant at Christie’s, led a record-breaking live auction supported by Boucheron and Le Bristol, as well as a successful paddle raise for the FHS Education Program. The latter was supported by Bertille Hantraye, a current FHS intern at the Museum at FIT, who shared her internship experience. The festive evening concluded with dancing to the fabulous Bob Hardwick Sound, and a festive after party that kept the venue buzzing past midnight with tunes spun by DJ Olivier Meiji.

About French Heritage Society

Open to all lovers of France, French Heritage Society aims to ensure that the treasures of French architectural and cultural heritage survive to delight, educate and inspire future generations. Over the past 44 years, FHS has awarded over $17M to more than 700 restoration and cultural grants to properties throughout France and in the United States. FHS has also selected and supported nearly 600 university students who have crossed the Atlantic for internships at esteemed institutions. FHS has 12 chapters, 11 in the U.S. and one in Paris, and membership includes access to all.
