Yankees news: Bombers bring back Tim Hill as they prepare to bolster bullpen

By Matt Ferenchick · Pinstripe Alley · November 16, 2026

MLB.com | Bryan Hoch : The Yankees have reportedly made one offseason move now, although it’s not exactly the flashiest. Reports have it that Tim Hill has agreed to a one-year deal to return to the Yankees. Hill fell off a bit in 2026 from his previous years in the Bronx, but the reported $2.25 million price tag is hardly something to quibble with (and he did have interest from the Dodgers and Mets). For some more, Michael had the breaking news post for us on the site yesterday .

MLB.com | Manny Randhawa : After opting not to tender a qualifying offer to Jazz Chisholm Jr., the Yankees have something of an opening at second base. While the team theoretically could just put Anthony Volpe there or something, one free agent to consider could be Brandon Lowe. With the Pirates last year, Lowe put up an .824 OPS, having the best season of his career according to fWAR. This isn’t a report definitively linking them to Lowe, but he could be one the team should consider.
