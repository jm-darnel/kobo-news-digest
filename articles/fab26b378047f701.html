Black Friday shoppers search for deals at Queens Center Mall

By Jillian Jorgensen · NY1 · November 27, 2026

What were Black Friday shoppers looking for at Queens Center Mall this year?

“For the sales, for the deals,” Rosa Gonzalez said.

Shoppers flocked to the Queens mall and many others across the city and the country for what’s widely considered the busiest shopping day of the year.

Gonzalez told NY1 that she was looking for a new winter coat.

“I just like shopping in general. I love shopping, especially when there’s a good deal. Yeah, I’m on that,” she said.

But the rise of online shopping and concerns about inflation and the economy, the retail landscape is changing. Americans were expected to spend $182 million this Black Friday, down from $187 million a year ago.

“My friend and I were just saying that it’s so empty right now. Like, usually it’s, like, packed,” Gonzalez said.

Jason Teixeira said he paid a full price for the shoes he came to the mall to buy, and also noticed a sizable drop in shoppers.

“This is the most dead that I’ve ever seen the mall,” he said. “I worked here for, like, five years. And you weren’t even able to get a parking spot at 6 a.m. And now it’s dead. Just a little crazy.”

Did he think it was a result of online shopping?

“That and the economy,” he said. “I think everybody, everybody’s a little broke right now. A lot of people don’t have money right now.”

Still, some shoppers were able to find good deals and get their Christmas presents purchased early.

“All presents, things like this. And I have a lot from Macy’s and Hollister,” one shopper said.

She said that she shops on Black Friday every year and agrees that the crowds have gotten smaller, and that this year, some things were expensive. But she still likes to come out for the deals she can find.

“They have a little discount, and then they give some giveaways,” she said.

If people don’t get a chance to shop on Black Friday, Cyber Monday has become a tradition for online shopping deals.
