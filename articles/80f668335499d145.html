Bronx market moves 30 million pounds of produce ahead of Thanksgiving

By Alyssa Paolicelli · NY1 · November 24, 2026

Workers at the Hunts Point Produce Market in the Bronx are putting in holiday hours this week to move produce to city grocery stores ahead of Thanksgiving.

Family-owned businesses and their employees at the market have been filling pallets and loading trucks to help stock shelves with Thanksgiving staples.

Some of the market’s 10,000 employees began working at 3 p.m. Monday, and were still on the clock late Tuesday morning.

Dan Barabino, vice president of operations at Top Banana, said the market plays a major role in the region’s food supply. Top Banana has been a produce distributor at the market since 1996.

“About 65% of all the produce in the tristate area comes through this market, and it's super important to us to make sure we have high-quality, fresh and excellent produce on your table every day of the year, but specifically Thanksgiving,” Barabino said.

“We move about the same amount of product over the course of the week. However, when we usually have five days to move that volume, we do it in three,” Barabino said. “So it's a very compressed week. I would say today is about 30% more."

"So each of the three days are about 30% more volume, which is obviously unique business conditions, but something we're used to," Barabino added.

According to the American Farm Bureau, a classic Thanksgiving dinner for 10 people costs an average of $58.63 this year, up 6% from last year. That is a national average; in the city, the average can be up to $30 more.

E. Armata Inc. has operated at Hunts Point for more than a century, and has seen produce prices fluctuate over the years.

“There's a lot of crops that the volume is down on, but the volume being down, the price just has to more or less go up,” said Michael Armata, a fourth-generation buyer at E. Armata Inc.

“That and the price of fuel being so high. The costs are very, very expensive for a lot of items for Thanksgiving, unfortunately," Armata added.
