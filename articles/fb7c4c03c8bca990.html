NY to pay $1.2M to a woman who accused ex-Manhattan corrections officer of sexual assault

By Giulia Heyward · Gothamist · November 20, 2026

New York will pay $1.2 million to a woman who sued the state after she accused a former corrections officer of repeatedly sexually assaulting her at a women’s prison in Manhattan, according to lawyers.

Dawkins has also been named in more than 15 other ASA suits, with prison records revealing that the state corrections department had received several complaints from other incarcerated women about the former corrections officer, according to Slater Slater Schulman LLP, the law firm that represented the woman in this case.

“Three years after New York’s Adult Survivors Act lookback window closed, survivors are only now having their day in court after years of unnecessary delays that only serve to retraumatize our clients,” said Adam Slater, a founding and managing partner at the firm. “This outcome shows the State is prepared to accept responsibility and be held accountable for the harm it caused generations of women.”

“New York passed the Adult Survivors Act to help survivors of sexual abuse and assault seek justice,” New York Attorney General Letitia James said in a statement. “In every matter, the Office of the Attorney General is committed to following the facts and the law where they lead. In this case, the evidence led to conceding the state’s liability and agreeing on compensation.”

Attorney information for Dawkins was not made immediately available.
