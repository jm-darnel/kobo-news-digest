Fifth Avenue Welcomes the Holiday Season with Signature Traditions, Seasonal Experiences and Special Events

Harlem World · November 17, 2026

From signature holiday window displays to the annual Open Streets celebration organized in partnership with the New York City Department of Transportation, Fifth Avenue will once again welcome New Yorkers and visitors from around the world to experience its beloved holiday traditions in the heart of Manhattan.

“The holiday season in New York centers on Fifth Avenue,” said Edward F. Pincar Jr., President of the Fifth Avenue Association. “This year we will celebrate Bergdorf Goodman’s 125th Anniversary with iconic window displays, take in the splendor of the Saks Fifth Avenue light show, marvel at the Fifth Avenue Snowflake rising above 57th Street, and once again welcome New Yorkers and visitors from around the world to experience the traditions that make Fifth Avenue so special.”

On Wednesday, November 18th at 5 p.m., the 3,300-pound crystal snowflake will be lit above one of Manhattan’s busiest intersections at Fifth Avenue and 57th Street. The illumination marks the beginning of the holiday season on Fifth Avenue, and this year is being sponsored by Tiffany & Co. and Bergdorf Goodman. In a partnership between the Fifth Avenue Snowflake for Humanity Foundation, the Fifth Avenue Association, and St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital, the snowflake sparkles in honor of the children and families fighting pediatric cancer. The 30-foot spectacle features more than 16,500 individual Baccarat crystal prisms and weighs about as much as a small elephant. Suspended above the Avenue, it has become one of Midtown’s most recognizable holiday decorations.

Holiday Decor

Fifth Avenue’s beloved seasonal decor will return to the Avenue in partnership with NEST NEW YORK for a fourth year starting November 20th, with trees and gift boxes designed by French designer Pierre Marie lining the sidewalks from 47th street to 59th street, and magnificent, larger-than-life pass-through arches at 59th Street. The displays will feature QR codes for visitors to scan to shop the best-selling NEST NEW YORK Holiday collection directly from their phones.

Additional décor on Fifth will include “You’ve Arrived on Fifth” Polaroid-style installations, offering visitors a distinctive setting to capture holiday moments against the backdrop of Fifth Avenue’s celebrated architecture and holiday lights.

Fifth Avenue Holiday Storefronts

Fifth Avenue’s renowned retailers will once again mark the holiday season with elaborate window displays and façade installations. Saks Fifth Avenue will unveil its annual holiday light show on November 23rd, while Cartier’s celebrated façade will illuminate the Avenue at 52nd Street. Bergdorf Goodman will also commemorate its 125th anniversary with a special edition of its renowned holiday windows. Tiffany’s Bird on a Rock installation is returning for a third year, along with special holiday installations inside their flagship store Landmark.

Christmas on Fifth Holiday Tours

Historical tours will return on Thursday, December 10th and Saturday, December 12th. Hosted by historian Kevin Draper in collaboration with New York Historical Tours, visitors will learn about the history of the holidays on Fifth Avenue and make stops at Tiffany & Co. and the Cartier Mansion. To reserve a space on a tour visit New York Historical Tours.

Fifth Avenue Holiday Open Streets

In partnership with the New York City Department of Transportation, Open Streets will return on Sunday, December 13th from 12 p.m. to 6 p.m. and run from 48th Street to 57th Street. The street will close to vehicular traffic to create a pedestrian-friendly space for live music performances, special food and beverage, exclusive products and shopping experiences that include one-of-a-kind pop-ups from famed Fifth Avenue brands such as Nike, Swarovski, Canada Goose, Aritzia, and Lacoste.

Visitors can enjoy curated Fifth Avenue specialties from beloved eateries like a bagel with caviar called “The A-Lister” from Rollin Bagels; hot cocoa made from a rich, slow-melt blend of Belgian chocolate and whole milk by Raclette Street; and a fiery Fifth Avenue butter masala pasta from Gnocchi on 9th. Crowd favorite Ring for Spritz’s interactive display will return for a second year thanks to a partnership with Figlia. Visitors will be able to ring a bell mounted on a decorative wall and then be handed over a non-alcoholic spritz through a curtained window.

More information and a full schedule of programming can be found at fifthavenue.nyc and on the Fifth Avenue Association’s official Instagram page @fifthavenue.

Annual Holiday Map

Featuring original art by Victoria Tentler-Krylov, an illustrator widely recognized for her recurring cover illustrations for The New Yorker, the new holiday memento will allow visitors to easily plan out their shopping and holiday experiences along the Avenue. The new map will be available to visitors starting Saturday, November 21st in participating hotels and businesses on Fifth Avenue.

A limited quantity of free wrapping paper featuring original illustrations by Tentler-Krylov will be available to visitors who purchase gifts on Fifth Avenue during Open Streets on Sunday, December 13th.
