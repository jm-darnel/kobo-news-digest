Community board votes to replace iconic Tompkins Square Park pull-up bars

By Willow Higgins · Gothamist · November 25, 2026

“We think that those bars, the way they've been built…somehow we struck gold with this equipment of how versatile, how sturdy, how open-ended they are,” said Salo Coslovsky, an associate professor of urban planning at NYU who is at the forefront of the preservation fight and fears the new equipment won’t be the same.

Construction will follow a phased schedule that, according to the community board’s resolution, will keep the outdoor fitness area open while the pool and basketball court are under construction, which is expected to take 15 months.

“We at Parks have a long-standing tradition of soliciting and honoring community input and remain committed to creating an inclusive, fully accessible exercise area at Tompkins Square Park while minimizing disruptions to the fitness community,” Parks Commissioner Tricia Shimamura said in a statement. “We look forward to moving this project forward and working with our partners to enhance this beloved public space.”

Members of Manhattan Community Board 3 did not respond to requests for comment in time for the publication of this article.

That includes inviting Mayor Zohran Mamdani, who recently tore his Achilles tendon, to come out to the park for physical therapy.

Accessibility concerns were of top concern for the community board and the parks department when advancing the proposal.

But Coslovsky said that he and other people fighting to preserve the bars agree that the area needs to be made more accessible.

“ That's not only a legal mandate, but a moral imperative. So we are on the same page,” Coslovsky said.

Still, accessibility concerns are being used as an excuse to demolish the existing equipment, he said.

“ There’s a procedural kind of sequence that Parks has to go through,” Coslovsky said. “Wherever they go, we’re going to be there.”
