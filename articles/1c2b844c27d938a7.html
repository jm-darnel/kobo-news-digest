Today on Pinstripe Alley – 11/9/26

By Madison Pavich · Pinstripe Alley · November 9, 2026

Another weekend in the books, and hopefully another weekend closer to baseball being played again. We’re fully into November now, and there’s just over a week until we’ll know who is going to accept or decline their qualifying offers and nine days until the non-tender deadline arrives. That’ll give us 10 days with the full scope of the free agent field before the owners meet on Dec. 1st and make the lockout that we’re all expecting official, so perhaps we’ll get some intriguing deals go down in that middle period. If something does break, you know we’ll be on call to cover it as soon as possible.

It’ll be a chill day on the site as we ease into the work week, with two things on the schedule: Peter gives our latest report card to Jasson Dominguez after a frustrating season that saw him lose out on playing time due to injury and underperformance and then John remembers when the Bird was the word for Greg Bird’s 34th birthday.
