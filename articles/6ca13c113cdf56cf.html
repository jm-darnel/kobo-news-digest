NYPD K-9 trained to sniff out electronic devices

By Emma Barnett · NY1 · November 11, 2026

Twinkie, a 3-year-old black lab retriever mix, is an electronic storage detection canine. Her handler, NYPD Financial Crimes Task Force Detective James Lilla, says she is trained to detect a chemical smell found on electronic devices.

“The skill is finding these devices, whether they be thumb drives, SD cards, cellphones, anything that transmits or stores data,” Lilla said. “Twinkie can find it.”

Twinkie was trained to find electronic devices, credit cards and other items that store or transmit data.

Lilla has worked with Twinkie, who was a gift from the United States Secret Service to the NYPD, since August 2025. He says he traveled to Hoover, Alabama, for about two weeks of training after Twinkie had already been trained by the Secret Service.

Twinkie’s training included practice searches in different settings, including hotels, museums, hockey rinks, water and cars.

Unlike most dogs, Twinkie does not eat from a food bowl. She has to work for her food as it is the reward for finding these devices.

“When I put the food bag on, that’s where she’s identifying, ‘Oh, we’re working now,’” Lilla explained. “The only way she eats is if we work. So we’re training every day, or if we’re in a deployment somewhere, doing a search warrant, it’s the only time that she’ll eat. When she retires, she can eat from the bowl any day she wants. But until then, she has to feed from the hand at the device to keep the intrigue for that chemical that we’re looking for every day.”

Twinkie was instrumental in a case that led the NYPD to Los Angeles, Lilla said.

“During that search warrant, the agents and the detectives did their search. They found their devices, what they needed to find. And then we ran through Twinkie through the room. Twinkie ended up finding an external hard drive stuck in the corner of the closet that the agents and detectives missed,” Lilla told NY1. “We would have walked out of that apartment missing that external hard drive in the closet if we didn’t have Twinkie here.”

Lilla says while he cannot share what was on the hard drive while the case is still ongoing, Twinkie’s sharp nose is a game changer.
