Suspect in Central Park rape cases arrested while working in Miami

By Olivia Leach · NY1 · November 10, 2026

A Bronx man, who was accused in three Central Park rapes, was arrested Tuesday while working at a Burger King in Miami, according to investigators.

NYPD detectives assigned to the U.S. Marshals New York/New Jersey Regional Fugitive Task Force took 30-year-old Dereck Rivera-Deleon into custody. Miami Beach Police said investigators tracked him down after victims identified him and DNA evidence connected him to three rapes in Central Park.

Investigators said the attacks happened in 2021, 2023 and on Oct. 24. In the most recent case, Rivera-Deleon allegedly raped a 39-year-old woman at knifepoint in the park near East 67th Street and Fifth Avenue, according to officials.

Investigators said Rivera-Deleon will be charged in all three attacks.

“Central Park for me is my backyard,” Miriam Halpern said. “I live very close, and I come every day with my dog.”

Halpern said she was surprised the suspect was found in Florida.

“Wow, surprise, especially because it was in Florida, how that guy left so fast,” she said.

Felicia Desanctis said she was glad an arrest had been made, but the case remains concerning.

“I’m glad they caught the guy, and yes, it’s very concerning because I come to the park pretty late actually,” Desanctis said.

In a statement, Ricky J. Patel, U.S. marshal for the Southern District of New York, said authorities will continue pursuing violent offenders who flee.

“When violent offenders flee to evade justice, we will ensure there is no corner of the country where they can hide,” Patel said.

Some New Yorkers said they want to see more police officers patrolling Central Park on foot.

“I wanna see a couple cops. I don’t see that many,” Brian Keith said. “I walk my dog every day. I don’t want to have to deal with lunatics on my own. Let the cops deal with it.”

U.S. marshals have not indicated when Rivera-Deleon will be extradited back to New York to face charges.
