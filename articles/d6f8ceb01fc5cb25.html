Letter to the Editor: Harlem women voice opposition to Rep.- Elect Chevalier’s vote against Hakeem Jeffries as House Speaker

By AmNews Readers · Amsterdam News · November 23, 2026

Dear Editor, As Black women of Harlem, we are writing to express our strong objection to Representative-elect Darializa Avila Chevalier’s vote against Rep. Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker of the House of Representatives.

Harlem needs tangible results from our representatives for working families, seniors, and young people in New York’s 13th Congressional District. Delivering on those priorities—whether securing federal funding for public housing, protecting healthcare, or investing in local infrastructure will require working in collaboration with house leadership.

At a moment when Democrats are poised to retake the House majority and elect Jeffries as the first Black Speaker in U.S. history, we expect our congressional representative to stand firmly on the side of unity and progress. True leadership means working within our coalition to advance our community’s agenda, not fracturing New York’s delegation before the new Congress even begins.

With the official House floor vote scheduled for January 3, 2027, there is still time to course-correct. We call upon Chevalier to honor the voices of the Black women who anchor this district and support Jeffries for Speaker.
