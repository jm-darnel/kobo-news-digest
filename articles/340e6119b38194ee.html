Abreu To Introduce E-bike Bill Requested By Mayor To Hold Apps Accountable

Harlem World · November 19, 2026

City Hall Majority Leader Shaun Abreu will introduce a bill requested by the Mamdani administration at this afternoon’s stated meeting to hold delivery apps and companies accountable for the e-bikes that their workers use.

New York City has seen a sharp increase in injuries and fires resulting from e-device battery fires. By requiring continuous compliance and monitoring, the bill addresses concerns of battery degradation that can ultimately result in fires. The City has made holding delivery apps accountable a priority. An Abreu bill mandating tipping transparency went into effect earlier this year, and six months later it had already secured a $104 million increase in earnings for workers. Mayor Mamdani also announced a $131.5 million labor settlement with DoorDash, the largest of its kind in NYC history. Abreu has recently introduced several bills to improve e-bike safety for both riders and pedestrians. These include a trade-in program for a transition to safer devices, bike share accident data reporting, and the use of dynamometers to determine on the street if a device is legal. These are part of a larger legislative package that the Council is currently considering.

Delivery apps have a role to play in keeping New York’s streets and sidewalks safe for everyone, and this bill makes that responsibility clear.

“…getting the dangerous, illegal devices off our streets is critical.”

City Council Majority Leader Shaun Abreu said, “It’s important that the companies that profit from deliveries take responsibility for the safety of the devices their workers ride. My bill will protect deliveristas and pedestrians, while also reducing fire hazards. Protecting everyone on our streets will require a comprehensive approach, and getting the dangerous, illegal devices off our streets is critical. We expect companies to uphold a basic standard of safety and legal compliance, and I look forward to working with the Mayor and my Council colleagues to pass this bill.”

“Delivery cyclists deserve to work with safe and street-legal devices as they serve our city every day and night and as New York’s delivery landscape continues to evolve, the need for transparency from delivery service companies is long overdue,” said NYC DOT Commissioner Mike Flynn on today’s introduction. “Ensuring that delivery trips are completed on safe and compliant devices improves safety for all New Yorkers. We’re proud to champion this legislation and we’re thankful to Chair Abreu for his collaboration.”
