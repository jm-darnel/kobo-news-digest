Today on Pinstripe Alley – 11/10/26

By Jake Devin · Pinstripe Alley · November 10, 2026

As we awake today, we now three weeks remaining with a Major League Collective Bargaining Agreement in place, 21 days before we are faced with the inevitable owner-imposed lockout. As we inch toward the winter, we do have a few things to keep us occupied this week, as Cy Young winners will be announced tomorrow night, which will presumably act as a coronation for Cam Schlittler, before we get to the MVP winners on Wednesday night. It is odd not to have a Yankee in the running for AL MVP these days.

On the site today, Josh discusses the possibility of David Bednar accepting the qualifying offer, and how it would fit with the Yankees’ general approach to roster construction, while a longer-term deal for Bednar could conflict with the team’s plans. Matt will also grade Camilo Doval’s 2026 season (it wasn’t great!), and Kevin writes up Jack Clark, a long lost member of the late-80’s Yankees.
