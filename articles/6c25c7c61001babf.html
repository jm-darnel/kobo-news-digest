Iggy, Bronx and Brooklyn hope for a home for the holidays

By Tara Alexander · NY1 · November 13, 2026

"Finding Home" is NY1's weekly pet adoption segment. It's a partnership with the ASPCA and the Animal Care Centers of NYC to find loving homes for animals that are up for adoption throughout the five boroughs.

This week NY1 would like to introduce Iggy: A 7-year-old curious pup that likes taking in the world around him. When it is time for fun, Iggy enjoys playtime and, of course, treats.

Also in the spotlight this week: A brother and sister duo named Bronx and Brooklyn. They are a bonded pair of 8-years-old cats. Both are incredibly affectionate.

If you'd like to learn more about Iggy, Bronx or Brooklyn, you can check out the ASPCA website.

Adoption fees are being waived for animals six years old and up. Adopters will also receive some pet supplies.
