Union Settlement Hosts 2026 Annual Turkey Drive To Provide Thanksgiving Meals For 400 East Harlem Families

Harlem World · November 16, 2026

Union Settlement, East Harlem’s oldest and largest social service provider, will hold its Annual Turkey Drive on Thursday, November 19, 2026, from 3:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. at 237 East 104th Street.

In East Harlem, Thanksgiving means a crowded table, plenty of laughter and a full plate for everyone. Many local households are dealing with higher costs this year, and the Turkey Drive helps neighbors who need a little extra support celebrate with the people they love.

Never miss new stories in Harlem

“Thanksgiving is about gathering, gratitude and taking care of one another, and that spirit runs deep in East Harlem,” said Dr. Derrick Boone, Psy.D., Chief Executive Officer of Union Settlement. “Our Turkey Drive is one way we make sure no family in our community goes without a holiday meal. We are grateful to every neighbor, partner and supporter who helps us bring warmth to 400 tables this season.”

About Union Settlement

Union Settlement was founded in 1895 and is East Harlem’s oldest and largest social service provider. Each year it serves about 15,000 residents through education, wellness and community- building programs that help neighbors improve their lives and reach their goals. Learn more at unionsettlement.org.
