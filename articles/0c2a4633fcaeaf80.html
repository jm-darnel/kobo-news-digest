Frank DiLella's theater picks: 'Much Ado About Nothing,' 'Here There Are Blueberries' and more

By Adolfo Mena Cejas, Frank DiLella · NY1 · November 27, 2026

With the many entertainment choices New York City has to offer, it can be difficult to make a choice. Look no further. “On Stage” host Frank DiLella has curated a list of “must-see” shows this week, directing you to the best of Broadway, off-Broadway and beyond.

“Much Ado About Nothing”

At the Winter Garden Theatre, Tom Hiddleston, Hayley Atwell and Mason Alexander Park star in Jamie Lloyd’s revival of “Much Ado About Nothing.” Park recently stopped by the “On Stage” studio to discuss the production.

“Here There Are Blueberries”

At the Ethel Barrymore Theatre, “Here There Are Blueberries” is inspired by a discovery at the United States Holocaust Memorial Museum. The play began with a photo album of Nazi-era photographs sent to archivist Dr. Rebecca Erbelding, who is played by Delia Cunningham. “Here There Are Blueberries” was conceived, co-written and directed by Moises Kaufman of Tectonic Theatre Project.

“Seven Guitars”

At Lincoln Center Theatre, “Seven Guitars” returns in a revival directed by Ruben Santiago Hudson. The August Wilson play is set in the 1940s and follows Floyd “Schoolboy” Barton, a blues musician trying to make a name for himself and win back the woman he loves.
