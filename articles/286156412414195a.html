Yankees come up empty at Silver Slugger Awards

By Matt Ferenchick · Pinstripe Alley · November 18, 2026

MLB awards season concluded on Tuesday night with the announcement of the 2026 Silver Slugger Award winners. Louisville Slugger had previously announced the finalists , with the Yankees landed two in this year’s nominees: Ben Rice at designated hitter and Cody Bellinger in the outfield. Unfortunately, neither came away with the victory on Tuesday.

Ben Rice ended up finishing behind Yordan Alvarez for the AL’s DH honor. That shouldn’t come as a massive surprise considering that the Astros’ slugger also took home the AL MVP award. Rice might’ve had a shot at the first base award had he been put in that race, but alas that’s not how it worked out. Meanwhile in the outfield, Bellinger finished behind Randy Arozarena, Chase DeLauter, and Riley Greene.

This is the first year since 2023 where the Yankees failed to have at least one Silver Slugger winner. Not so coincidentally, that’s also the last time Aaron Judge had missed significant time with injury until this year. Several other Yankee hopes also had so-so seasons or worse, leading to the Bombers taking the goose egg for 2026.

Here is the full list of winners for this season:
