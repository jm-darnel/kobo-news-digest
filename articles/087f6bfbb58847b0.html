What Chuck D thinks of Mamdani’s rise

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 12, 2026

Chuck D is the leader and co-founder of the legendary rap group Public Enemy. He is also a social activist, multimedia producer, visual artist, digital music pioneer, and the author and illustrator of a new book, “Day One: 2024 Was 1984 FAFO.”

Chuck D joined NY1's Errol Louis to discuss the inspiration behind his book, his artistic style, and his remarkable career in hip-hop. He also shared the story behind Public Enemy’s iconic logo and explained why embracing imperfection and working with analog tools can help counter our dependence on screens.
