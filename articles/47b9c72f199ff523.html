28-year-old man fatally stabbed on Brooklyn street after intervening in fight, NYPD says

By Brittany Kriegstein · Gothamist · November 17, 2026

Police said they are looking for a person who fatally stabbed a Brooklyn man after he intervened in an argument between his attacker and another man with a dog.

NYPD officials said 28-year-old Isaiah Davis, 28, was stabbed around 12:30 a.m. on Tuesday on Wyona Street near Fulton Street in Cypress Hills, Brooklyn. They said Davis' attacker had been fighting with another man and a dog — possibly trying to rob him — when Davis got in the middle of it.

Police said the attacker left, came back and stabbed Davis in the torso. EMS responded and took Davis to Brookdale Hospital Medical Center, where doctors pronounced him dead, police said.

Officials said the attacker and Davis did not appear to know each other. They said Davis lived in Crown Heights, about 3 miles away from the incident. It was not immediately clear what happened to the other man and the dog after the stabbing, or what the argument was about.
