NYPD searches for 6 sewer spelunkers seen exiting a Manhattan manhole

By Brittany Kriegstein · Gothamist · November 25, 2026

Police have released pictures of six people wanted for climbing out of a Manhattan manhole last week — the latest in a series of similar incidents that have captivated public attention and raised questions for investigators.

NYPD officials said the latest incident happened just before 5 a.m. on Nov. 18, when a witness saw five people exiting the city’s sewer system through a manhole on 2nd Avenue and East 17th Street in Gramercy Park.

On Tuesday, police released photos of the two women and four men they are looking for. Most appear to be carrying various tools, including sledgehammers.

NYPD officials have said some of the sewer spelunkers appear to be motivated by treasure hunting. But they warn that the city’s manholes can be dangerous and unpredictable places: a 56-year-old woman died after falling into an open Con Edison manhole in Midtown last spring.
