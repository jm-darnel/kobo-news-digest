Understanding the site of the deadly Brooklyn building collapse

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 12, 2026

As investigators work to determine what caused the deadly building collapse in Brooklyn, an architect is helping explain what stood on the site before the collapse and how the construction work may have affected the surrounding buildings.

Using maps and overhead images, architect Bill Gati walks through the site near Bedford and Flatbush avenues.

Gati, who has 40 years of experience working alongside the city’s Department of Buildings, joined "Mornings On 1" to break down the site and explain what the images reveal.
