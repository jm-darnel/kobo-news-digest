LISTEN: An Unimaginable and Unnecessary Catastrophe

By Faq Nyc · The City · November 16, 2026

A building collapse is a hugely complicated thing. I want to give a shout out to the firefighters and other first responders who were working furiously in the dark, in the rain to rescue every person they could find…

Hosts Christina Greer, Katie Honan and Harry Siegel talk about the many questions that still need answers after the Flatbush building collapse, and a moment of joy amid a scene of unspeakable loss.

Plus, they dig into Mayor Zohran Mamdani disbanding the NYPD’s Strategic Review Group, his latest conversation with President Donald Trump, and much more.
