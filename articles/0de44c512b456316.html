$85K watch taken from man who blacked out in woman’s SUV in NYC, NYPD says

By Charles Lane · Gothamist · November 19, 2026

A man who agreed to hang out with a stranger in her SUV in Chelsea earlier this month lost consciousness after imbibing a drink and woke up to find his $85,000 watch gone from his wrist, police said Thursday.

As they drove around, she offered him a cocktail, which a police spokesperson said held an unknown substance. At some point, he lost consciousness, police said.

When he came to a dozen blocks away, the watch, an Audemars Piguet Royal Oak, was gone from his wrist, according to police. He woke up on the street at West 24th Street and Seventh Avenue, police said.

Police described the woman as wearing a black and white top, blue jeans and black sneakers. The department is investigating the theft as a grand larceny and said no injuries were reported.

Authorities are asking anyone with information to call Crime Stoppers.
