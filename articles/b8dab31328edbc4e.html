Survivors recall trauma after Flatbush Avenue building collapse

By Samantha Liebman · NY1 · November 13, 2026

Eight people died in a building collapse on Flatbush Avenue, and survivors said they are now trying to recover after losing their homes and belongings.

Residents of 1323 Flatbush Ave. described the moment the building collapsed and the trauma that followed. Ciahola Lawrence, who lived in the building, said she had taken the day off from work to rest and was inside when it happened.

“When I opened my eyes, it was just light everywhere,” Lawrence said. “And I kept saying, 'What is this?'”

Lawrence said she was evacuated from the building and saw neighbors being carried out.

“As they’re taking me, evacuating me from the building, I could see my neighbors on stretchers,” Lawrence said. “They were already dead.”

Lawrence said she called her husband at work, and he rushed home. She said he passed out when he saw the extent of the collapse and had to be hospitalized.

Mamadou Mouctar Balde said his pregnant wife, Fatoumata Bah, was inside the building. Speaking through a translator, Balde said he panicked as he tried to get help for her.
