NYPD arrests and charges city schools employee in murder of Brooklyn jogger

By Kaya Laterman · Gothamist · November 7, 2026

A current employee of the Department of Education was arrested and charged with murder Saturday in the death of Brooklyn jogger last month, the NYPD said.

Police officials said they arrested Sayed Mohassib, 27, and charged him with murder.

Katie Suarez, 28, was stabbed in the neck as she was running on the path on Shore Parkway near Bay 17th Street on Oct. 30.

It's unclear if Mohassib had a relationship to the victim, but police said previously they believed the attack to be random. His attorney information was not immediately available.

On the night of the attack, Suarez was doing her usual routine, Chief of Detectives Joseph Kenny told reporters during a recent briefing.

She left work at a nearby bank around 6:35 p.m., changed into athletic gear, met up with her mother who took her work outfit, and then set off for her run, he said.

Her father had also just left work and was planning to meet up with both of them at a nearby Wendy’s restaurant and give them a ride home after Suarez finished her jog, according to Kenny.

At 7:56 p.m., her father called Suarez to find out when she would arrive, and she told him she was about half a mile away and would be there soon. Kenny said when she didn’t arrive on time, the father set off to look for her on the path and at 8:16 p.m. he found her unconscious under a tree, bleeding from the neck.

This is a developing story and has been updated with additional information.
