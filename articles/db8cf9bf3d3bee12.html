Detectives’ Endowment Association president reacts to Mamdani disbanding NYPD’s Strategic Response Group

By Spectrum News Staff · NY1 · November 17, 2026

On Monday, Mayor Zohran Mamdani and Police Commissioner Jessica Tisch announced that they will disband the NYPD’s Strategic Response Group, which handles counterterrorism and crowd control.

Scott Munro, the president of the Detectives’ Endowment Association, the union representing NYPD detectives, joined NY1 political anchor Errol Louis on “Inside City Hall” Tuesday to discuss more.
